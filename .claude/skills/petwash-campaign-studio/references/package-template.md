# Campaign Package — section template & quality bar

Model output: the "הטקס / The Ritual" package (station opening, no facts supplied —
everything unverified went to the compliance log, station stayed city-level, pricing
got a legal flag). Match that density.

## 0. Brief Recap (3 lines max)
Promoting · Audience & channels · Assumptions ("no date/venue/pricing supplied — all
such details TO VERIFY").

## 1. Campaign Concept
- Big idea: ONE sentence, HE then EN.
- Insight: one short paragraph — the human truth the campaign stands on.
- Emotional territory: 2–3 words (e.g., ritual / trust / quiet pride).
- Campaign name: HE / EN.
- Channel plan table: Channel | Asset | Role. Default channels: Instagram Reels
  (hero + cutdown), Stories (6s bumper + locator), YouTube pre-roll (15s),
  in-station screen loop, outdoor near station (typography-first, stage-white).

## 2. Hero Film Script (30–60s)
Per scene: `SCENE n — t:tt–t:tt`, IMAGE, CAMERA, SOUND, SUPER (HE) / SUPER (EN).
Silence is a tool — prefer supers + diegetic sound over VO; if VO is used, write it
HE first with an EN parity line. End card: stage-white, ink-black wordmark, one line,
optional single gold accent element.

## 3. Storyboard
Table: # | Duration | Frame | Camera | Light | Audio. One row per shot, including
split sub-shots (3a/3b/3c). Lighting vocabulary: soft morning window light, high-key
white, natural daylight, flat white.

## 4. Cutdowns
- 15s: name the shots reused, the single super pair, end card.
- 6s bumper: hero moment (~3.5s) + end card (~2.5s) + one line HE/EN.

## 5. AI Video Generation Prompts
See ai-video-prompts.md. One prompt block per shot + a one-line `Post:` note
(supers, sound, grade added in post — NEVER baked into the AI prompt).

## 6. Supporting Copy
- 3 tagline options, HE / EN pairs.
- Instagram captions: launch, mid-flight, reminder. Hashtags minimal (#PetWash).
- Landing section: headline, one body paragraph, CTA — HE block then EN block.
- In-station screen loop: 3 short lines, HE / EN.

## 7. Compliance & Verification Log
Table: Claim | Where used | Status (verified source / TO VERIFY / REMOVED / NOT USED)
| Note. Pricing or promotion wording → ⚠️ LEGAL REVIEW row even when NOT USED, so the
reviewer sees it was considered and excluded.

## Quality bar
- Every Hebrew line reads native-premium; read it aloud mentally in Hebrew first.
- No section padding, no "options menus" — one confident answer per slot.
- The whole package should fit in one screen-scroll per section.
