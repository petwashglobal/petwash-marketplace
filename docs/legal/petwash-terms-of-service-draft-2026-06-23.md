## 1. Who we are and what these terms cover
PetWash is operated by Pet Wash Ltd ("PetWash", "we", "us"). These terms govern your use of the
PetWash app, website and services. By creating an account or using any service you accept them. If
you do not agree, do not use the services.

PetWash provides:
- **K9000 self-service wash stations** — operated by PetWash directly.
- **The PetWash marketplace** — booking independent, verified providers (grooming, dog walking, pet
  sitting, training, transport, hosting).
- **PetWash Privilege** — membership, wallet, credits and benefits.
- **eGift, Academy and Shop** — stored-value gifts, courses and products.

## 2. The marketplace is an intermediary, not the provider
For marketplace services, PetWash connects you with **independent providers**. The provider — not
PetWash — performs the service and is responsible for it. PetWash is **not** the employer, agent for
performance, or guarantor of any provider, and **is not an insurer** (see §11).

## 3. Eligibility and your account
You must be 18+ and able to form a binding contract. Keep your login secure; you are responsible for
activity on your account. Provide accurate information and keep it current.

## 4. Prices — all-in, no surprises (Consumer Protection Law §17a)
Every price is shown **inclusive of VAT and all mandatory charges**, as a single total, **before you
confirm**. We do not add hidden fees at checkout. Where a service has variable elements (e.g.
extra-pet surcharge, extra nights), the basis is disclosed before you book. VAT is charged at the
statutory rate (currently 18%).

- **K9000 washes:** fixed per-wash or credit-pack pricing.
- **Marketplace services:** the provider sets the price (above a quality minimum); you see the full
  total before booking. PetWash charges the provider a **15% platform commission** (this is already
  included in the price you see; you pay one total).
- **Discounts / promo codes** apply to **K9000 washes only**. Member and eligible discounts are
  capped and honestly displayed; we never advertise a discount we do not actually give.

## 5. Payments, wallet and stored value
You can pay by supported payment methods, PetWash **wallet** balance, **eGift** credit, or Privilege
credits. Wallet top-ups and eGift balances are **stored value** redeemable for PetWash services per
the eGift & Wallet Policy. Stored value is not a bank deposit, earns no interest, and is handled in
accordance with applicable Israeli payment-services regulation. [LEGAL FLAG C2: confirm the funds-
handling model — PSP-held / trust account / licence — before stored value scales.]

Payment is taken at booking but, for marketplace bookings, **released to the provider only after**
the service is completed and your cancellation window has closed (see §6).

## 6. Cancellations and refunds (statutory rights preserved)
You have the cancellation rights granted by the Consumer Protection Law and its cancellation
regulations, including, where applicable, a **14-day cooling-off period** for qualifying
transactions, with any cancellation fee capped at the **lower of 5% of the transaction or ₪100**.

- Refunds are **tiered and fair** by how far ahead you cancel, and shown before you confirm.
- If a service cannot be delivered, you are made whole through the app. A refund in progress is
  shown truthfully as "refund being processed" — we never display money as moved before it is.
- Stored-value (eGift/wallet) and made-to-order items follow the eGift & Wallet Policy and the law.

## 7. Your conduct and the provider relationship
Use the services lawfully and treat providers and animals with respect. Keep communication and
payment **in the app** — going off-platform to avoid commission or oversight is prohibited and may
end your access. Provide accurate pet information (health, behaviour, needs) so services are safe.

## 8. Reviews and content
Reviews must be honest and first-hand. We may remove content that is unlawful, fraudulent, or
abusive. You grant PetWash a licence to display content you submit (e.g. reviews, pet photos) for
operating and promoting the services.

## 9. Privacy and communications (Privacy Protection Law incl. Amendment 13)
We process personal data under the Privacy Protection Law (including Amendment 13) and our Privacy
Policy. We do not sell your personal data. Marketing messages are sent only with your **opt-in**
consent as required by the Communications Law (Spam Law); you can withdraw consent at any time.
In-app chat keeps your contact details private from providers.

and content are protected. You may use them only to use the services.

available". Independent providers are responsible for their own service and insurance. To the extent

aggregate liability for a claim relating to a booking is limited to the amount you paid for that
booking. Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law (including for personal injury
caused by negligence or for consumer rights that cannot be waived).

## 12. Disputes, governing law

Israel, and the competent courts of Israel have jurisdiction. This does not remove mandatory
