## 1. What this covers
- **Wallet** — a balance you load to pay for PetWash services faster.
- **eGift** — prepaid gift credit you buy for yourself or someone else.
Both are **stored value redeemable for PetWash services**. They are **not** bank deposits, earn no
interest, and cannot be exchanged for cash except where Israeli law requires.

## 2. Buying and loading
Prices are shown all-in (incl. VAT where applicable). An eGift is delivered to the recipient you
specify; a wallet top-up is added to your balance. We issue the appropriate tax document on purchase
and on redemption per VAT rules (disclosed-agent treatment where relevant; VAT at the statutory
rate, currently 18%).

## 3. Redemption
Stored value is redeemed against eligible PetWash services. **Discounts/promo codes apply to K9000
washes only** and do not stack onto marketplace commissions. Redemption is recorded; an eGift is
bound to the recipient/account it is activated on and cannot be re-spent once redeemed.

## 4. Expiry and "breakage"
Stored value is valid for at least the minimum period required by Israeli law. Where a balance
expires, it does so only as permitted by law, and we notify you before expiry where required. VAT on
unredeemed balances is handled per the applicable rules.

## 5. Refunds and cancellation
Statutory cancellation rights apply (see the Terms of Service §6). Refunds of stored value follow the
law and the original payment method where required. A refund in progress is shown truthfully as
"refund being processed".

## 6. Lost, stolen, fraud
Keep your account secure; stored value tied to your account is used as if by you. Report unauthorised
use immediately. We may suspend balances we reasonably believe are fraudulent pending review.

## 7. How funds are held [LEGAL FLAG — C2, resolve before scaling]
PetWash holds stored value in accordance with applicable Israeli payment-services regulation. The
operating model (PSP-held funds / segregated trust account / payment-services licence) must be
confirmed with counsel and the payment partner before stored value scales, because holding customer
funds can trigger licensing/safeguarding duties. This clause will be finalised to match the chosen
model.
