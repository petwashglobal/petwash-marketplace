# PetWash — Approved-Materials Undertaking (Operator / Provider)

**Working draft · Version 0.9 · 23 June 2026 · Pet Wash Ltd**
*Implements §14 of the master legal framework. §30 doc #12. Hebrew governs; English mirrors.*

> Binds station operators and relevant providers to use only PetWash-approved consumables and to
> follow product-safety and labelling law.

## 1. Approved products only
The operator/provider will use only shampoo, conditioner, tea-tree-oil blends, cleaning, sanitising
and related products **approved in writing by PetWash**. No substitution, dilution change, or
unapproved additive without prior written approval.

## 2. No medical claims
No claim that a product "cures", "treats disease", "prevents disease" or "suits every skin problem"
may be made without proper professional/regulatory authorisation. Permitted phrasing only, e.g.:
"contains an Australian tea-tree-oil blend", "for cosmetic/cleaning use only", "avoid contact with
eyes, ears and mouth", "discontinue use and consult a vet if sensitivity occurs".

## 3. Safety, storage, traceability
The operator/provider will: keep SDS / safety sheets where required; track expiry, batch and storage
conditions; follow any recall process; store products safely and out of reach of animals/children;
and use products per manufacturer and PetWash instructions.

## 4. Sourcing
Consumables are purchased only through PetWash or its approved supply channel. Counterfeit, grey-
market or unapproved sourcing is a breach.

## 5. Regulatory note (Israel)
Cosmetic/pet products may require Ministry of Health registration, correct labelling and import
compliance. PetWash and the supply channel will confirm registration; the operator must not sell or
apply products it knows to be non-compliant.

## 6. Breach
Unapproved products may cause animal harm, brand damage and legal exposure. Breach permits
suspension, removal from the platform, and liability for resulting damage/indemnity.

## Operator/provider confirmation
> "I undertake to use only PetWash-approved products, to make no unauthorised medical claims, to keep
> required SDS and traceability, and to follow product-safety law. I understand breach may end my
> participation and expose me to liability."
