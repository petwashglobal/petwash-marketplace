## 1. Who controls your data
Pet Wash Ltd is the data controller. Our database is registered/treated per the Privacy Protection
Law. Privacy contact / security officer: [privacy@petwash.co.il — confirm].

## 2. What we collect
- **You give us:** name, phone, email, address, password; pet details (name, type, breed, age,
  health/behaviour notes); booking details; messages in in-app chat; reviews; support requests.
- **Payment:** payment method tokens and transaction records (full card numbers are handled by our
  payment processor, not stored by us); wallet/eGift/credit balances.
- **Providers additionally:** identity document and verification data, tax/`osek` status, bank/payout
  details, insurance documents.
- **Automatically:** device and app data, approximate location (for finding nearby stations/
  providers — only with permission), usage and log data, cookies/identifiers (see Cookie Notice).

## 3. Why we use it (purposes) and legal basis
- Provide and operate the services, bookings, payments and the wallet — to perform our contract with
  you.
- Verify providers, prevent fraud, keep animals and people safe, and enforce our terms — legitimate
  interests and legal obligation.
- Comply with tax, accounting and consumer-law obligations.
- Send **service** messages (booking, receipts, security). **Marketing** messages only with your
  separate opt-in (Communications/Spam Law); withdraw any time.
- Improve the services using aggregated/de-identified analytics.

## 4. Sharing
- **Providers** receive only what they need to perform your booking (not your raw contact details —
  chat is in-app).
- **Processors** acting for us: payment processors (SUMIT/Nayax/payment gateway), cloud hosting,
  SMS/email, analytics, mapping — under data-processing terms, using data only on our instructions.
- **Authorities** where legally required.
- We **do not sell** personal data. We do not share it for third-party marketing without consent.

## 5. International transfers
Where data is processed outside Israel (e.g. cloud providers), we use providers offering protection
consistent with Israeli law and appropriate safeguards.

## 6. Retention
We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal retention
duties (e.g. tax/accounting records for the periods required by law). Provider identity documents
follow our retention/destruction and legal-hold policy. When no longer needed, data is deleted or
de-identified.

## 7. Your rights (Privacy Protection Law)
You may **review** your data, request **correction** of inaccurate data, and request **deletion**
where applicable. Submit requests in-app or to the privacy contact; we respond within the timeframe
required by law and may verify your identity first. You may withdraw marketing consent at any time.

## 8. Security
We apply technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data
(encryption in transit, access controls, audit logging, encryption of stored national-ID data). No
system is perfectly secure; we work to protect your data and to meet our breach-notification duties
under Amendment 13.

## 9. Children
The services are for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 10. Changes & contact
We may update this policy; material changes are notified in-app. Current version always available in
the app. Contact: Pet Wash Ltd, עוזי חיטמן 8, ראש העין 4806859; privacy contact [confirm].
