# PetWash — Service-Specific Terms (Addenda)

**Working draft · Version 0.9 · 23 June 2026 · Pet Wash Ltd**

> Original PetWash drafts, Israel-tuned. These addenda supplement the Terms of Service for individual
> services; the ToS governs generally, these add service-specific safety, consent and liability
> terms. Hebrew governs for Israeli users. Provider obligations are also covered by the Provider &
> Host Rulebook (Build D).

---

## A. Pet sitting & home hosting
- Owner provides accurate feeding, medication, exercise and emergency-vet instructions **in writing**
  before the stay. Owner confirms vaccinations are current.
- **Emergency-vet authority:** owner pre-authorises reasonable emergency veterinary care up to a
  stated limit if the owner cannot be reached; costs are the owner's responsibility.
- **Premises (hosting):** the host confirms a safe, secure space (no escape risk, climate control,
  fresh water, separation of incompatible animals). PetWash may require a premises self-check.
- Multi-pet and multi-night pricing is shown before booking; surcharges apply **per night** where
  stated. The host is responsible for the animal's wellbeing for the booked duration.
- Daily check-in (photo/message) for multi-day stays.

## B. Dog walking
- Owner discloses behaviour, reactivity, and whether **off-lead** is permitted; off-lead requires
  explicit owner consent and is at the owner's risk where local law allows it.
- GPS/route tracking may be used for safety and proof-of-service; location data is handled per the
  Privacy Policy.
- The walker follows lead/harness and local leash and clean-up laws.

## C. Pet transport
- Animals are transported with appropriate restraint/carrier and climate safety. The transporter
  keeps the vehicle clean and does not leave an animal unattended in unsafe conditions.
- Owner provides handling notes and any travel-anxiety/medication information in advance.
- Pricing (distance/time basis) is shown before booking.

## D. K9000 self-service stations
- Use the station per the on-machine and in-app instructions. Use is **at your own risk**; you are
  responsible for your dog's suitability for self-wash and for supervising it at all times.
- Do not use on aggressive, unwell, or unsuitable animals. Follow water-temperature and product
  guidance; products are for external canine use only.
- Report faults via the app; do not use a machine flagged out of service. PetWash maintains the
  stations but is not liable for misuse.

## E. Common to all services
- **Not insurance:** PetWash is a platform, not an insurer (see ToS §11). Providers carry their own
  insurance; owners are encouraged to hold suitable pet cover.
- **Incident reporting:** report any injury, illness or incident immediately in-app. Incident forms
  record facts for resolution and **are not an admission of liability** by any party.
- **Welfare first:** any provider may decline or stop a service if an animal's safety or welfare is
  at risk; refunds follow the cancellation policy.
