# PetWash — Cookie Notice

**Working draft · Version 0.9 · 23 June 2026 · Pet Wash Ltd**

> Original PetWash draft, Israel-tuned. Companion to the Privacy Policy. Hebrew governs for Israeli
> users. Pairs with a consent banner that defaults to the privacy-preserving option (non-essential
> off until you opt in).

## 1. What cookies we use
- **Strictly necessary** — sign-in, security, session, cart/checkout. Always on; the site can't work
  without them.
- **Functional** — remember language (he/RTL default), preferences.
- **Analytics** — aggregated usage to improve the app. Only with your consent.
- **Marketing** — measure campaigns. Only with your consent.

## 2. Your choices
On first visit you choose which non-essential categories to allow; necessary cookies need no consent.
You can change or withdraw consent at any time from the cookie settings. We do not use non-essential
cookies before you opt in.

## 3. Third parties
Some cookies/identifiers are set by processors we use (e.g. analytics, maps, payment). They act under
our instructions; see the Privacy Policy "Sharing" section.

## 4. Managing cookies
You can also control cookies in your browser; blocking necessary cookies may break parts of the
service.
