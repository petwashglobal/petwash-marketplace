# PetWash — Confidentiality & Non-Use of Information Addendum

**Working draft · Version 0.9 · 23 June 2026 · Pet Wash Ltd**
*Implements §20 + §25 of the master legal framework. §30 doc #20. Hebrew governs; English mirrors.*

> Binds providers, operators and partners to protect PetWash and customer information and not to
> misuse it. Supplements the Provider Agreement / Operator terms.

## 1. Confidential information
Includes: customer and provider data; pricing, commercial terms and financial models; the operations
manual, methods and know-how; product/supply information; software, designs and unreleased features;
business plans, franchise/licensing materials; and anything marked or reasonably understood as
confidential.

## 2. Obligations
The recipient will: use confidential information **only** to perform the agreed services/operation;
keep it secure; not disclose it to third parties without written consent; and not copy it beyond
what is needed.

## 3. No misuse of customer data — anti-circumvention
Customer details obtained through PetWash may **not** be used for the recipient's own marketing, to
solicit customers off-platform, or to circumvent PetWash (no off-platform payment, no diverting
bookings). This survives termination.

## 4. Data protection
Personal data is processed only on PetWash's instructions and per the Privacy Policy and the Privacy
Protection Law (incl. Amendment 13). Report any suspected data breach immediately.

## 5. Exclusions
Information that is public (not through breach), already lawfully held, independently developed, or
required to be disclosed by law (with notice where lawful).

## 6. Return / deletion on termination
On termination, the recipient stops using and **returns or deletes** confidential information and
customer data, keeping only what law requires, and confirms deletion on request.

## 7. Remedies
Breach may cause irreparable harm; PetWash may seek injunctive relief in addition to damages,
indemnity and removal from the platform.

## Confirmation
> "I undertake to keep PetWash and customer information confidential, to use it only to perform my
> services, not to use customer data for my own marketing or to circumvent PetWash, and to return or
> delete it on termination."
