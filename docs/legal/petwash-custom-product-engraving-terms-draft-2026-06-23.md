# PetWash — Custom-Product & Engraving Terms

**Working draft · Version 0.9 · 23 June 2026 · Pet Wash Ltd**
*Implements §15 of the master legal framework. §30 doc #14. Hebrew governs; English mirrors.*

> For personalised items — engraved collars, name tags, custom apparel — bearing a name, phone,
> size, colour, language or design you choose.

## 1. You verify the details
Before you confirm, you are responsible for checking the **exact** text, name, number, size, colour,
language and design. We produce what you approve.

**Required confirmation at checkout:**
> "I confirm I have checked the engraving/personalisation details and they are correct. I understand
> a personalised product may not be returnable or cancellable once production has begun, except for a
> defect, non-conformity, or a mandatory right under law."

## 2. Cancellation of personalised items
Because production starts to your specification, personalised items are, to the extent permitted by
the Consumer Protection Law, **exempt from the standard cooling-off return**. This does **not** remove
your rights where the item is **faulty, not as described, or differs from your approved details** —
those are remedied (repair/replace/refund) per law.

## 3. What we may refuse
We may decline an engraving/personalisation that contains: offensive content; unlawful content;
infringement of third-party rights; trademarks you don't own; unreadable text; or dangerous/
misleading content.

## 4. Production & accuracy
Minor variation in colour, finish or engraving depth inherent to the process is not a defect. We
proof against the details you approved.

## 5. Safety
Tags/collars are accessories, not restraints or tracking guarantees. Check fit and condition
regularly; supervise your pet.

## 6. Governing law
Israeli law; consumer rights preserved.
