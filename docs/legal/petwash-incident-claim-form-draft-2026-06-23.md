# PetWash — Incident / Claim / Damage Report Form

**Working draft · Version 0.9 · 23 June 2026 · Pet Wash Ltd**
*Implements §24 of the master legal framework. §30 doc #10. Hebrew governs; English mirrors.*

> Structured record for any unusual event (injury, illness, escape, bite, property damage, complaint).
> Filing facts is **not** an admission of liability by anyone. Late filing may affect eligibility for
> assistance, refund, review or defence.

## Header (top of form)
> "This form records the facts of an incident for review and resolution. Completing it is **not** an
> admission of fault or liability by any party. Liability is determined by law, the agreements, the
> insurance documents and the evidence."

## Fields to capture
- **Booking number** (required)
- **Reporter** (customer / provider / operator) + name + contact
- **Customer identity** · **Provider identity**
- **Service type** (K9000 / sitting / hosting / walking / transport / grooming / training / station)
- **Location** (customer home / provider home / station / route) + address/site
- **Date & time** of the incident
- **Description** — what happened, in sequence (free text)
- **Animal(s) involved** + relevant health/behaviour notes
- **Photos / video** (upload)
- **Was a vet contacted?** (Y/N) + details + cost
- **Was the owner notified?** (Y/N) + when
- **Property damage?** (Y/N) + description
- **Injury?** (to animal / person) + description
- **Witnesses** (names/contact)
- **Monetary claim/demand, if any** + amount + basis
- **Insurance** — does the reporter hold relevant cover? insurer/policy (if claiming)

## Submission & handling
- Submit in-app immediately after the event; attach evidence.
- Routes to the admin alerts/dispute queue; the related payout is held while a dispute is open.
- The reporter receives a reference number; PetWash responds within the policy timeframe.
- Records (time, IP, device, content) are retained per the Privacy Policy.

## Reporter confirmation
> "I confirm the above is true and complete to the best of my knowledge. I understand this report is
> not an admission of liability and that PetWash is a platform, not an insurer."
