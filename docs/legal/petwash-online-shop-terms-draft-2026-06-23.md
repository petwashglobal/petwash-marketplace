# PetWash — Online Shop Terms

**Working draft · Version 0.9 · 23 June 2026 · Pet Wash Ltd**
*Implements §15–§17 of the master legal framework. §30 doc #13. Hebrew governs; English mirrors.*

> Terms for buying physical products from the PetWash Shop. Supplements the Terms of Service.

## 1. Products and prices
Product descriptions, images and prices are shown as accurately as possible. Prices are **all-in,
inclusive of VAT** (currently 18%), with shipping shown before you confirm (Consumer Protection Law
§17a). We may correct obvious pricing errors before dispatch.

## 2. Orders
An order is an offer; a contract forms when we confirm/dispatch. We may decline or limit orders
(stock, suspected fraud, pricing error). Stock is subject to availability.

## 3. Payment
Pay via supported methods or eligible PetWash credit. We issue the appropriate tax document.

## 4. Shipping & delivery
Estimated times are not guarantees. Risk passes on delivery. Provide an accurate address; redelivery
of returned-undeliverable parcels may incur cost.

## 5. Returns, cancellation & refunds (consumer rights preserved)
Your statutory rights apply, including, where applicable, a **14-day cooling-off** period for
qualifying purchases, with any cancellation fee capped at the **lower of 5% or ₪100**. Exceptions
permitted by law include **custom/personalised** items (see the Custom-Product Terms), products made
to your specification, and items unsealed/used where return is precluded by law. Faulty or
not-as-described goods are remedied per law. Refunds go to the original method where required;
"refund being processed" is shown truthfully.

## 6. Product safety
Use products per their instructions and intended purpose. For consumables, see the labelling/safety
notes; no unauthorised medical claims are made. Keep products away from animals/children except as
intended.

## 7. Liability
To the extent permitted by law, our liability for a product claim is limited to the price paid for
that product; statutory product-liability and consumer rights are unaffected.

## 8. Governing law
Israeli law; courts of Israel. Mandatory consumer protections are preserved.
