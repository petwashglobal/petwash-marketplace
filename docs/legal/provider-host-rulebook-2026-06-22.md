# PetWash™ Provider & Host Rulebook

**Version 1.0 — 22 June 2026**
**Pet Wash Ltd · עוזי חיטמן 8, ראש העין 4806859**

> This rulebook is the binding standard for every approved provider and host on the PetWash
> platform. By accepting bookings you agree to it. It complements (does not replace) your signed
> Provider Agreement, the Terms of Service, and Israeli law. Where this rulebook and your signed
> agreement differ, the signed agreement governs.

---

## 1. Who this applies to

- **Service Providers** — groomers, dog walkers, pet sitters, trainers, transporters, mobile
  groomers, and station assistants who accept bookings through PetWash.
- **Hosts** — providers who board/host animals overnight or for multi-day stays.

PetWash Ltd owns and operates the K9000 self-service wash stations directly. The marketplace is a
**joint platform** connecting independent providers with customers. Providers are independent
contractors, **not employees** of PetWash Ltd.

---

## 2. The commission & how you get paid

- **Platform commission is 15%** on every marketplace booking (like Rover / MadPaws). It is
  deducted automatically from the booking total; you receive the remainder.
- You set your own prices, subject to the **minimum price floors** below (no ₪0.02 or symbolic
  jobs — they damage trust and the brand).

| Service | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Dog walking | ₪49 |
| Pet sitting (per visit/night) | ₪99 |
| Pet transport | ₪79 |
| Training session | ₪149 |
| Grooming | ₪129 |
| Mobile grooming | ₪149 |
| Station assistance | ₪39 |

- You may price **above** these freely. Prices must be honest — no bait pricing, no "free" that
  isn't free, no surprise add-ons at handover.
- **Discounts:** platform-wide discounts and promo codes apply to **K9000 washes only** (PetWash
  owns those 100%). You may run your **own** reasonable promotions on your own services, but you
  may not advertise PetWash-funded discounts you don't fund.

### When you actually get paid (payout gates)

A payout is released only after **all** of these are true:

1. The booking is marked **completed** (service delivered).
2. The customer's **refund/cancellation window** has closed.
3. Your **tax details are verified** (osek / invoicing on file).
4. There is **no open dispute** on the booking.
5. You are **approved for payout** (account in good standing).

Payment at time of booking is **not** payment to you — funds are held until the gates clear. This
protects both you and the customer.

## 3. Cancellations & refunds

- Customers have statutory cancellation rights under Israeli Consumer Protection law, including a
  **14-day cooling-off** period for qualifying transactions and a cancellation fee capped at the
  **lower of 5% or ₪100**.
- **Provider-initiated cancellations** hurt customers and your standing. Repeated last-minute
  cancellations lead to review and possible removal.
- Tiered refunds apply based on how far ahead a customer cancels (set in the booking flow). You do
  not get to keep 100% on every cancellation, and customers are not refunded 100% on every
  cancellation — the platform applies the fair tiered policy automatically.
- If a service can't be delivered, the customer is made whole through the platform's refund
  process. Do not take side payments to "fix" a refund.

---

## 4. Conduct & quality standards

- **Be on time, communicate, and document.** Use in-app chat — never move the customer off-platform
  to avoid commission or oversight. Off-platform solicitation is grounds for removal.
- **Animal welfare first.** Never leave an animal in distress, in a hot vehicle, or unsupervised
  beyond the agreed scope. Report injuries or illness to the owner immediately and to PetWash.
- **Cleanliness & safety.** Sanitary equipment, clean transport, safe restraints. Mobile groomers
  and transporters must keep vehicles clean and climate-safe.
- **Respect property and privacy.** Enter only the areas needed for the service. Do not photograph
  a customer's home interior except as needed to document the pet's condition, and never publish it.
- **No discrimination.** Serve customers fairly regardless of background.

---

## 5. Hosting / long-stay rules (Hosts)

- Provide a **safe, secure space**: no escape risks, climate control, fresh water always available,
  separation from incompatible animals.
- Agree feeding, medication, and exercise instructions **in writing** before the stay.
- **Daily check-in** with the owner (photo/message) for multi-day stays.
- **Emergency plan:** know the nearest vet, keep the owner's emergency contact and the animal's
  vaccination/medical info, and have authority-to-treat limits agreed in advance.
- Multi-pet and multi-night pricing must be transparent: surcharges applied **per night** where
  stated, not silently stacked.
- You are responsible for the animal's wellbeing for the full duration of the booked stay.

---

## 6. Identity, verification & documents

- You must complete identity verification (typed ID with checksum validation, selfie/face match
  where required, and a signed declaration) before going live.
- Keep your details current. Provide a valid **osek / tax status** so payouts and invoicing work.
- PetWash periodically re-confirms verification (roughly every 6 months). Lapsed verification
  pauses payouts until renewed.
- Official documents you submit are handled under our retention and privacy policy (Privacy
  Protection Law incl. Amendment 13). We destroy or legal-hold per policy; we do not over-collect.

---

## 7. Insurance — important

- **PetWash is a platform, not an insurer.** Nothing here is insurance and PetWash does not
  guarantee against loss, injury, or damage.
- You are responsible for carrying any insurance appropriate to your service (e.g. liability for
  grooming, transport, hosting). Where PetWash offers or references a commercial policy, it is the
  insurer's policy on the insurer's terms — read it.
- Operate within your competence and the law.

---

## 8. Tax & invoicing

- You are responsible for your own tax obligations as an independent contractor.
- The platform issues the documentation it is responsible for; you are responsible for your own
  reporting where applicable. VAT is handled at the platform's disclosed rate (currently 18%).
- Do not issue misleading receipts or duplicate official numbers.

---

## 9. Ratings, reviews & standing

- Customer reviews are real and shown as-is. Do not solicit fake reviews or retaliate against
  honest ones.
- Persistent low ratings, safety incidents, off-platform solicitation, dishonest pricing, or
  welfare violations lead to review, suspension, or permanent removal.

---

## 10. Disputes

- Raise issues through the in-app support/dispute flow. Do not pressure customers privately.
- During an open dispute the related payout is held until resolved.
- PetWash's resolution under the platform policy is final for platform purposes; this does not
  remove your or the customer's legal rights.

---

## 11. Changes to this rulebook

PetWash may update this rulebook. Material changes will be communicated, and continued use of the
platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
