# PetWash — Brand & IP Licence (Provider / Operator)

**Working draft · Version 0.9 · 23 June 2026 · Pet Wash Ltd**
*Implements §20 + §13 of the master legal framework. §30 doc #7. Hebrew governs; English mirrors.*

> A limited licence to display the PetWash brand. Not a franchise, not a transfer of ownership.

## 1. Grant
PetWash grants the provider/operator a **limited, revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable,
non-sublicensable** licence to use the PetWash name, logo and approved marketing materials **solely**
to provide approved services / operate an approved station, strictly per PetWash brand guidelines.

## 2. Ownership
All IP — name, logo, trademarks, app, code, UI, QR, manuals, designs, photos, copy, packaging,
know-how, methods, franchise/licensing programs — remains owned by Pet Wash Ltd or its licensors.
The licensee acquires no ownership and builds no goodwill in its own name; all goodwill accrues to
PetWash.

## 3. Restrictions
The licensee shall not: alter, recolour or recreate the logo; register a similar domain or social
handle; imply ownership or partnership; use the brand outside the approved service/site; reverse-
engineer; or sub-licence. Logo usage must follow the rule: real asset only, top-centre, unmodified.

## 4. Quality control
PetWash may set and audit brand, service and safety standards, and require correction. Use of the
brand on substandard service is a breach.

## 5. Term & termination
The licence lasts only while the underlying provider/operator relationship is active and in good
standing. On termination the licensee must immediately: stop all use of the PetWash name; remove
signage; delete brand materials; return manuals; stop brand-related media; and not represent any
ongoing PetWash connection.

## 6. No guarantee
The brand licence carries no promise of revenue, traffic or profit.

## 7. Governing law
Israeli law; courts of Israel. Trademark to be recorded with the Israeli Trademark Register;
know-how protected as a trade secret.
