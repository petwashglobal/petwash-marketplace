# Provider Privacy & Confidentiality Rules

These rules govern how you handle the personal information of Members and the access you gain through bookings on the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash"). They reflect Israel's Protection of Privacy Law and its amendments.

## 1. What is confidential
1.1 Member personal data (name, address, phone, email), home access details (keys, codes, alarm details), pet details, schedules, payment context, and anything you observe inside a Member's home or about their household.

## 2. Use only for the booking
2.1 Use Member information **only** to perform the specific booking. Do not use it for marketing, your own business, lending, sale, or any other purpose.
2.2 Do not store Member contact details outside the Platform beyond what the active booking requires, and delete local copies (keys/codes, notes) when the booking ends.

## 3. Do not disclose
3.1 Do not share, post, sell, or disclose Member or household information to anyone. Do not photograph or record the inside of a home except as needed for the service and permitted by *provider-photo-media-rules*.
3.2 Do not contact a Member for purposes outside the booking, and do not use their details to take the relationship off-platform (see *provider-no-off-platform-circumvention-rule*).

## 4. Security
4.1 Protect access credentials (keys, codes, app login). Do not share your account. Report any loss or suspected breach to PetWash immediately.

## 5. Inside the home
5.1 Respect the privacy of the Member's home: enter only the areas needed for the service, do not open drawers/cupboards beyond what feeding/care requires, and never invite others in without the Member's consent.

## 6. Lawful requests and PetWash's role
6.1 PetWash is the controller of platform data; you process Member data on its behalf for the booking. Direct any data request (access, correction, deletion) from a Member to PetWash; do not action it yourself.

## 7. After the booking
7.1 Confidentiality survives the end of the booking and of your relationship with PetWash.

Breach may result in suspension, termination, and liability under Israeli privacy law.
