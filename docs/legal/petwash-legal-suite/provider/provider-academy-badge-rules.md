# Provider Academy & Badge Rules

These rules govern the PetWash Academy training and the badges that providers can earn on the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash").

## 1. What the Academy is
1.1 The Academy offers optional and (where stated) required learning on safety, care standards, and platform rules. Some service types or features may require completing a module before approval or before accepting certain bookings.

## 2. Badges
2.1 A **badge** marks that you completed a module, demonstrated a skill, or met a standard. Badges appear on your profile and may affect visibility or eligibility for certain bookings.
2.2 A badge is **not** a professional licence, certification, or guarantee. It does not replace any qualification, licence, or insurance the law requires (see *provider-legal-right-to-provide-declaration*, *provider-insurance-licence-status-declaration*).

## 3. Earn them honestly
3.1 Complete training and assessments **yourself and honestly**. Do not cheat, share answers, have someone else complete a module, or misrepresent a badge you did not earn.
3.2 Do not display, claim, or imply a badge, certification, or qualification you do not hold.

## 4. Keeping badges
4.1 Badges may **expire**, require refreshers, or be **revoked** if the underlying standard lapses, if you breach the rules, or if a related declaration becomes false.
4.2 PetWash may update Academy content and badge requirements; you may need to retrain to keep a badge active.

## 5. How badges are used
5.1 Badges help Members choose and help PetWash gate sensitive services. A required-but-missing badge may block bookings via `requireProviderServiceApproval(serviceType)`.

## 6. No off-platform use
6.1 Use PetWash badges only on the Platform and in line with brand rules. Do not use PetWash's name, marks, or badges to market yourself off-platform or in a misleading way.

## 7. Honesty
7.1 Faking, misusing, or misrepresenting Academy completion or badges is grounds for revocation, suspension, or termination per *provider-account-suspension-rules*.
