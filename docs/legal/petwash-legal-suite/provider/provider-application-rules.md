# Provider Application Rules

These rules govern how you apply to provide services through PET WASH LTD (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash").

## 1. Who may apply
1.1 You must be at least 18, legally entitled to live and work in Israel, and able to lawfully provide the service you apply for.
1.2 Each applicant is one natural person or one registered business entity. One account per provider.

## 2. What you must submit
2.1 Identity: full legal name and a valid government photo ID.
2.2 Contact: a phone number and email under your control.
2.3 Tax/business status (see *provider-tax-business-status-declaration*): עוסק פטור, עוסק מורשה, חברה בע״מ, individual, or foreign.
2.4 Service-specific evidence (licences, certificates, insurance) where required by the relevant manual or *provider-insurance-licence-status-declaration*.
2.5 The declarations required for your service type, completed truthfully.

## 3. Review and approval
3.1 PetWash reviews each application and each requested service type separately.
3.2 Approval is per service type. You may be approved for one service and not another.
3.3 PetWash may request more information, interview you, run lawful background or reference checks, or decline without giving reasons.
3.4 No booking or payout is enabled until the platform gate `requireProviderServiceApproval(serviceType)` records approval for that service type.

## 4. Accuracy and changes
4.1 All information must be true, current, and complete. False or misleading information is grounds for refusal, suspension, or termination.
4.2 You must update your information promptly when it changes (expired ID, changed tax status, lapsed insurance, new licence).
4.3 You must re-confirm your details periodically — see *provider-six-month-reconfirmation-declaration*.

## 5. No guarantee
5.1 Submitting an application does not guarantee approval, bookings, volume, or income.
5.2 PetWash may pause new approvals by region or service type.

Approval does not make you an employee or agent of PetWash. See *provider-independent-status-acknowledgement*.
