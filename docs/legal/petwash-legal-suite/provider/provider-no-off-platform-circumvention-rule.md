# Provider No-Off-Platform Circumvention Rule

This rule protects the integrity of the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. **517145033** ("PetWash"), and the safety, payment, insurance, and record-keeping that the Platform provides.

## 1. The rule
1.1 Members you meet through PetWash, and the bookings you receive through it, must be **kept on the Platform**. You must book, communicate, and be paid **through PetWash**.

## 2. What is prohibited
You must **not**:
2.1 Solicit, encourage, or agree with a Member to take a booking off-platform to avoid PetWash's fees, records, or protections.
2.2 Share or collect private contact details (phone, email, social, payment apps) for the purpose of booking or paying outside the Platform.
2.3 Accept or request **off-platform payment** (cash or transfer) for services that should run through the Platform.
2.4 Divert a Member introduced by PetWash to your own service or a third party's, during your engagement and for **twelve (12) months** after your last booking with that Member through PetWash.
2.5 Place leaflets, cards, or contact prompts that steer Members off-platform.

## 3. Why it matters
3.1 On-platform booking is what enables **safety records, incident handling, payment protection, dispute resolution, tax documentation, and (where applicable) cover**. Going off-platform strips these away and exposes the Member, the pet, and you.

## 4. Genuine pre-existing relationships
4.1 This rule does not restrict relationships you genuinely had **before and independent of** PetWash. It applies to Members and bookings that arise **through** the Platform.

## 5. Consequences
5.1 Circumvention is a **serious breach**. It may result in immediate suspension or termination, withheld or reversed payouts, recovery of fees PetWash would have earned, and any remedy available under Israeli law.

By accepting, I agree to keep PetWash bookings on the Platform.
