# Provider Payout Rules

These rules govern how you are paid for services provided through the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. **517145033** ("PetWash").

## 1. How payment flows
1.1 The Member pays **PetWash** through the Platform. PetWash facilitates payment, deducts its service fee, handles VAT according to your declared status, and remits your **payout**.
1.2 You are paid for **completed** bookings (and for any fee properly due under policy, e.g. a Member's late cancellation). You are not paid for bookings you cancel or do not complete, except as the policy provides.

## 2. When payout is released
2.1 Payout is released after the booking is **completed and confirmed** through the Platform (check-in/check-out recorded), subject to any standard hold or review window.
2.2 Each booking is paid **once**; payout is idempotent per booking — there is no double payment.
2.3 Payout may be **held or withheld** during dispute, suspected fraud, incident review, chargeback, or refund.

## 3. Clearance gates
3.1 Payout requires that you are **approved** for the service type (`requireProviderServiceApproval(serviceType)`) and that any required **insurance/licence, tax, and document** conditions are satisfied. Payout may be gated until these are met.
3.2 You must provide a **valid bank/payment destination** in your own name (or your registered business). Mismatched or unverifiable details delay payout.

## 4. Tax and VAT
4.1 Payout and any VAT handling follow your declared tax/business status — see *provider-tax-business-status-declaration*.
4.2 In the absence of a valid withholding-exemption certificate, statutory **withholding** may be applied. You are responsible for your own taxes and reporting.

## 5. Adjustments and recovery
5.1 PetWash may **adjust, offset, or reverse** payouts for refunds, chargebacks, duplicate payments, fraud, circumvention, or amounts you owe PetWash.
5.2 If you are overpaid in error, you must repay; PetWash may recover it from future payouts.

## 6. Records and disputes
6.1 Your payout statement is available on the Platform. Raise any payout dispute through PetWash within a reasonable time; do not collect or net amounts off-platform.

## 7. No employment pay
7.1 Payouts are contractor payments, **not wages**. No salary, benefits, severance, or social contributions are owed — see *provider-independent-status-acknowledgement*.
