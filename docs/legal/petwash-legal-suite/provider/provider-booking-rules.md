# Provider Booking Rules

These rules govern how you accept and perform bookings on the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash").

## 1. Only approved services
1.1 You may accept bookings **only** for service types you are approved for. The gate `requireProviderServiceApproval(serviceType)` blocks bookings outside your approval.

## 2. Accepting
2.1 You choose which bookings to accept. Once accepted, a booking is a commitment to the Member.
2.2 Do not accept a booking you cannot safely or competently perform, or that conflicts with another booking. **No double-booking** — do not accept overlapping bookings.
2.3 Review the pet's details, the location, and any special needs before accepting. If you need more information, ask through the Platform before accepting.

## 3. Pricing through the Platform
3.1 The **price is set and confirmed through the Platform**. Perform the service for the agreed scope and price.
3.2 Do not demand extra cash, tips as a condition, or off-platform payment. Genuine extra services must be approved and recorded through the Platform (e.g. an add-on or quote), not collected privately.

## 4. Performing the booking
4.1 Arrive on time, **start and finish via the Platform** (check-in/check-out), perform the full agreed service, and follow the applicable Service Manual and care plan.
4.2 Communicate through the Platform and keep the Member informed.

## 5. Location and identity
5.1 Confirm you are at the correct address and serving the correct pet. Do not let anyone else perform your booking in your place; the approved provider must perform the service.

## 6. Problems
6.1 If you cannot complete a booking, follow *provider-cancellation-rules*. If something goes wrong, follow *provider-incident-reporting-manual*.

## 7. No circumvention
7.1 Keep the booking on-platform. Do not move the Member off-platform now or for future bookings — see *provider-no-off-platform-circumvention-rule*.

Breach of these rules may affect payout, rating, and access.
