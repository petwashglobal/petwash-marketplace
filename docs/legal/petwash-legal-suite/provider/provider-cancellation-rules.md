# Provider Cancellation Rules

These rules apply when you cancel or cannot complete a booking accepted on the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash").

## 1. Honour what you accept
1.1 Accepting a booking is a commitment. Cancel only when genuinely necessary.

## 2. How to cancel
2.1 Cancel **through the Platform as early as possible** and give an honest reason. Do not simply fail to appear ("no-show").
2.2 The earlier you cancel, the less harm to the Member. Late cancellations and no-shows are recorded and may affect your rating, eligibility, and access.

## 3. Safety and legitimate cancellations
3.1 You may decline or end a booking on **genuine safety grounds** — e.g. an aggressive animal beyond your competence, an unsafe environment, undisclosed risk, or your own illness/emergency. Record the reason and, where relevant, file an incident report (*provider-incident-reporting-manual*).
3.2 Where the pet or situation is materially different from what was booked (undisclosed aggression, medical condition, or hazard), you may decline; document it.

## 4. Member cancellations
4.1 When a Member cancels, the Member-side cancellation/refund policy applies. Your payout for a Member-cancelled booking follows *provider-payout-rules*.

## 5. Payout effect
5.1 Provider cancellations and no-shows are generally **not paid**, except where a fee is due under the applicable policy (e.g. a Member's late cancellation). Payout is determined by the Platform per *provider-payout-rules*.
5.2 Do not collect any cancellation fee directly from a Member; all fees flow through the Platform.

## 6. Patterns
6.1 Repeated late cancellations or no-shows are a serious reliability failure and grounds for suspension or removal per *provider-account-suspension-rules*.

## 7. Do not abandon
7.1 Never abandon a pet in your care. If you must stop mid-booking, ensure the pet is safe and handed back to the Member or an authorised person, and notify PetWash.
