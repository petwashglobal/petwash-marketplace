# Provider Incident Reporting Manual

This manual tells you what to do when something goes wrong on a booking made through the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash"). Reporting is mandatory.

## 1. What is an incident
1.1 Any event affecting safety, health, or property, including: injury to a pet, person, or yourself; a bite or aggression; an escape or lost pet; a road/traffic event; a pet falling ill or needing a vet; property damage; a key/access problem; a near-miss; or any allegation by a Member.

## 2. Immediate steps (in order)
2.1 **Protect life and safety first** — secure the pet and people, prevent further harm.
2.2 **Get help** — call a veterinarian for the pet, or emergency services (police/ambulance/fire) for people, as the situation needs.
2.3 **Notify the Member** as soon as it is safe to do so, honestly and clearly.
2.4 **Notify PetWash** as soon as practicable, and the **same day** at the latest.

## 3. File the report on the Platform
3.1 Submit an incident report **through the Platform** with: date/time, location, the booking, what happened, who/what was involved, actions you took, any vet/medical help, and any witnesses.
3.2 Attach **evidence** — photos/video where appropriate and consistent with *provider-photo-media-rules*, vet documents, receipts.
3.3 Report **all** incidents, including those with no apparent harm (near-misses) and minor ones. Do not decide on your own that an incident is "too small".

## 4. Honesty and no cover-up
4.1 Report truthfully and promptly. **Do not conceal, delay, alter, or destroy** information about an incident. A cover-up is treated more seriously than the incident itself.

## 5. Serious incidents
5.1 For a bite, serious injury, lost pet, or death, notify PetWash **immediately** and cooperate fully with any review or with the authorities.

## 6. After the report
6.1 Cooperate with PetWash's review. Payout for the affected booking may be held pending review.
6.2 Keep details confidential per *provider-privacy-confidentiality-rules*; do not post about the incident publicly.

Failure to report, or a cover-up, is grounds for suspension or termination per *provider-account-suspension-rules*.
