# Provider Safety Code

This Safety Code applies to every provider on the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash"). Following it is a condition of providing services. Service-specific safety rules are in each Service Manual.

## 1. Animal safety first
1.1 Put the **welfare and safety of the animal** above speed or convenience. Never use force, fear, pain, or punishment-based handling.
1.2 Know and watch for **signs of stress, fear, illness, injury, heat stress, or aggression**. Stop and reassess if an animal is in distress.
1.3 Never leave an animal in a parked vehicle, in unsafe heat or cold, or unsupervised in a hazardous environment.

## 2. People and public safety
2.1 Keep animals under control at all times. Use a secure, appropriate leash/harness in public; never let a dog off-leash unless the booking and law allow it and it is safe.
2.2 Prevent bites, escapes, and incidents involving other people, children, and other animals. Yield to and protect the public.
2.3 Comply with municipal rules (leashing, muzzling where required, waste removal, restricted areas).

## 3. Your own safety
3.1 Do not take on an animal or situation that is beyond your competence or that you reasonably believe is unsafe. You may decline or end a booking on genuine safety grounds (see *provider-cancellation-rules*).
3.2 Carry a charged phone and the emergency contacts for each booking.

## 4. No impairment
4.1 Never provide services while under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or any substance that impairs judgment or reaction.

## 5. Emergencies
5.1 In an emergency, act to protect life and safety first, seek veterinary or medical/emergency help as needed, then notify the Member and PetWash as soon as practicable.
5.2 Report every incident through the Platform per *provider-incident-reporting-manual*.

## 6. Hygiene and equipment
6.1 Keep equipment clean and safe. Follow infection-control and hygiene practices appropriate to the service.

## 7. Honesty about risk
7.1 Disclose to the Member, and record on the Platform, any condition, behaviour, or event that affects the animal's safety or care.

Breach of this Safety Code is grounds for suspension or termination per *provider-account-suspension-rules*.
