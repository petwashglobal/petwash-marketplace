# Provider Photo & Media Rules

These rules govern photos and video you take while providing services through the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash"). Photos build trust and document care — but they must be handled responsibly.

## 1. Purpose
1.1 Take photos/video only for **legitimate service purposes**: care updates, check-in/out, before/after, evidence for an incident, or proof of completion. Some services require photos (see each Service Manual).

## 2. What to capture
2.1 Focus on the **pet and the service**. Capture clear, honest images that genuinely reflect what happened.
2.2 **Do not** photograph the inside of a Member's home beyond what the service requires, valuables, documents, people without consent, children, or anything private. Respect *provider-privacy-confidentiality-rules*.

## 3. Authenticity
3.1 Do not stage, fake, edit deceptively, reuse old photos, or use another pet's image. Photos must be genuine and from the actual booking.

## 4. Where photos go
4.1 Upload service photos **through the Platform**, attached to the booking. The Member and PetWash may view them. Do not send service photos through private channels off-platform.

## 5. No personal or public use
5.1 Do not post, share publicly, sell, or use Member, pet, or home images for your **own marketing, social media, or any purpose** without the **Member's explicit consent and PetWash's permission**.
5.2 Never use images in a way that identifies a Member's home, address, or absence (security risk).

## 6. Consent and removal
6.1 If a Member asks you not to take photos, respect it (except where a photo is needed to document an incident). If a Member asks for an image to be removed, route the request to PetWash.

## 7. Licence to PetWash
7.1 By uploading, you grant PetWash a licence to store and use service media for operating the Platform, safety, dispute handling, and records, consistent with privacy law. You confirm you have the right to upload what you upload.

Misuse of photos/media is grounds for suspension or termination.
