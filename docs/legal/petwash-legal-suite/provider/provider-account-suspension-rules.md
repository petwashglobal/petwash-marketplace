# Provider Account Suspension & Termination Rules

These rules explain when and how PET WASH LTD (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash"), may suspend or terminate a provider's access.

## 1. Grounds
PetWash may suspend or terminate your access, in whole or for a service type, for reasons including:
1.1 Breach of the Provider Terms, a Service Manual, the Safety Code, or any provider rule or declaration.
1.2 A **safety risk** to pets, Members, or the public; mishandling; cruelty; or impairment.
1.3 **False, forged, or misleading** information or documents; failed verification; lapsed insurance/licence.
1.4 **Fraud, theft, or payout abuse**; **off-platform circumvention** (*provider-no-off-platform-circumvention-rule*).
1.5 Failure to report or a cover-up of an incident (*provider-incident-reporting-manual*).
1.6 Privacy or confidentiality breach; review manipulation; harassment or discrimination.
1.7 Persistent unreliability — late cancellations, no-shows, low ratings.
1.8 Legal or regulatory requirement, or loss of your legal right to provide.

## 2. Immediate vs. notice
2.1 For serious matters (safety, fraud, forgery, circumvention, cover-up), PetWash may suspend or terminate **immediately and without prior notice**.
2.2 For lesser matters, PetWash may warn, restrict, gate, or give you a chance to fix the issue before suspending.

## 3. Effect of suspension/termination
3.1 You may be unable to receive or perform new bookings; existing bookings may be reassigned or cancelled with the Member's protection in mind.
3.2 **Payout** for completed bookings is still due, subject to holds for dispute, fraud, refund, chargeback, or amounts you owe (*provider-payout-rules*).
3.3 Your confidentiality and no-circumvention obligations **survive** termination.

## 4. Review
4.1 You may ask PetWash to review a suspension/termination by contacting support. PetWash will consider genuine new information. PetWash's decision on platform access is final, subject to your rights under Israeli law.

## 5. Your right to leave
5.1 You may stop providing services at any time. Complete or properly cancel accepted bookings first (*provider-cancellation-rules*).

Suspension or termination does not, by itself, create or imply any employment relationship.
