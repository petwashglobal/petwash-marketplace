# Provider Review & Rating Rules

These rules govern reviews and ratings on the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash"). Honest reviews protect Members, pets, and good providers.

## 1. Reviews are honest and earned
1.1 Member reviews and ratings reflect their genuine experience. Your rating is built from real bookings.

## 2. No manipulation
2.1 Do **not** create fake reviews, write your own reviews, use other accounts, or arrange reviews from people who were not genuine Members.
2.2 Do **not** offer payment, discounts, gifts, refunds, or anything of value in exchange for a positive review or for removing/changing a review.
2.3 Do **not** pressure, coerce, threaten, harass, or retaliate against a Member over a review or rating, on or off the Platform.

## 3. Responding to reviews
3.1 You may respond professionally where the Platform allows. Stay courteous and factual; do **not** disclose private Member or household information (see *provider-privacy-confidentiality-rules*).

## 4. Disputed or unfair reviews
4.1 If you believe a review is fake, abusive, or violates the rules, **report it to PetWash** rather than acting yourself. PetWash may moderate, hold, or remove reviews that breach policy or law.
4.2 PetWash does not remove a review simply because it is negative; honest criticism stays.

## 5. How ratings are used
5.1 Ratings, completion rate, cancellations, no-shows, and incidents inform your eligibility, visibility, and access. Persistently low ratings or serious patterns may lead to suspension or removal per *provider-account-suspension-rules*.

## 6. Integrity
6.1 Review manipulation is a **serious breach** and may result in suspension, termination, and reversal of any benefit gained.
