# Provider Customer Care Standards

These standards define the level of care and conduct expected of every provider on the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash"). They protect Members, their pets, and the PetWash brand.

## 1. Professional conduct
1.1 Be punctual, courteous, and respectful to Members, their families, and the public.
1.2 Present yourself and communicate professionally. No harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or inappropriate conduct of any kind.

## 2. Communication
2.1 Communicate with Members **through the Platform** before, during, and after the booking. Respond to booking messages promptly.
2.2 Confirm bookings, give honest arrival/timing updates, and inform the Member without delay of any problem, delay, or concern about the pet.
2.3 Be honest. Never overstate what you did, hide a problem, or mislead a Member.

## 3. Reliability
3.1 Honour accepted bookings. Arrive on time, complete the full service, and follow the Member's reasonable instructions and the pet's care plan.
3.2 If you genuinely cannot attend, cancel as early as possible per *provider-cancellation-rules* so the Member can make other arrangements.

## 4. Care quality
4.1 Follow the applicable Service Manual and the pet's specific needs. Treat each pet gently and attentively.
4.2 Leave the Member's pet, home, and belongings as you found them or better.

## 5. Check-in / check-out and reporting
5.1 Use the Platform's start/finish and check-in features for each booking. Provide the photos and report required by the service (see *provider-photo-media-rules*).

## 6. Handling concerns
6.1 If a Member raises a concern, listen, stay professional, and try to resolve it. Escalate disputes to PetWash rather than arguing; do not retaliate.

## 7. Brand
7.1 Represent PetWash with care. Do not make promises on PetWash's behalf, misuse its name or marks, or behave in a way that harms the brand.

Repeated or serious failure to meet these standards may affect your rating, eligibility, and access per *provider-account-suspension-rules*.
