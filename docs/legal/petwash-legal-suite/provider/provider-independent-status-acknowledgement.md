# Provider Independent-Contractor Status Acknowledgement

I acknowledge and agree, in relation to **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. **517145033** ("PetWash"), as follows.

## 1. I am an independent contractor
1.1 I provide services as an **independent contractor / self-employed provider (נותן שירות עצמאי)**, and **not** as an employee, worker, agent, partner, joint-venturer, or franchisee of PetWash.
1.2 No employer–employee relationship (יחסי עובד–מעביד) exists or is intended between me and PetWash, now or in the future.

## 2. I control my own work
2.1 I decide **whether, when, and how often** to make myself available and to accept or decline bookings. PetWash does not require me to work or to be available.
2.2 I provide my own services using my own skill, judgment, methods, and (where applicable) my own equipment and supplies, subject only to the safety, brand, and platform rules below.
2.3 I may provide similar services to others, including outside the Platform, subject to *provider-no-off-platform-circumvention-rule*.

## 3. I bear my own business costs and taxes
3.1 I am responsible for my own **taxes, VAT, National Insurance (ביטוח לאומי), pension, business registration, expenses, and insurance**.
3.2 I am **not** entitled to salary, severance (פיצויי פיטורים), paid leave, sick pay, holiday pay, pension contributions, or any other employee benefit from PetWash.

## 4. PetWash still sets platform standards
4.1 I understand that requiring me to follow **safety rules, brand standards, service manuals, and platform policies** is consistent with my independent status and does **not** make me an employee. These protect pets, Members, and the brand.
4.2 PetWash may approve, gate, suspend, or remove me from the Platform without that creating an employment relationship.

## 5. Consequences
5.1 If any authority or court were to reclassify the relationship, I agree this Acknowledgement reflects the parties' genuine intent, and I will cooperate to address any resulting tax or contribution liability as between me and PetWash to the extent permitted by Israeli law.
