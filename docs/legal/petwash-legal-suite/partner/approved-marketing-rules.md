# Approved Marketing Rules

Rules for partner marketing that references PetWash. **PET WASH LTD** (no. 517145033).

## 1. Pre-approval
Any public-facing marketing that uses the PetWash™ brand (posts, signage, ads, flyers, press) must be **approved by Company in advance**.

## 2. Accurate claims only
- No "organic", "vet-approved", or "hypoallergenic" claims — products are pet-formulated, not certified organic.
- No guarantees, certifications, or outcomes not approved by Company.
- No comparisons to, or naming of, competitors.
- No medical/health claims about animals.

## 3. Brand integrity
Follow the [Brand-Use Rules](./brand-use-rules.md): official logo only, correct palette, no modification.

## 4. Consumer & advertising law
Marketing must comply with the Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981 (no misleading advertising) and applicable advertising standards. Prices/terms shown must be accurate.

## 5. Spam / direct marketing
Any electronic direct marketing (SMS/email) must comply with Section 30A of the Communications Law (anti-spam) — prior consent and opt-out. Partner must not use Company's customer data for marketing (partners have no access to it).

## 6. No use of customer data
Partners do not receive customer personal data and must not collect, scrape, or market to PetWash customers using platform data.

## 7. Local promotions
Site-level promotions (e.g. opening offers) require Company approval and must not misstate pricing handled through the platform/Nayax.

## 8. Takedown
Company may require correction or removal of any non-compliant material; Partner complies promptly.
