# Station Safety Responsibilities

Allocation of safety responsibilities for a PetWash station at a partner site. **PET WASH LTD** (no. 517145033).

## 1. Shared goal
Keep the public, their animals, and staff safe. Both parties act promptly on any hazard.

## 2. Company responsibilities
- Provide a unit that is safe when correctly installed and maintained.
- Maintain/service the unit per the [Maintenance Responsibility Matrix](./maintenance-responsibility-matrix.md).
- Respond to reported equipment faults; take the unit offline remotely (via platform availability) when a fault is reported.

## 3. Partner responsibilities
- Keep the **site** safe: lighting, flooring, drainage, no trip/slip hazards, secure surroundings.
- Maintain the site's electrical supply up to the connection point.
- Visually monitor the station during opening hours and report visible hazards immediately.
- Do not allow use of an obviously damaged or unsafe unit; restrict access and report.

## 4. Emergencies
On any electrical, water, fire, or injury hazard: make the area safe, restrict access, and report immediately per the [Local Incident Escalation Manual](./local-incident-escalation-manual.md). Call emergency services where life/limb is at risk.

## 5. No tampering
Partner must not open, repair, bypass safety devices, or modify the unit. Only Company technicians service internals (wet + electrical environment).

## 6. Insurance
Each party maintains insurance appropriate to its responsibilities (Company: equipment/product; Partner: premises/public liability). Proof on request.

## 7. Liability
Liability for a defect follows the responsible party: equipment defect → Company; site/premises hazard or tampering → Partner. Final allocation per the [Location-Partner Terms](./location-partner-terms.md) and Israeli law.
