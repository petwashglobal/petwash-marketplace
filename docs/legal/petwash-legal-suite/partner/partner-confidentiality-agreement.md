# Partner Confidentiality Agreement

Between **PET WASH LTD** (no. 517145033) ("Company") and the location partner ("Partner").

## 1. Confidential Information
Non-public information the Partner learns through the relationship: Company fee/revenue structures, platform data, pricing logic, technical/operational details, business plans, and any data shown in the partner dashboard.

## 2. Assigned-location scope
Partner is given access **only to its own assigned location(s)**. Partner must not seek, accept, or use any company-wide data, other partners' data, or customer personal data. If Partner is ever exposed to such data in error, it must report and not use it.

## 3. No customer personal data
Partner does not receive end-customer personal data. Partner must not attempt to collect, derive, or retain customer personal data from the station or platform.

## 4. Obligations
- Use Confidential Information only to perform the hosting relationship.
- Do not disclose to third parties or use for any other business.
- Protect it with reasonable security.

## 5. Duration
Confidentiality survives termination: indefinitely for trade secrets; for the statutory retention period for any records.

## 6. Privacy law
To the extent Partner ever processes any personal data in connection with the site, it complies with the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 (incl. Amendment 13).

## 7. Breach
Breach entitles Company to injunctive relief and damages under Israeli law and may terminate the hosting relationship.

## 8. Governing law
Israeli law; Tel Aviv district courts.
