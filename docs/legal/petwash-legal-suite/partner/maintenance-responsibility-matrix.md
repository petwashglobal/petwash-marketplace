## Rules
1. **No partner self-repair of the unit.** Partner reports; Company services. Unauthorised works void related liability.
2. **Reporting SLA.** Partner reports faults/hazards promptly (same day for safety items) via the dashboard/incident channel.
3. **Access.** Partner ensures technician access during agreed hours; emergency access any time.
4. **Consumables.** Company-supplied consumables are Company's; site-supplied utilities are Partner's.
5. **Disputes** over responsibility are resolved per the [Location-Partner Terms](./location-partner-terms.md).
