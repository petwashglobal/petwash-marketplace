# Licensed-Operator Agreement — Draft Structure

**Structure/outline only** for a future licensed-operator (franchise-style) agreement between **PET WASH LTD** (no. 517145033) and an operator. This is a drafting skeleton, **not** an executable contract; each clause requires CLO drafting and negotiation. Signature level 3 (full execution) applies to the final agreement.

## Intended clause structure

1. **Parties & definitions** — Company, Operator, Licensed Territory/Site, Station, System, Marks.
2. **Grant of licence** — limited, revocable right to operate PetWash station(s) at defined site(s) using the System and Marks; non-exclusive unless expressly stated; no rights beyond the grant.
3. **Term & renewal** — initial term, renewal conditions, no automatic perpetual rights.
4. **Fees & revenue model** — licence/operating fee, revenue-share, payment terms, tax (VAT, osek requirement before payouts).
5. **Operator obligations** — standards of operation, site upkeep, safety, customer service, compliance with all PetWash manuals.
6. **Company obligations** — equipment, platform, training, support, brand assets.
7. **Brand & IP** — use only per [Brand-Use Rules](./brand-use-rules.md); no modification; reversion on termination.
8. **Confidentiality & data** — assigned-location scope only; no customer personal data; Amendment 13 compliance.
9. **Insurance & liability** — required cover; indemnities; allocation per safety responsibilities.
10. **Quality & audit** — Company inspection rights for the operator's own site(s) only.
11. **Marketing** — pre-approval per [Approved Marketing Rules](./approved-marketing-rules.md); no competitor naming; accurate claims only.
12. **Compliance** — consumer law, advertising law, municipal licensing, tax.
13. **Restrictions** — non-circumvention; reasonable, enforceable non-compete consistent with Israeli law (overbroad restraints unenforceable).
14. **Termination** — for cause, for convenience (notice), insolvency; consequences; station removal per [Station Termination & Removal Rules](./station-termination-removal-rules.md).
15. **Dispute resolution & governing law** — Israeli law; Tel Aviv district courts (or arbitration if agreed).
16. **General** — assignment, notices, entire agreement, Hebrew controlling.

## Drafting notes
- Disclose franchise economics fairly; avoid misleading pre-contract representations (Israeli good-faith duty, Contract Law 5733-1973).
- Keep restraints proportionate; Israeli courts narrow overbroad non-competes.
- Final text and exhibits drafted/approved by CLO before any signature.
