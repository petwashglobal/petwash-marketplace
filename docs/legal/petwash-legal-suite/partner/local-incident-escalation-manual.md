# Local Incident Escalation Manual

Operational manual for partners handling and escalating incidents at their own PetWash site. **PET WASH LTD** (no. 517145033).

## 1. Scope
Covers what the partner does at its site when something goes wrong. The partner handles only its own location; it does not access or act on other locations.

## 2. Safety first
1. Protect people and animals: make the area safe, restrict access to a faulty/unsafe unit.
2. For electrical/water/fire/injury hazards or any risk to life/limb — **call emergency services first**, then notify Company.
3. Do not attempt to repair, open, or bypass the unit.

## 3. Report to Company — immediately for safety
Report via the dashboard incident channel (or the emergency contact for urgent safety issues). Include: site, time, what happened, whether anyone/any animal was hurt, and what you have done.

## 4. Severity
| Severity | Examples | Partner action |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| Critical | Injury, electrical/fire/water hazard | Make safe, call emergency services, restrict access, notify Company now |
| High | Visible equipment damage, leak | Restrict access, report same day |
| Medium | Repeated payment/connectivity failures | Report; Company diagnoses (often Nayax) |
| Low | Cosmetic/cleanliness | Address site cleanliness; log |

## 5. Equipment vs site
- Equipment fault → Company services (do not self-repair).
- Site hazard (lighting, flooring, drainage) → Partner remedies promptly.
See the [Maintenance Responsibility Matrix](./maintenance-responsibility-matrix.md).

## 6. Customers
Be courteous; do not collect customer personal data; direct payment/booking disputes to PetWash support (partners cannot issue refunds or see customer accounts).

## 7. Records & confidentiality
Keep a simple local log of incidents at your site. Incident details are confidential; do not publicise or post about them.

## 8. Follow-up
Cooperate with Company's investigation and any corrective action at your site.
