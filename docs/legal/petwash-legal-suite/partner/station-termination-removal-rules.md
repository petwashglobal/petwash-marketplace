# Station Termination & Removal Rules

Rules for ending a hosting/operator relationship and removing a PetWash station. **PET WASH LTD** (no. 517145033).

## 1. Grounds for termination
- Expiry/non-renewal of the [Location-Partner Terms](./location-partner-terms.md).
- Termination for convenience with the agreed notice.
- For cause: safety breach, brand misuse, confidentiality breach, non-payment, loss of site rights, or false declaration.

## 2. Notice
Notice periods follow the signed agreement. Safety/legal grounds may permit immediate suspension of the station (taken offline) pending removal.

## 3. Final accounting
On termination, a final revenue-share report and settlement is prepared for the partner's location only (per [Revenue-Share Report Rules](./revenue-share-report-rules.md)). Outstanding amounts each way are reconciled.

## 4. Removal of the station
- Only Company technicians remove/relocate the unit.
- Partner provides access for removal within the agreed window.
- Removal includes the Nayax module, branding, and signage. The unit remains Company property.

## 5. Site reinstatement
Reinstatement of the site (electrical/plumbing make-good) is allocated per the agreement; ordinary wear excepted.

## 6. Brand & access wind-down
- All PetWash™ brand use ceases; branded materials removed/returned ([Brand-Use Rules](./brand-use-rules.md)).
- Partner dashboard access is revoked on or before the effective date.

## 7. Surviving obligations
Confidentiality, data, IP, and any accrued payment obligations survive termination. Records retained per tax law (7 years).

## 8. No customer disruption mid-removal
The station is marked unavailable before physical removal so no bookings/payments are taken on a unit being removed.
