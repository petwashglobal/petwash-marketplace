# Brand-Use Rules

Rules for partner use of the PetWash™ brand. **PET WASH LTD** (no. 517145033).

## 1. Ownership
The PetWash™ name, logo, and visual identity are the Company's intellectual property. Partner receives a limited, revocable, non-exclusive permission to reference hosting a PetWash station — nothing more.

## 2. Use the real logo only
Use **only** the official PetWash™ logo asset supplied by Company. Do **not** recreate, redraw, recolour, distort, or modify it. Keep clear space; do not place it on clashing backgrounds.

## 3. Brand palette
The brand is pure white background, black text, and bright metallic gold (#D4AF37) accents only. Do not introduce other golds, cream/ivory, brown, or off-palette colours in PetWash-branded materials.

## 4. Permitted references
Partner may state, factually, that it hosts a PetWash™ self-service station at its site. Partner must not imply ownership of the brand, franchise rights (unless separately granted), endorsement beyond the hosting relationship, or any certification/guarantee.

## 5. Prohibited
- Registering domains, social handles, or business names using the PetWash mark.
- Altering the logo or creating sub-brands.
- Using the brand in misleading, off-brand, or competitor-comparative messaging.

## 6. Approval
Any public-facing material using the PetWash brand requires prior Company approval per the [Approved Marketing Rules](./approved-marketing-rules.md).

## 7. Termination
On termination of the hosting/operator relationship, all brand use ceases and branded materials are removed/returned.

## 8. Enforcement
Unauthorised brand use is an IP infringement under Israeli trademark law and a breach of this agreement.
