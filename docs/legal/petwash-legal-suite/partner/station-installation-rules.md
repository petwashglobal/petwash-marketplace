# Station Installation Rules

Rules governing installation of a PetWash station at a partner site. **PET WASH LTD** (no. 517145033).

## 1. Who installs
Only Company-authorised technicians install, relocate, or remove the station. Partner must not move, modify, or open the unit.

## 2. Electrical & plumbing
- Electrical connection by a licensed electrician to Israeli standards (IEC/SI wiring code); RCD/earth protection required given the wet environment.
- Water supply and drainage connected per the unit's requirements and local plumbing code.
- The Nayax payment module is connected and provisioned by Company.

## 3. Siting
- Stable, level base able to bear the unit; safe public approach; no trip/slip hazards.
- Adequate ventilation and drainage; protection from flooding where outdoors.
- Clear of fire exits and accessibility paths.

## 4. Signage & branding
Approved PetWash™ signage and safety/usage instructions are installed by or with Company approval per the [Brand-Use Rules](./brand-use-rules.md). Partner does not add or alter station signage.

## 5. Commissioning
Before going live: power and connectivity confirmed, Nayax payment tested, safety check passed, and the station marked available in the platform. A commissioning record is kept.

## 6. Compliance
Installation must comply with municipal/planning permissions obtained for the site (see [Site-Access Declaration](./site-access-declaration.md)).

## 7. Changes
Any later change (relocation, electrical works, decommission) goes through Company; Partner must not perform unauthorised works on the unit.
