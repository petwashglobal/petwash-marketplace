# Site-Access Declaration

Declaration by the location partner ("Partner") to **PET WASH LTD** (no. 517145033).

## 1. Right to grant access
Partner declares it has the legal right (owner / authorised lessee / authorised manager) to grant Company access to the site at [address] and to permit installation and operation of a PetWash station there.

## 2. Permitted access
Partner grants Company and its authorised technicians reasonable access to install, inspect, service, repair, and remove the station, and to restore power/connectivity. Emergency access (safety) is permitted at any time.

## 3. Site readiness
Partner declares the site has, or will have by the agreed date: adequate space, a compliant electrical supply, water and drainage (as required by the unit), and lawful access for the public.

## 4. Permissions & licensing
Partner declares it has obtained, or will obtain, the municipal/planning permissions required to host the station at the site. Site-level regulatory compliance is the Partner's responsibility.

## 5. No conflicting rights
Partner declares the site is not subject to any lease, mortgage, or third-party right that prevents hosting the station, or that all required consents have been obtained.

## 6. Notification of change
Partner will notify Company promptly of any change affecting access (sale of premises, lease termination, works, closures).

## 7. Truth of declaration
Partner declares the above is true and complete. A false declaration may void the hosting arrangement and create liability under Israeli contract law.
