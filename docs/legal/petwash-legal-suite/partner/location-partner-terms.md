# Location-Partner Terms

Agreement between **PET WASH LTD** (company no. 517145033) ("Company") and the location partner/host ("Partner") for hosting a PetWash self-service station.

## 1. Purpose
Partner provides a physical site; Company installs and operates a PetWash station. Partner is a **host**, not an employee, agent, or franchisee of the Company (unless a separate franchise/operator agreement is signed).

## 2. The site
Partner grants Company and its technicians access to the agreed site/area to install, power, service, and operate the station. Partner ensures the site is suitable (space, drainage, electrical supply, access).

## 3. Ownership
The station, brand, software, and data remain the Company's property. Partner acquires no rights in them.

## 4. Revenue share
Company and Partner share revenue per the agreed schedule (Exhibit A). Figures and reporting follow the [Revenue-Share Report Rules](./revenue-share-report-rules.md). Partner sees only their own location's figures.

## 5. Responsibilities
- Partner: keep the site safe, accessible, powered, and lawful (municipal permissions for the site).
- Company: maintain the station per the [Maintenance Responsibility Matrix](./maintenance-responsibility-matrix.md).

## 6. Confidentiality & data
Partner is bound by the [Partner Confidentiality Agreement](./partner-confidentiality-agreement.md) and sees only assigned-location data — never company-wide or customer personal data.

## 7. Brand
Partner may reference hosting a PetWash station only per the [Brand-Use Rules](./brand-use-rules.md). No unauthorised use of the PetWash™ mark.

## 8. Term & termination
Term and notice are per Exhibit A. On termination, station removal follows the [Station Termination & Removal Rules](./station-termination-removal-rules.md).

## 9. Liability & insurance
Each party maintains appropriate insurance. Allocation of liability for site vs. equipment is set in the safety responsibilities document.

## 10. Governing law
Israeli law; Tel Aviv district courts have exclusive jurisdiction.
