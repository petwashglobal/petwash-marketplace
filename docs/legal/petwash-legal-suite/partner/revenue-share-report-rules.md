# Revenue-Share Report Rules

How revenue-share is reported to location partners. **PET WASH LTD** (no. 517145033).

## 1. Scope — assigned location only
A partner's report shows **only their own assigned location(s)**: gross station revenue, applicable fees, the partner share, and the period. Partners never see company-wide totals, other locations, or any customer personal data.

## 2. Source of truth
Station revenue is measured through Nayax/platform transaction records. Reported figures derive from settled transactions, net of refunds and chargebacks.

## 3. Report contents
Each period report includes: location, period, number of transactions, gross revenue, deductions (fees/refunds), partner-share rate, and partner-share amount. No personal data of end users is included.

## 4. Cadence & payment
Reports issue per the agreed cadence (e.g. monthly). Partner-share is paid per the schedule in the [Location-Partner Terms](./location-partner-terms.md), subject to the partner having valid invoicing (osek) where required by Israeli tax law.

## 5. Tax
The partner-share is consideration that the Partner must invoice/report per their own tax status. Company issues official documents via its official issuer (SUMIT) as applicable. VAT handled per law.

## 6. Adjustments
Refunds, chargebacks, and corrections are reflected in the period they settle; prior over/under-payments are reconciled transparently and shown on the report.

## 7. Queries & audit
Partner may query their own figures. Company retains transaction records (7 years, tax law) and can substantiate the report for the partner's location only.

## 8. Confidentiality
Reports are confidential. Partner must not disclose Company fee structures or platform data beyond their own figures.
