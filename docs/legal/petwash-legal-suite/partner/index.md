# Location-Partner & Franchise Legal Suite (§3G)

Legal documents and operational rules for location partners, hosts, and franchise candidates of **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel. Governing law: Israel.

> These documents are **DRAFTS** and **not binding** until signed off by the CLO / legal. Hebrew is the controlling language once translated.

## Core principle — data scoping

A location partner sees **only their own assigned location(s)** — their station(s), their revenue-share figures, their local incidents. Partners **never** see company-wide data, other partners' locations, customer personal data, or aggregate financials. Dashboard access is gated by `partner confidentiality + assigned-location`.
