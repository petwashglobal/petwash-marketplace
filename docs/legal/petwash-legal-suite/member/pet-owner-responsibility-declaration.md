# Pet Owner Responsibility Declaration

This Declaration is made by you to **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel. By confirming, you declare that:

## 1. Ownership and authority
You are the owner of the pet, or are authorised by the owner to use PetWash Services for it, and you are aged 18 or over.

## 2. Health and fitness
To the best of your knowledge, your pet is healthy and fit for the Service booked, is not contagious, is not suffering from a condition that the Service could worsen, and has any vaccinations reasonably expected for the Service. You will disclose relevant health, age, pregnancy, or medication factors when asked.

## 3. Behaviour
You have disclosed any history of biting, aggression, severe anxiety, or other behaviour that could pose a risk to people, animals, or property. You will inform staff or the Provider of such factors before the Service.

## 4. Supervision and control
For self-service stations, you remain in control of and responsible for your pet throughout and will follow the Self-Service Wash Safety Rules. You will not leave your pet unattended in equipment.

## 5. Emergencies
You authorise PetWash staff or the Provider to take reasonable steps, including seeking veterinary care, if your pet appears to need urgent help, and you accept responsibility for resulting veterinary costs unless caused by our proven negligence.

## 6. Responsibility for harm
You accept responsibility for injury or damage caused by your pet to people, animals, property, or equipment, except to the extent caused by PetWash's proven negligence.

## 7. Accuracy
The information you provide about your pet is accurate. Withholding or misstating material information may affect safety and may limit any claim against PetWash.

## 8. No blanket guarantee
You understand PetWash provides facilities and a booking platform and **does not guarantee any particular outcome and does not represent that any Service is risk-free or "covered for everything."** Suitability of a Service for your specific pet is a judgement only you can make.

## 9. Confirmation
By confirming with a one-time code and your name, you adopt this Declaration as your own statement. It is read with the Member Terms and Station Use Terms.

## 10. Language
This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls. Contact: **support@petwash.co.il**.
