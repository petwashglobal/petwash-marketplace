# Privacy Notice

## 1. Controller
**PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel, is the controller of your personal data. This Notice explains how we collect, use, and protect it under the Israeli **Protection of Privacy Law**, including **Amendment 13**. Contact: **support@petwash.co.il**.

## 2. What we collect
- **Identity and contact**: name, email, phone, and (where required for tax/identity) national ID.
- **Account and usage**: bookings, station use, wallet pass, loyalty activity, device and app logs.
- **Payment**: handled by our payment partners; we store transaction records and tax documents, not full card numbers.
- **Pet data**: details you add about your pet (see the Pet Profile and Health-Data Notice).
- **Communications**: messages with support or Providers, and consent records.

## 3. Why we use it (purposes and legal basis)
We use your data to: create and manage your account; deliver and support Services; process payments and issue tax documents; operate the wallet pass and loyalty programme; ensure safety and security; comply with legal duties (including tax retention); and, only with your separate consent, send marketing. Our basis is performance of our contract with you, your consent where required, our legitimate operational interests, and compliance with law.

## 4. Sharing
We share data with: Providers, only as needed to deliver a booked service; payment, messaging, hosting, and analytics processors acting on our instructions; and authorities where legally required. We do not sell your personal data.

## 5. International transfer
Some processors operate outside Israel. Where data is transferred abroad, we use providers and safeguards consistent with Israeli privacy law.

## 6. Retention
We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Tax and accounting records are retained for the period required by Israeli law (currently seven years). Other data is deleted or anonymised when no longer needed.

## 7. Security
We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data. No system is perfectly secure; we work to reduce and respond to risks, and will notify affected users and the authorities as required by law in the event of a notifiable security event.

## 8. Your rights
Under Israeli privacy law you may request access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, and deletion where applicable, and may withdraw consent for optional processing (such as marketing). To exercise these rights, see the Data Request and Privacy Rights Policy or email support@petwash.co.il. You are not obliged to provide data, but some Services cannot be delivered without it.

## 9. Cookies and tracking
Our website uses cookies and similar technologies as described in the Cookie Notice.

## 10. Children
The Platform is not directed at people under 18.

## 11. Changes
We may update this Notice; material changes are notified in-app or by email, and the current version governs.

## 12. Language and contact
This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls. Privacy contact: **support@petwash.co.il**.
