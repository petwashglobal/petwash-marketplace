# Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Scope
This Policy sets the rules for using the Platform and Services of **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel. It is part of the Member Terms. Contact: **support@petwash.co.il**.

## 2. General conduct
Use the Platform and Services only for lawful purposes and as intended. Treat staff, Providers, other members, animals, equipment, and premises with respect and care.

## 3. Prohibited activities
You must not:
- break any law or infringe any third party's rights;
- provide false identity, payment, or pet information, or use another person's account;
- tamper with, damage, overload, or misuse stations, equipment, or the Platform;
- attempt to access, scrape, reverse-engineer, or disrupt the Platform or its security;
- use the Services to harm, neglect, or endanger an animal, or to wash animals the Service is not intended for;
- commit or facilitate fraud, chargeback abuse, voucher/loyalty abuse, or money laundering;
- harass, threaten, or abuse staff, Providers, or others;
- resell, sublicense, or commercially exploit the Services or PetWash brand without permission.

## 4. Provider interactions
When dealing with independent Providers, keep communication and payment within the Platform where the Service requires it, and follow safety and conduct rules. PetWash facilitates these interactions but is not the Provider.

## 5. Security and accounts
Keep your credentials and wallet pass secure, and report suspected compromise promptly. You are responsible for activity under your account.

## 6. Monitoring and enforcement
We may investigate suspected breaches and take proportionate action, including warning, restricting features, reversing abusive transactions, suspending or closing accounts (per the Account Suspension and Termination Policy), and reporting unlawful activity to authorities.

## 7. Reporting
Report misuse or safety concerns to support@petwash.co.il.

## 8. Law and language
Israeli law governs. This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls.
