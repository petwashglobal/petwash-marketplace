# Reviews and User Content Policy

## 1. Scope
This Policy applies when you submit reviews, ratings, photos, or other content ("**User Content**") to the Platform operated by **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel. Contact: **support@petwash.co.il**.

## 2. Honest, first-hand reviews
Reviews must reflect your genuine, first-hand experience of a Service. Do not post fake, paid-for, incentivised-but-undisclosed, or competitor-driven reviews.

## 3. Prohibited content
Do not submit content that is unlawful, defamatory, harassing, hateful, obscene, deceptive, infringing, privacy-violating, or that contains another person's personal data without their consent, malware, or spam.

## 4. Your responsibility and rights
You confirm you own or are entitled to submit your User Content and that it does not infringe others' rights. You keep your rights in it but grant PetWash a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to host, display, reproduce, and use it to operate and promote the Services, including after you delete it where copies remain in backups or legitimate records.

## 5. Moderation
We may, but are not obliged to, review content. We may decline, edit for length/format, hide, or remove content that breaches this Policy or the law, and may remove content following a justified complaint.

## 6. No endorsement
User Content reflects the author, not PetWash. We do not adopt or verify every statement made by users.

## 7. Reporting
Report content you believe breaches this Policy or your rights to support@petwash.co.il with details, so we can assess it.

## 8. Privacy
Content and related data are handled under the Privacy Notice (Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, including Amendment 13).

## 9. Consequences
Breaching this Policy may lead to content removal and account action under the Account Suspension and Termination Policy.

## 10. Law and language
Israeli law governs. This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls.
