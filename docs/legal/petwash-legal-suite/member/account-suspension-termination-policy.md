# Account Suspension and Termination Policy

## 1. Scope
This Policy explains when and how **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel, may suspend or close a member account, and your right to close yours. It is part of the Member Terms. Contact: **support@petwash.co.il**.

## 2. Grounds for action
We may suspend or close an account where there is: breach of the Member Terms, Acceptable Use Policy, or Safety Rules; fraud, chargeback or voucher/loyalty abuse, or money laundering concerns; non-payment of amounts due; risk to safety of people, animals, staff, or Providers; provision of false information; or a legal or regulatory requirement.

## 3. Proportionate measures
Depending on the issue we may: issue a warning; restrict specific features (e.g. wallet pass, station access, loyalty); suspend the account temporarily; or close it permanently. We aim to choose the least disruptive measure that addresses the issue.

## 4. Notice and urgency
Where practicable we give notice and a chance to respond or remedy. For serious or urgent risks (e.g. fraud or safety), we may act immediately and explain afterwards.

## 5. Effect of closure
On closure, your right to use the Services ends. We handle any refundable wallet balance per the Wallet Pass Terms and Refund and Service Recovery Policy, settle outstanding amounts, and deal with loyalty points per the Loyalty Program Terms. We retain records required by law (including tax records for the legally required period).

## 6. Member-initiated closure
You may close your account at any time via support@petwash.co.il or account settings, after settling amounts due. Some records are retained as required by law.

## 7. Reinstatement and appeal
If you believe action was taken in error, contact support@petwash.co.il. We will review reasonable appeals fairly. Reinstatement is at our discretion and may require conditions.

## 8. Privacy
Account and enforcement records are handled under the Privacy Notice (Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, including Amendment 13).

## 9. Law and language
Israeli law governs; the Tel Aviv–Jaffa district courts have jurisdiction as set out in the Member Terms. This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls.
