# Station Use Terms

## 1. Scope
These Terms govern your use of PetWash self-service wash stations operated by **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel. They are read with the Member Terms and the Self-Service Wash Safety Rules. Contact: **support@petwash.co.il**.

## 2. Self-service nature
Stations are unattended, self-service equipment. You operate the station yourself and remain in control of and responsible for your pet at all times. PetWash does not handle or supervise your pet.

## 3. Eligibility and condition of pet
Use a station only if your pet is healthy enough to be washed, not aggressive, not in obvious distress, and free of conditions that washing could worsen. You confirm this each time you use a station (see the Pet Owner Responsibility Declaration).

## 4. Payment and pricing
You pay per session via the app, wallet pass, or station payment device. Prices are in ₪ and include VAT at 18% unless stated. A session is consumed on use. A tax document is issued for each charge.

## 5. Proper use
Follow all on-station and in-app instructions and the Safety Rules. Use the station and its consumables only as intended, for domestic pets, and keep the time limits shown. Do not tamper with, overload, or misuse the equipment, or use it for anything other than washing a pet.

## 6. Faults and malfunctions
If a station malfunctions, stop, secure your pet, and report it via in-app support with the station ID. Charges for a cycle not delivered due to our fault are refunded or re-credited per the Refund and Service Recovery Policy.

## 7. Emergencies
If your pet shows distress, stop immediately and remove your pet. In a medical emergency seek veterinary help; you authorise reasonable emergency steps and are responsible for related costs unless caused by our proven negligence.

## 8. Hygiene and shared facility
Leave the bay clean for the next user. You are responsible for damage you, your pet, or your misuse causes to the station or premises.

## 9. Liability — no blanket guarantee
To the extent permitted by Israeli law, our liability for station use is limited to the amount paid for the affected session. We are **not liable for harm arising from your handling of your pet, your failure to follow the Safety Rules, or your pet's pre-existing condition or behaviour.** We do not represent stations as "guaranteed safe for every pet"; suitability depends on your pet, which only you can assess. Nothing limits liability for our negligence that cannot lawfully be limited.

## 10. CCTV and security
Stations or premises may use CCTV for safety and security; footage is handled under the Privacy Notice.

## 11. Suspension
We may restrict station access for misuse, safety, or non-payment, per the Account Suspension and Termination Policy.

## 12. Law and language
Israeli law governs; the Tel Aviv–Jaffa district courts have exclusive jurisdiction. This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls.
