# Pet Profile and Health-Data Notice

## 1. Purpose
This Notice explains how **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel, handles the information you add to a pet profile. It supplements the Privacy Notice. Contact: **support@petwash.co.il**.

## 2. What you may provide
A pet profile may include your pet's name, species/breed, age or date of birth, weight, photo, vaccination or health notes, behaviour notes, and care preferences.

## 3. Why we use it
We use pet-profile data to: deliver and tailor Services; help keep your pet and others safe (e.g. flagging behaviour or health factors to a Provider); send relevant reminders you have opted into; and improve our Services. Health and behaviour notes you add are used to operate the Service you request and to support safe handling.

## 4. Personal data, not the pet's
Animal data is not "personal data" about a person, but a pet profile is linked to you and is treated as your personal data under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, including Amendment 13. You control what you add.

## 5. Sharing
Relevant pet details are shared with a Provider only as needed to deliver a booked service and to support safe handling. We do not sell pet-profile data.

## 6. Accuracy and your control
You are responsible for the accuracy of what you enter; inaccurate health or behaviour information can affect safety. You can view, edit, or delete a pet profile in your account, subject to records we must keep for legal or safety reasons.

## 7. Retention
We keep pet-profile data while your account is active and as needed afterwards for legal, tax, or safety reasons, then delete or anonymise it.

## 8. Reminders and marketing
Care reminders tied to a pet profile are service-related where you enable them. Promotional messaging still requires separate marketing consent.

## 9. Language and contact
This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls. Questions: **support@petwash.co.il**.
