# SMS and Email Communication Notice

## 1. Purpose
This Notice explains the messages **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel, sends to you and how to control them. Contact: **support@petwash.co.il**.

## 2. Transactional messages (service messages)
To operate your account and Services we send essential SMS and email messages, including: phone/email verification and one-time codes; booking confirmations, reminders, and changes; payment and tax-document notices; loyalty and wallet pass updates; and security or account alerts. These are part of the Service and are not marketing. You receive them while you hold an active account; they cannot be fully switched off without affecting the Service.

## 3. Marketing messages
Promotional SMS and email are sent only with your separate consent under the Israeli Communications Law (anti-spam rules) and the Marketing Consent Notice. You can withdraw marketing consent at any time.

## 4. One-time codes and verification
Verification codes are personal — never share them. We will never ask you for a code by phone or message.

## 5. Frequency and charges
Message frequency depends on your activity. Your mobile carrier's standard rates may apply to SMS you receive; PetWash does not charge for the messages themselves.

## 6. How to opt out
Withdraw marketing consent via your account settings, the unsubscribe link in marketing emails, the opt-out instruction in marketing SMS, or by emailing support@petwash.co.il. Opting out of marketing does not stop transactional service messages.

## 7. Accuracy of contact details
Keep your phone and email current so you receive important service messages. We are not responsible for messages not received due to outdated or incorrect contact details.

## 8. Privacy
Communications and consent records are handled under the Privacy Notice (Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, including Amendment 13).

## 9. Language and contact
This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls. Questions: **support@petwash.co.il**.
