# Data Request and Privacy Rights Policy

## 1. Purpose
This Policy explains how to exercise your privacy rights with **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel, under the Israeli **Protection of Privacy Law**, including **Amendment 13**. It supplements the Privacy Notice. Contact: **support@petwash.co.il**.

## 2. Your rights
You may:
- **Access** — ask what personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
- **Correct** — ask us to fix inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **Delete** — ask us to delete data where we are not required or entitled to keep it;
- **Withdraw consent** — for optional processing such as marketing;
- **Object/limit** — ask us to limit certain optional uses where the law allows.

## 3. How to make a request
Send your request to support@petwash.co.il or use the privacy settings in the Platform. Tell us which right you wish to exercise and provide enough detail to locate your data.

## 4. Verifying identity
To protect your data, we verify your identity before acting on a request (for example, confirming control of the registered email/phone). We may ask for reasonable additional verification for sensitive requests.

## 5. Our response
We aim to respond within the timeframe required by Israeli law. We will tell you what action we have taken or, if we decline a request in whole or part (for example, where we must retain data for tax or legal reasons), our reasons and your options.

## 6. Limits
Some data must be retained by law (e.g. tax and accounting records for the legally required period) or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, and so cannot always be deleted on request. We will explain any such limit.

## 7. Fees
We do not normally charge for handling requests. Where the law permits a fee for excessive or repetitive requests, we will tell you in advance.

## 8. Complaints
If you are unhappy with how we handle a request, contact support@petwash.co.il. You may also approach the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority. This does not affect your other legal rights.
