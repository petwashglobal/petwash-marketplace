# Self-Service Wash Safety Rules

Operated by **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel. These Rules are binding when you use a self-service station and supplement the Station Use Terms. Contact: **support@petwash.co.il**.

## 1. You stay in control
Never leave your pet unattended in the station. Keep your pet secured (e.g. using the provided restraint) and within reach at all times. You operate the station yourself; PetWash staff are not present to handle your pet.

## 2. Check your pet is suitable
Do not wash a pet that is aggressive, frightened beyond comfort, unwell, injured, recovering from surgery, has a skin condition that washing could worsen, or is otherwise unfit. If unsure, consult a vet first. Puppies, elderly, and pregnant animals need extra care.

## 3. Water temperature and products
Test water temperature before use to avoid scalding or chilling. Use only the products provided or permitted, in the amounts intended. Keep water and products away from your pet's eyes, ears, nose, and mouth.

## 4. Use the equipment correctly
Follow the on-station and in-app steps. Do not point the dryer too close, on high heat, or for too long. Do not place a pet in or near equipment in a way it is not designed for. Do not let children operate the station.

## 5. Stop if there is distress
If your pet shows fear, pain, breathing difficulty, or distress, stop immediately, remove your pet, and seek veterinary advice if needed. In an emergency, call a vet; report serious incidents to support@petwash.co.il.

## 6. Hygiene and slips
Floors may be wet — move carefully. Clean visible mess and dispose of waste properly. Do not use the station if you see a fault, leak, or damaged part; report it instead.

## 7. Your responsibility
You are responsible for your own and your pet's safety and for any harm caused by not following these Rules. PetWash provides the facility; safe use depends on you.

## 8. Acknowledgement
By confirming, you state that you have read and will follow these Rules and that your pet is, to your knowledge, fit to be washed.
