# Refund and Service Recovery Policy

## 1. Scope
This Policy explains refunds and service recovery for Services billed by **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel. It is read with the Member Terms and, for stations, the Station Use Terms. Your statutory rights under Israeli consumer protection law apply and are not reduced by this Policy. Contact: **support@petwash.co.il**.

## 2. Statutory cancellation rights (Israeli Consumer Protection Law)
Your rights under the Consumer Protection Law 5741-1981 apply and are not reduced by this Policy:
- **Distance-sale cancellation:** for goods, you may cancel from the day of the transaction until **14 days after receiving the product** (or the disclosure document, whichever is later); for a booked service, generally within **14 days of the transaction and at least 2 days (excluding rest days) before the service is due to begin**.
- **Change-of-mind fee:** where you cancel for convenience, we may retain a cancellation fee of **no more than 5% of the transaction value or ₪100, whichever is lower**; the balance is refunded within **14 days** of your cancellation notice.
- **Our fault / non-delivery / defect:** if a Service is not delivered, is defective, or breaches the agreement, you receive a **full refund with no cancellation fee**.
- **Extended right:** customers aged 65+, persons with disabilities, and new immigrants have an extended cancellation right (up to **4 months**) on qualifying assisted (e.g. phone/chat) transactions.
Refunds are made to the original payment method where possible.

## 3. Self-service stations
A self-service wash is consumed at the point of use. If a station fails to deliver a paid cycle due to a fault on our side (e.g. machine malfunction taking payment without delivering service), we will refund or re-credit the affected charge. Report the issue promptly with the station ID, date, and time so we can verify it.

## 4. Booked third-party services
For services delivered by independent Providers, refund eligibility depends on the cancellation window and the Provider's terms shown at booking. PetWash will facilitate eligible refunds. Where a Provider fails to deliver a confirmed service, you are entitled to a refund of amounts paid for that service.

## 5. Cancellations and windows
Cancellation windows and any fees are shown before you confirm a booking. Cancelling within an allowed window is refundable per the displayed terms; cancelling outside it may incur a fee that reflects costs reasonably incurred.

## 6. No-shows
If you do not arrive for a booked service without cancelling, a no-show fee may apply as disclosed at booking, reflecting the Provider's reserved capacity.

## 7. Goods
Returns and refunds for physical goods follow the applicable shop returns policy and Israeli consumer law.

## 8. Service recovery
Where something goes wrong that is our responsibility, we may, at our discretion and in addition to any refund, offer a credit, re-service, or goodwill gesture. A goodwill gesture is not an admission of liability.

## 9. How refunds are made
Approved refunds are made to the original payment method where possible, net of amounts properly retained (e.g. a disclosed cancellation fee). A corrected tax document is issued where required. Refund timing also depends on your payment provider.

## 10. How to request
Email support@petwash.co.il or use in-app support with your booking/transaction reference. We aim to acknowledge promptly and resolve fairly.

## 11. Law and language
Israeli law governs. This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls.
