# Marketing Consent Notice

## 1. What you are agreeing to
By giving marketing consent you allow **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel, to send you promotional messages about PetWash Services, offers, and news.

## 2. Opt-in, not bundled
Marketing consent is optional and separate from creating an account or using the Services. You can use PetWash fully without it. Consent is given by an active, deliberate choice (a ticked, un-pre-filled box) and is recorded with date and channel.

## 3. Channels
With your consent we may contact you by **email** and/or **SMS**. You may consent to one channel and not another. This is in line with the Israeli Communications Law (anti-spam) rules.

## 4. Content
Marketing may include promotions, new Services, loyalty offers, and seasonal campaigns. We do not sell your details to third parties for their own marketing.

## 5. Withdrawing consent
You can withdraw at any time, easily and free of charge: via account settings, the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, the opt-out reply in any marketing SMS, or by emailing support@petwash.co.il. We act on withdrawals promptly. Withdrawing marketing consent does not stop essential service (transactional) messages.

## 6. Privacy
Marketing data and consent records are handled under the Privacy Notice (Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, including Amendment 13).
