# Complaints and Support Policy

## 1. Our commitment
**PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel, aims to handle complaints fairly, promptly, and clearly. This Policy explains how to raise an issue and what to expect.

## 2. How to contact us
- **Email**: support@petwash.co.il
- **In-app support**: through the Help/Support section of the Platform

Include your name, account or booking/transaction reference, and a clear description of the issue. For station issues, include the station ID, date, and time.

## 3. What we cover
We help with bookings, payments, tax documents, the wallet pass, loyalty, station faults, and account questions. For issues with a service delivered by an independent Provider, we will help facilitate a resolution between you and the Provider; the underlying service contract is between you and the Provider.

## 4. How we handle complaints
1. **Acknowledge** — we aim to confirm receipt promptly.
2. **Investigate** — we review the facts and records and may ask for more detail.
3. **Respond** — we provide a reasoned reply and, where appropriate, a remedy under the Refund and Service Recovery Policy.

## 5. Timeframes
We aim to resolve straightforward issues quickly and to update you where a matter needs more time. Indicative response targets are published in our help materials and may vary with complexity and volume.

## 6. Escalation
If you are not satisfied with the first response, you may ask for the matter to be escalated for senior review. We will consider it fairly.

## 7. Your statutory rights
This Policy does not limit your rights under Israeli consumer protection or other law, including any right to approach the relevant authorities or the courts.

## 8. Privacy
Complaint records are handled under the Privacy Notice (Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, including Amendment 13).

## 9. Governing law and language
Israeli law governs; the Tel Aviv–Jaffa district courts have jurisdiction as set out in the Member Terms. This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls.
