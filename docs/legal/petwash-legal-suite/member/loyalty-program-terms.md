# Loyalty Program Terms

## 1. The programme
The PetWash loyalty programme, operated by **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel, lets eligible members earn and redeem points or rewards for using PetWash Services. Contact: **support@petwash.co.il**.

## 2. Eligibility and enrolment
Open to account holders aged 18 or over. Enrolment is free and optional. One loyalty membership per person.

## 3. Earning
You earn points or benefits on qualifying transactions as described in-app. Earning rates, qualifying activity, and any caps may change with notice. Points have no cash value and are not your property or money; they are a promotional benefit.

## 4. Redeeming
Points or rewards may be redeemed only as described in the programme, while you hold an active account. Rewards cannot be exchanged for cash, sold, or transferred unless we state otherwise.

## 5. Expiry and inactivity
Points may expire after a period of inactivity or after a stated validity period, as shown in-app. We will indicate balances and expiry in your account.

## 6. Adjustments and abuse
We may correct, reduce, or cancel points or rewards that were issued in error, obtained through abuse, fraud, returned/cancelled transactions, or breach of these Terms. We may suspend a member from the programme for such conduct.

## 7. Tax
Any tax consequences of rewards you receive are your responsibility, to the extent the law imposes them.

## 8. Changes and ending the programme
We may change or end the programme with reasonable notice. On closure we will give you a fair opportunity to redeem outstanding eligible points where practicable.

## 9. Privacy
Loyalty activity is processed under the Privacy Notice (Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, including Amendment 13). Enrolment does not by itself constitute marketing consent.

## 10. Liability, law, language
To the extent permitted by Israeli law, our liability under the programme is limited to restoring wrongly removed eligible points. Israeli law governs; the Tel Aviv–Jaffa district courts have exclusive jurisdiction. This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls.
