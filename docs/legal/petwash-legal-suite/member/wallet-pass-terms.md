# Wallet Pass Terms

## 1. What the pass is
The PetWash wallet pass is a digital membership credential issued by **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel, added to your phone's wallet app. It identifies you at stations and may carry a stored balance and loyalty status.

## 2. Issuance and ownership
The pass is personal to you and remains the property of PetWash. It is licensed for your use and may not be shared, copied, sold, or transferred. The QR/credential on the pass is secured; do not attempt to alter or forge it.

## 3. Stored value (if used)
Where the pass holds a balance, that balance is a prepayment for PetWash Services. It is denominated in ₪, is not a bank deposit, earns no interest, and is not redeemable for cash except where Israeli law requires. Top-ups are processed through our approved payment partners and a tax document is issued. Balance use, expiry, and any value caps are shown at top-up and in your account.

## 4. Using the pass
Present the pass at a station or to a Provider as instructed. You are responsible for transactions made with your pass. Tell us immediately if your phone or pass is lost or compromised so we can help secure your account.

## 5. Security and misuse
We may suspend or void a pass we reasonably believe is forged, duplicated, compromised, or used fraudulently, and may reverse transactions that result from such misuse.

## 6. Fees and pricing
Prices charged via the pass include VAT at 18% unless stated. Any applicable fees are disclosed before the charge.

## 7. Refunds
Refunds of pass charges follow the Refund and Service Recovery Policy and your statutory rights. Where a stored balance is refundable, refunds are made to the original payment method where possible.

## 8. Liability
To the extent permitted by Israeli law, our liability for the pass is limited to restoring a wrongly deducted balance or the amount of the affected transaction. We are not liable for loss caused by your sharing of the pass or failure to secure your device.

## 9. Privacy
Pass data is processed under the Privacy Notice (Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, including Amendment 13).

## 10. Changes and termination
We may update the pass, its features, or these Terms, with notice for material changes. We may deactivate a pass on account closure or for the reasons in the Account Suspension and Termination Policy; any refundable balance is handled per Section 7.

## 11. Governing law and language
Israeli law governs; the Tel Aviv–Jaffa district courts have exclusive jurisdiction. This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls. Contact: **support@petwash.co.il**.
