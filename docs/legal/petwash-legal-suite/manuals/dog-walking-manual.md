# Dog Walking Service Manual

This manual sets the mandatory standard for dog-walking bookings on the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash"). It applies together with the Provider Terms, *provider-safety-code*, and *provider-incident-reporting-manual*. You may walk dogs only if approved for the **walking** service type.

## 1. Before the walk
1.1 Confirm the dog, the address, the route or area, the duration, and any special needs (medical, reactivity, off-leash rules) on the Platform before starting.
1.2 Check the dog's collar/harness and ID are secure. Use a **secure leash and properly fitted harness/collar**. Bring waste bags and water in heat.

## 2. Leash and escape risk
2.1 Keep the dog **on-leash at all times** unless the booking, the location, and the law expressly allow off-leash and it is safe. Never wrap the leash around your hand in a way that injures you or the dog.
2.2 Prevent escape: keep a firm hold near roads, gates, and doorways; double-check that gates/doors are closed at pickup and drop-off; never tie a dog unattended.

## 3. Roads, traffic, and environment
3.1 Walk on safe paths, cross at safe points, and keep the dog on the inside away from traffic. Avoid hazards (broken glass, hot pavement, aggressive loose animals).
3.2 **Heat and rain:** in hot weather walk in cooler hours, keep walks short, carry water, watch for heat stress (heavy panting, drooling, weakness) — stop and cool the dog and seek a vet if needed. Avoid hot surfaces. Adjust or shorten walks in storms or unsafe conditions.

## 4. Reactive or aggressive dogs
4.1 If a dog is reactive or aggressive beyond your competence, or shows undisclosed aggression, **do not force the walk** — keep yourself, the dog, the public, and other animals safe, end the walk safely, and report it (*provider-incident-reporting-manual*, *provider-cancellation-rules*). Use a muzzle where required by law or by the Member's instruction.
4.2 Keep distance from other dogs, children, and people if the dog is reactive.

## 5. Pickup, drop-off, and check-in/out
5.1 Confirm the dog's identity at pickup. **Check in (start)** on the Platform at the beginning and **check out (finish)** at the end. Return the dog to the Member or an authorised person and secure the home/gate.
5.2 Never hand the dog to an unauthorised person and never leave it unattended outside.

## 6. Emergencies
6.1 Carry a charged phone and the Member's and emergency contacts. In an emergency, secure the dog, seek veterinary help, notify the Member, then PetWash. For a lost dog, search immediately and report at once.

## 7. Photo and report
7.1 Provide the walk **report and photos** through the Platform (route/duration, toileting, behaviour, water, any concern), consistent with *provider-photo-media-rules*. Be honest.

## 8. Incident report
8.1 Report any bite, injury, escape, traffic event, illness, or near-miss per *provider-incident-reporting-manual* — the same day at the latest.
