# Pet Sitting Service Manual

This manual sets the mandatory standard for pet-sitting bookings on the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash"). It applies with the Provider Terms, *provider-safety-code*, *provider-privacy-confidentiality-rules*, and *provider-incident-reporting-manual*. You may sit pets only if approved for the **sitting** service type.

## 1. Home access, keys, and codes
1.1 Use keys, codes, and alarm details **only** to perform the booking. Keep them secure, never copy or share them, and return keys / forget codes at the end. Report any lost key or access failure to the Member and PetWash immediately.
1.2 Confirm the access method, alarm steps, and emergency contacts on the Platform before the first visit.

## 2. Privacy in the home
2.1 Enter only the areas needed for the service. Do not open drawers, cupboards, or rooms beyond what care requires. Do not bring guests, and do not photograph private areas (see *provider-photo-media-rules*). Respect the household's privacy (*provider-privacy-confidentiality-rules*).

## 3. Feeding, water, litter, cleaning
3.1 Follow the Member's **feeding plan** exactly (food, amount, times). Keep **fresh water** available. Maintain **litter trays / toileting areas**, clean up after the pet, and leave the home tidy.

## 4. Medication limits
4.1 Administer medication **only** when the Member has given clear written instructions on the Platform (medicine, dose, time, method) and you are competent and willing to do so.
4.2 **Do not** give any medication, dosage, or treatment not instructed, and do not make medical decisions for the pet. If the pet seems unwell or a dose is missed/refused, contact the Member and, if urgent, the vet.

## 5. Emergency vet
5.1 Know the Member's preferred and nearest **emergency veterinarian** before the booking. If the pet is ill or injured, seek veterinary care, then notify the Member and PetWash. Keep receipts and records.

## 6. Overnight and missed visits
6.1 For **overnight stays**, be present as agreed and provide responsible supervision through the night; do not leave the pet alone longer than agreed.
6.2 Never **miss a scheduled visit**. If you cannot attend, arrange cover only through PetWash and notify the Member at once; do not leave a pet unfed or unattended (see *provider-cancellation-rules* — do not abandon a pet).

## 7. Property and damage
7.1 Treat the home and belongings with care. If anything is damaged (by the pet or otherwise), document it, tell the Member honestly, and file an incident report.

## 8. Photo and report
8.1 Provide visit **reports and photos** through the Platform (feeding, toileting, behaviour, wellbeing, any concern), consistent with *provider-photo-media-rules*.

## 9. Incident report
9.1 Report illness, injury, escape, property damage, access problems, or near-misses per *provider-incident-reporting-manual* — the same day at the latest.
