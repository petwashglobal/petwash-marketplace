# Service Manuals — Index

Service-specific operating standards for providers on the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. **517145033**, Israel. All manuals are **DRAFT** pending CLO/legal sign-off and are not binding until approved. They apply together with the [Provider Legal Suite](../provider/index.md), in particular the *Provider Safety Code*, *Provider Customer Care Standards*, and *Provider Incident Reporting Manual*.

You may perform a service only if approved for that service type — enforced by `requireProviderServiceApproval(serviceType)`. Where translated, the **Hebrew version controls**.
