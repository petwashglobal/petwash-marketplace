# Pet Training Service Manual

This manual sets the mandatory standard for pet-training bookings on the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash"). It applies with the Provider Terms, *provider-safety-code*, and *provider-incident-reporting-manual*. You may train only if approved for the **training** service type.

## 1. No guaranteed results
1.1 Training outcomes depend on the animal, its history, the owner's follow-through, and time. **Do not guarantee specific results, a cure, or a timeline.** Set honest, realistic expectations with the Member and record them on the Platform.

## 2. Training-method declaration
2.1 Use **humane, force-free / positive, welfare-based methods**. Never use fear, pain, intimidation, or punishment-based equipment or techniques that risk harm. Declare your methods to the Member; do not use a method the Member has not agreed to.

## 3. Aggression disclosure
3.1 Ask about and assess any history of **aggression, biting, or reactivity** before working with the animal. If a dog has a bite history or shows aggression beyond your competence, do not proceed unsafely — recommend appropriate handling, decline if needed, and report it (*provider-cancellation-rules*, *provider-incident-reporting-manual*).

## 4. Owner participation
4.1 Lasting training needs the **owner's involvement**. Explain what the owner must do, coach them, and make clear that progress depends on their consistency between sessions.

## 5. Public safety
5.1 In public sessions, keep the animal under control: use a secure leash/harness, muzzle where appropriate, keep distance from people, children, and other animals, and follow municipal rules. Protect the public at all times (*provider-safety-code*).

## 6. Session notes and homework
6.1 After each session, provide **session notes** through the Platform: what was worked on, the animal's response, and the **homework / practice** for the owner before the next session.

## 7. Progress expectations
7.1 Track progress honestly across sessions. If progress stalls or the issue is beyond training (e.g. a medical or serious behavioural problem), tell the Member and recommend a vet or qualified specialist; do not give veterinary or medical advice you are not qualified to give.

## 8. Cancellation
8.1 Honour scheduled sessions; cancel early through the Platform when genuinely necessary (*provider-cancellation-rules*). For safety reasons (aggression, unsafe environment), you may pause or decline and document why.

## 9. Incident reporting
9.1 Report any bite, injury, escape, or near-miss during a session per *provider-incident-reporting-manual* — the same day at the latest.
