# Grooming Service Manual

This manual sets the mandatory standard for grooming bookings on the platform of **PET WASH LTD** (פט וואש בע״מ), company no. 517145033 ("PetWash"). It applies with the Provider Terms, *provider-safety-code*, and *provider-incident-reporting-manual*. You may groom only if approved for the **grooming** service type.

## 1. Pre-service check
1.1 Before starting, assess the pet's **condition**: coat, skin, ears, eyes, nails, lumps, parasites, and overall health and temperament. Note anything unusual with the Member before proceeding.
1.2 Confirm the requested service and any limits on the Platform. Do not proceed if the pet is unfit for grooming (e.g. injured, in pain, or in distress) — pause and consult the Member.

## 2. Skin sensitivity and allergies
2.1 Ask about **skin sensitivity, allergies, and reactions** to products. Use suitable products and methods. Stop if the pet shows irritation or a reaction, and inform the Member.

## 3. Matted coat warning
3.1 Severe **matting** can hide skin problems and makes grooming risky. Where dematting or shaving a matted coat could cause pain or injury, **warn the Member first**, explain the risk, and proceed only with consent — or decline that part. Never cut skin or pull mats roughly.

## 4. Nail and injury risk
4.1 Trim nails carefully to avoid cutting the quick. **Handle blades, scissors, clippers, and dryers safely** to avoid nicks, cuts, burns, or heat injury. If you injure the pet, stop, give first aid, seek a vet if needed, tell the Member, and file an incident report.

## 5. Extra-charge approval
5.1 If the pet needs work beyond the booked scope (heavy matting, extra time, additional service), **get the Member's approval and record the add-on/extra charge through the Platform** before doing it. Do not collect extra payment off-platform (*provider-no-off-platform-circumvention-rule*).

## 6. Hygiene and equipment
6.1 Keep tools, blades, tables, and the area **clean and disinfected** between pets. Maintain equipment in safe working order. Follow infection-control practices to prevent spread between animals.

## 7. Aggressive-pet refusal
7.1 If a pet is **aggressive, panicked, or unsafe** to groom beyond your competence, you may **stop or decline** for safety. Use safe restraint or a muzzle where appropriate; never force a distressed pet. Document and report it (*provider-cancellation-rules*, *provider-incident-reporting-manual*).

## 8. Before / after photos and report
8.1 Take **before and after photos** and provide a report through the Platform (condition noted, service done, products used, any concern), consistent with *provider-photo-media-rules*.

## 9. Incident reporting
9.1 Report any injury, reaction, illness, or near-miss per *provider-incident-reporting-manual* — the same day at the latest.
