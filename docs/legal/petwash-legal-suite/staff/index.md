# Staff & Admin Legal Suite (§3F)

Internal legal documents and operational manuals for employees, contractors, and administrators of **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel. Governing law: Israel.

> These documents are **DRAFTS** and are **not binding** until signed off by the CLO / legal. Hebrew is the controlling language once translated.

## Core principles (apply to every document below)

1. **Every admin has their own personal login.** No shared admin accounts. Actions are attributable to a named individual.
2. **Least privilege.** Access is scoped to the role's job function only:
   - Support staff cannot view tax/financial documents.
   - Marketing cannot view invoices or customer financial data.
   - Developers cannot view production secrets or customer PII in the clear.
   - Provider-reviewers cannot approve payouts.
3. **Reason + audit log for document views.** Opening any customer/provider/financial record requires a stated reason and is recorded in an immutable audit log.
4. **Data minimisation.** Staff access only the minimum data needed for the task at hand.
