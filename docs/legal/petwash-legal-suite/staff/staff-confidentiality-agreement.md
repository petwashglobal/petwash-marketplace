# Staff Confidentiality & Data Protection Agreement

Between **PET WASH LTD** (company no. 517145033) ("Company") and the undersigned employee/contractor ("you").

## 1. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information you access through your role, including: customer and provider personal data, national ID numbers, payment and bank details, pricing and revenue data, tax documents, source code, secrets/credentials, security configurations, business plans, and partner/location data.

## 2. Your obligations
- Use Confidential Information **only** to perform your assigned duties.
- Do not copy, export, photograph, screenshot, forward, or store Confidential Information outside Company systems.
- Do not access records you have no work reason to access (no "browsing").
- Treat customer personal data per the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 (incl. Amendment 13) and Company policy.
- Report any suspected breach immediately per the [Security Breach Escalation Manual](./security-breach-escalation-manual.md).

## 3. Personal login & accountability
You will be issued a **personal, named login**. You must not share it, and you are accountable for all actions taken under it. Shared admin accounts are prohibited.

## 4. Least privilege
You receive access only to the systems and data your role requires. You must not attempt to obtain or use access beyond your role (see [Role-Based Access Policy](./admin-role-based-access-policy.md)).

## 5. Return / deletion
On termination you must return all Company property and stop accessing all systems immediately. Access is revoked the same day.

## 6. Survival & duration
Confidentiality obligations survive termination indefinitely for trade secrets and for the legal retention period (7 years) for tax/financial records.

## 7. Breach
Breach may result in disciplinary action, termination, and civil/criminal liability under the Computers Law, 5755-1995 and the Protection of Privacy Law.

## 8. Governing law
Israeli law. Courts of the Tel Aviv district have exclusive jurisdiction.
