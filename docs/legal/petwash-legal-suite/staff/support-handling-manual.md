# Support Handling Manual

Operational manual for support agents at **PET WASH LTD** (no. 517145033).

## 1. Identity & access
- Use your personal login. Never act under a shared account.
- You may view booking/account status and message history. Payment data is masked; you cannot view tax documents, full card/bank details, or approve payouts.

## 2. Verifying the customer
Before discussing account specifics, confirm identity via the channel of contact (logged-in session, registered phone/email). Do not disclose account data to unverified callers.

## 3. Handling common requests
- **Booking issue:** check status in the admin console (enter a reason if opening records). Explain next steps.
- **Refund request:** follow the [Refund Review Manual](./refund-review-manual.md). Issue only within your cap; above cap, escalate for four-eyes approval.
- **Complaint about a provider/station:** log it; route to provider-review or station-ops as appropriate.
- **Privacy/data request (access/deletion):** do **not** action yourself — route to CLO/DPO per the privacy process.

## 4. In-platform communication
Keep customer–provider contact inside the PetWash inbox (no phone-number sharing). Do not give out personal contact details.

## 5. Data handling
- Open only the records needed; every sensitive view is logged with a reason.
- Never copy data to personal email/chat or take screenshots.
- Do not promise legal outcomes, certifications, or guarantees not in approved copy.

## 6. Tone & brand
Calm, precise, helpful. No over-promising. No comparisons to other brands. Use approved wording only.

## 7. Escalation
- Money disputes above cap → finance.
- Safety/animal-welfare → [Incident Handling Manual](./incident-handling-manual.md).
- Suspected data breach → [Security Breach Escalation Manual](./security-breach-escalation-manual.md).

## 8. Records
Log every interaction. Support records are retained per Company retention policy.
