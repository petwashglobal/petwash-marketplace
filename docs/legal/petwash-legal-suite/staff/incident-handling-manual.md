# Incident Handling Manual

Operational manual for handling operational, safety, and service incidents at **PET WASH LTD** (no. 517145033). (Security/data breaches → [Security Breach Escalation Manual](./security-breach-escalation-manual.md).)

## 1. What counts as an incident
- Animal injury, distress, or welfare concern during a wash/grooming/sitting/walk.
- Station fault posing a safety risk (electrical, water, mechanical).
- Customer or provider safety/harassment report.
- Service outage affecting bookings/payments.

## 2. Immediate priorities (in order)
1. **Safety of animals and people first.** Advise stopping use / vacating; call emergency services where life/limb is at risk.
2. Contain the hazard (e.g. take a station offline).
3. Preserve evidence (photos, logs, timestamps) without compromising privacy.

## 3. Logging
Open an incident record with: time, location/station/booking, parties, description, actions taken, and severity (Low/Medium/High/Critical). Every record access is logged with a reason.

## 4. Severity & response
| Severity | Examples | Response |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Critical | Animal seriously injured; electrical hazard; multiple users at risk | Stop service, notify manager + CEO immediately, take station offline |
| High | Single injury, station fault, harassment report | Same-day manager review, provider/station action |
| Medium | Service failure, repeated complaint | Investigate within 24h |
| Low | Minor service issue | Routine handling |

## 5. Communication
Keep affected users informed in-platform. Do not admit legal liability or make guarantees; state facts and next steps. Route insurance-relevant matters to CLO/finance.

## 6. Animal welfare
Treat any welfare concern as High minimum. Document and, where appropriate, suspend the relevant provider pending review.

## 7. Escalation
- Injury/insurance/legal exposure → CLO.
- Hardware safety → [Station Operations Manual](./station-operations-manual.md) + manufacturer/Nayax.
- Data/security → security breach process.

## 8. Closure & review
Close only after the hazard is resolved and follow-ups assigned. High/Critical incidents get a brief post-incident review.
