# Contractor Safety Rules

Apply to any contractor performing on-site work for **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel — including at PetWash stations, public locations, and partner premises.

## 1. Legal compliance
The contractor complies with the Israeli Work Safety Ordinance and all applicable safety regulations, and holds the licences, certifications, and insurance required for the work.

## 2. Trained personnel only
Only competent, trained personnel perform the work. The contractor is responsible for its workers' supervision, qualifications, and conduct on site.

## 3. Personal protective equipment
Workers use appropriate PPE (gloves, eye protection, slip-resistant footwear, etc.) suitable to the task, including when handling water, electricity, chemicals, or cleaning agents.

## 4. Electrical and water safety
Stations combine water and electricity. The contractor must isolate power before electrical work, follow lockout/tagout, never work on live circuits unsafely, and avoid creating slip or shock hazards. Only qualified electricians perform electrical work.

## 5. Public and pet safety
Where the public or pets may be present, the contractor cordons off the work area, displays warning signage, prevents access to hazards, and never leaves the site unsafe or unattended with hazards exposed.

## 6. Chemicals
Cleaning agents and chemicals are stored, labelled, used, and disposed of safely per their safety data sheets and Israeli law. No pet-unsafe residue may be left in or around a station.

## 7. Hazard reporting and incidents
The contractor reports hazards, near-misses, and incidents to PetWash promptly. Serious incidents are reported immediately and to the authorities where the law requires. PetWash may suspend work after an incident pending review.

## 8. No unsafe shortcuts
The contractor will not bypass safety controls to save time or cost. Unsafe work may be stopped by PetWash at any time without liability to the contractor.

## 9. Insurance and indemnity
The contractor maintains valid liability and employer insurance and indemnifies PetWash against claims arising from the contractor's acts, omissions, or breaches.

## 10. Consequences
Breach may halt work, halt payment pending investigation, and terminate the engagement. **Payment remains subject to all supplier payment gates and admin approval.**

## 11. Governing law
These Rules are governed by Israeli law. This English version is for convenience; the Hebrew version controls once issued.
