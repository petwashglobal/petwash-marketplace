# Supplier Duplicate-Invoice Declaration

Made to **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel, with each invoice submitted for payment.

I declare:

1. **No duplicate.** The invoice I am submitting has **not** been submitted, paid, or queued for payment before, in whole or in part, by me or any related party.

2. **Unique numbering.** The invoice number is unique in my sequence and is not reused, re-dated, or re-issued to obtain a second payment for the same supply.

3. **No re-bill of paid work.** I am not re-billing goods or services PetWash has already paid for, including under a different invoice number, PO, or entity.

4. **No split to evade controls.** I have not split a single supply across multiple invoices to evade approval thresholds or the duplicate check.

5. **Credit notes.** Where goods were returned or an amount was over-charged, I have issued a proper credit note rather than silently re-invoicing.

6. **I authorise the check.** I authorise PetWash to run an automated and manual duplicate-invoice check against invoice number, amount, date, PO, and supplier identity, and I understand **payment is blocked if a duplicate or suspected duplicate is found** until the matter is resolved.

7. **Consequences.** I understand that submitting a duplicate or re-issued invoice for double payment may reverse payment, terminate the relationship, and be referred to the authorities. **Payment is released only when the duplicate-invoice check passes, the invoice matches the order, and all other gates and admin approval are satisfied.**
