# Supplier Six-Month Reconfirmation

A periodic check that a Supplier's details remain true. Required by **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel, at least every **six (6) months** and on any material change.

## 1. Why
Tax classification, bank details, insurance, and licences change. To keep the payment gates meaningful, the Supplier must reconfirm its details periodically.

## 2. What is reconfirmed
The Supplier reconfirms (and corrects where needed):
- legal name, ID/company number, contact details;
- tax/business classification and VAT treatment (Patur / Murshe / Chevra);
- valid withholding-tax certificate;
- bank account ownership and details;
- required licences, permits, and insurance still in force;
- that no prior declaration has become false.

## 3. Declaration
I confirm that the above details are current and true as of today, or I have updated them, and that I will notify PetWash immediately of any further change. I understand changed bank details require re-verification before any payment to them.

## 4. Effect of not reconfirming
If a reconfirmation is overdue, or a required document has lapsed, PetWash may **pause payment** until the Supplier reconfirms and re-verifies. Reconfirmation does not, by itself, release a payment — all standard gates and admin approval still apply.

## 5. Reminders
PetWash may send reminders by email or in-app before the reconfirmation is due. It remains the Supplier's responsibility to reconfirm on time.

## 6. Records
Reconfirmation records are kept for the period Israeli law requires.
