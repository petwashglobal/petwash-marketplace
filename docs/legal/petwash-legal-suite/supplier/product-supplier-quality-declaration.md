# Product Supplier Quality Declaration

Made to **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel, by a supplier of products (e.g. shampoo, conditioner, tea tree oil, consumables, spare parts).

For the products I supply, I declare:

1. **Specification.** The products meet the agreed specification and the description on the order, label, and any product sheet. They are fit for their stated pet-care purpose.

2. **Safety and law.** The products comply with applicable Israeli law and standards, including labelling, safety data, and import requirements where relevant, and are safe for their intended use on or around pets when used as directed.

3. **Pet-formulated claims.** Any "pet-formulated", "pet-safe", or ingredient claim I make is accurate and substantiated. I will **not** make false, exaggerated, or unsubstantiated claims (for example, I will not describe a product as "certified organic", "vet-approved", or "hypoallergenic" unless I hold valid proof, and I will provide that proof on request).

4. **Ingredients and batch.** I will disclose ingredients/composition and provide batch and traceability information, and a safety data sheet where applicable.

5. **Consistency.** Delivered goods match approved samples and prior batches in quality. I will notify PetWash before any formulation, source, or specification change.

6. **Defects and recall.** I will promptly notify PetWash of any defect, contamination, or safety issue, support a recall if needed, and accept return/replacement/credit for non-conforming goods.

7. **Authenticity.** Goods are genuine and not counterfeit; spare parts are approved/compatible as stated.

8. **Consequences.** A false quality claim or non-conforming supply may block or reverse payment, require return or recall, terminate the relationship, and be referred to the authorities. **Payment is released only when this declaration is valid and the goods conform, alongside the other supplier payment gates and admin approval.**
