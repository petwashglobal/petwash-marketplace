# Station Maintenance Contractor Manual

For contractors maintaining PetWash Smart Hub stations for **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel.

## 1. Scope
Covers preventive and corrective maintenance of station hardware, plumbing, electrical supply, enclosure, and signage. It does **not** authorise any change to payment terminals or networked control — see §6.

## 2. Authorisation and scheduling
Work is performed only under a PetWash work order. The contractor schedules with PetWash to minimise downtime, follows the Contractor Safety Rules, and signs in/out of each station.

## 3. Preventive maintenance
At each scheduled visit the contractor: inspects water supply, drainage, and seals; checks the dryer, hoses, fittings, and dispensing systems; verifies electrical safety (earthing, RCD, no exposed wiring); checks lighting, doors, and enclosure integrity; cleans and sanitises pet-contact surfaces; and confirms signage (including VAT-inclusive pricing) is correct and legible.

## 4. Corrective maintenance
On a fault, the contractor isolates the hazard, makes the station safe, repairs within scope, and records parts used and work done. Out-of-scope or major faults are reported to PetWash before proceeding.

## 5. Parts and consumables
Only PetWash-approved parts and pet-safe consumables are used. Counterfeit or unapproved substitutes are prohibited.

## 6. Payment terminal and network — do not touch
The contractor must **not** alter, reconfigure, swap, or re-cable the payment terminal, its connectivity, or any networked control device. Issues there are reported to PetWash, who handles them through the authorised payment/connectivity process. This boundary is mandatory.

## 7. Hygiene and pet safety
The station must be left clean, dry where required, and free of chemical residue or hazards to pets and people before it is returned to service.

## 8. Records and reporting
For each visit the contractor logs: station ID, date/time, work done, parts used, faults found, photos where useful, and station status on leaving (in service / out of service). Reports support the invoice and the payment match.

## 9. Sign-off and invoicing
PetWash signs off completed work. The contractor invoices only signed-off, ordered work; the invoice must match the work order. **Payment is subject to all supplier payment gates (declarations, tax status, bank verification, invoice match, duplicate check) and admin approval.**

## 10. Governing law
Governed by Israeli law. This English version is for convenience; the Hebrew version controls once issued.
