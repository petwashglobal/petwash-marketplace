# Supplier & Contractor Terms

These Terms govern the supply of goods and/or services to **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel ("**PetWash**", "**we**") by a supplier or contractor (the **"Supplier"**). Contact: **suppliers@petwash.co.il**.

## 1. Scope
These Terms apply to every order, purchase order, statement of work, or engagement between PetWash and the Supplier, unless a separately signed master agreement says otherwise.

## 2. Independent status
The Supplier is an independent business, not an employee or agent of PetWash. The Supplier is solely responsible for its own personnel, taxes, National Insurance, licences, permits, and insurance.

## 3. Onboarding before any payment — critical
Before PetWash will pay the Supplier any amount, **all** of the following must be present and valid:
1. signed Supplier truth, tax/business, invoice-truth, duplicate-invoice, bank-ownership, and document-upload declarations;
2. verified Israeli tax/business status (Osek Patur / Osek Murshe / company), with VAT consistency;
3. verified bank-account ownership in the Supplier's legal name;
4. an invoice that **matches** the approved order/PO and delivery (amount, VAT, items);
5. a passed **duplicate-invoice check** (no previously paid or pending invoice with the same number/amount);
6. **admin approval**.

**If any one of these is missing or fails, payment is blocked** — enforced by `requireSupplierPaymentApproval(...)`. No exceptions, no manual bypass.

## 4. Orders
Only a PetWash purchase order or written authorisation creates a commitment to buy. The Supplier must not deliver or invoice without it.

## 5. Pricing, VAT and invoicing
Prices are as agreed and, for VAT-registered Suppliers, stated plus **18% VAT**. Invoices must be lawful Israeli tax invoices showing the Supplier's name, ID/company number, Osek status, invoice number and date, line items, VAT, and total, and must match the order. Patur suppliers must not charge VAT.

## 6. Payment terms
PetWash pays approved, matched, non-duplicate invoices on the agreed terms (e.g. net 30 / Shotef+30) to the verified bank account only. PetWash may withhold tax at source where Israeli law requires and where the Supplier has not provided a valid exemption.

## 7. Quality and compliance
Goods and services must meet the agreed specification, the relevant PetWash manual or quality declaration, all applicable Israeli standards and safety law, and animal-welfare requirements where relevant.

## 8. Confidentiality and data
The Supplier will keep PetWash information confidential and handle any personal data only as instructed, under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law including Amendment 13.

## 9. Liability and indemnity
The Supplier is responsible for its acts and omissions and indemnifies PetWash against third-party claims arising from the Supplier's breach, defective goods/services, or negligence.

## 10. Term and termination
Either party may terminate on the agreed notice. PetWash may suspend or terminate immediately for safety, fraud, false declaration, or material breach, and may halt payment pending investigation.

## 11. Governing law
These Terms are governed by Israeli law; the competent courts of the Tel Aviv–Jaffa district have exclusive jurisdiction.
