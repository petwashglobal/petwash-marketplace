# Cleaning Contractor Manual

For contractors providing cleaning and sanitation at PetWash stations and locations for **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel.

## 1. Scope
Routine and deep cleaning of station bays, pet-contact surfaces, drainage points, enclosures, and surrounding area. Cleaning supports a calm, clean, pet-safe experience and the eco-conscious operating standard.

## 2. Authorisation
Cleaning is performed under a PetWash work order/schedule. The contractor follows the Contractor Safety Rules and signs in/out.

## 3. Pet-safe products only
Only PetWash-approved, pet-safe cleaning agents are used at correct dilution. Products that are toxic to dogs, leave harmful residue, or have strong irritant fumes are prohibited on pet-contact surfaces. Surfaces are rinsed and left residue-free.

## 4. Routine clean checklist
Per visit: remove hair and debris from bays and drains; clean and sanitise wash surfaces, harness/tether points, and touchpoints; wipe screens and dispensers; empty and reline waste bins; check and clear drainage; clean floor and approach area; restock consumables where in scope; report low stock.

## 5. Drainage and water care
Hair and debris are removed to drain traps, bagged, and disposed of properly — never flushed in a way that blocks plumbing or sends excess hair/runoff into municipal systems. This supports cleaner shared and public drainage.

## 6. Deep clean
On schedule or after heavy use: descale, deep-sanitise, clean behind/under fittings, and inspect seals for mould. Report wear or damage to PetWash.

## 7. Hygiene standard
Leave the station visibly clean, sanitised, dry where required, restocked, and free of hazards and chemical residue before returning it to service.

## 8. Waste and environment
Waste is segregated and disposed of lawfully. Water and product use is efficient and not wasteful. No chemicals are poured to ground or storm drains.

## 9. Records and reporting
Log station ID, date/time, tasks done, products used, stock taken/needed, issues found, and status on leaving. Reports support the invoice match.

## 10. Sign-off and invoicing
The contractor invoices only ordered, completed cleaning; the invoice must match the work order. **Payment is subject to all supplier payment gates (declarations, tax status, bank verification, invoice match, duplicate check) and admin approval.**

## 11. Governing law
Governed by Israeli law. This English version is for convenience; the Hebrew version controls once issued.
