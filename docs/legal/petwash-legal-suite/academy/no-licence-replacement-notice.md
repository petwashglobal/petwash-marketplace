## Read this carefully before relying on any Academy credential.

A PetWash Academy certificate or badge is an **educational credential**. It confirms only that you completed a course. It is **not** and can **never** be used as a substitute for any of the following:

1. **Government or municipal licences and permits** — including any business licence, animal-handling permit, kennel/boarding permit, or local authority approval required to operate in your area under Israeli law and municipal by-laws.

2. **Insurance** — including public liability, professional, animal-care, or any other insurance required to work with pets or operate equipment. The Academy provides no insurance and does not assess your insurance.

3. **Business and tax registration** — including Osek Patur / Osek Murshe / company registration, VAT registration, and reporting duties under Israeli tax law.

4. **PetWash provider approval and payout eligibility** — these are granted only through a separate PetWash admin review based on valid licences, insurance, tax status, and identity/KYC verification. **No Academy course, certificate, or badge approves you as a provider or entitles you to bookings or payment.**

## Your responsibility
It is **your** responsibility to obtain and maintain every licence, permit, registration, and insurance that the law requires for your activity. If you are unsure what applies to you, seek your own professional or legal advice.
