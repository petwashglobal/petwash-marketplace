All PetWash Academy content — including videos, slides, text, images, assessments, the PetWash™ name and logo, and course structure — is owned by or licensed to PetWash and protected under the Israeli Copyright Law 2007 and international law.

## 2. Your licence
On enrolment you receive a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to access and study the content for your own learning, for as long as your enrolment is valid. No other rights are granted.

## 3. What you may not do
You may not, without PetWash's prior written permission:
- copy, download (beyond any function the platform expressly provides), screen-record, or photograph content for redistribution;
- share your login or give others access to the content;
- republish, post publicly, sell, sub-licence, or commercially exploit any content;
- reverse-engineer assessments or build a competing course from PetWash materials;
- remove or alter copyright, trademark, or attribution notices.

## 4. Your own submissions
Where you submit work (e.g. an assessment answer or practice photo), you keep your rights in it but grant PetWash a licence to use it to assess you and operate the Academy, consistent with the Privacy Notice and Amendment 13.

## 5. Trademarks
PetWash™ and related marks may be used only as the Academy displays them. You may not use the PetWash brand to imply endorsement, partnership, or provider approval you have not been granted.

## 6. Enforcement
Breach may lead to suspension or removal from the Academy, revocation of certificates/badges, and legal action for infringement. PetWash may report serious infringement to the authorities.

## 7. Reporting infringement
To report suspected misuse of PetWash content, contact **academy@petwash.co.il**.

## 8. Governing law
These Rules are governed by Israeli law. This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls.
