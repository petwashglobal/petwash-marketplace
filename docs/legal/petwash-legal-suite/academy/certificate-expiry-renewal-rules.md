# Certificate Expiry & Renewal Rules

Issued by **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel.

## 1. Why certificates expire
Pet-care standards, hygiene practices, and equipment change. To keep credentials meaningful, PetWash Academy certificates and badges carry a validity period stated on the credential.

## 2. Validity period
Unless stated otherwise on the credential, a certificate/badge is valid for **24 months** from its issue date. Some courses may set a shorter or longer period, which is shown at enrolment and printed on the credential.

## 3. Effect of expiry
On expiry, a credential is marked **expired** and may no longer be displayed as current. An expired credential:
- does not become a licence, insurance, provider approval, or payout entitlement at any time (it never was);
- does not, by itself, suspend a separate provider approval, which is governed by the Provider documents and its own evidence (licences, insurance, KYC).

## 4. Renewal
To renew, a student completes the current renewal module or re-assessment for the course and signs an updated Course Completion Declaration. PetWash may require the full current course where content has materially changed.

## 5. Renewal reminders
PetWash may send reminders before expiry by email, SMS, or in-app. Reminders are a courtesy; it is the student's responsibility to track and renew.

## 6. Fees
Any renewal fee is shown before renewal, inclusive of 18% VAT where applicable.

## 7. Grace period
PetWash may, at its discretion, offer a short grace period after expiry during which renewal is possible without retaking the full course. No grace period is guaranteed.

## 8. Records
PetWash keeps issue, expiry, and renewal records for the period required by Israeli law and for credential verification.

## 9. Governing law
These Rules are governed by Israeli law. This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls.
