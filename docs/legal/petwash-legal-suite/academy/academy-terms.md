# PetWash Academy Terms

## 1. Who we are
The PetWash Academy (the **"Academy"**) is an educational programme operated by **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel ("**PetWash**", "**we**"). Contact: **academy@petwash.co.il**.

## 2. What the Academy is — and is not
The Academy provides training, courses, assessments, certificates, and digital badges relating to pet washing, grooming basics, station operation, hygiene, and customer care.

**The Academy is education only.** It is not a licensing body, not a government authority, and not a professional regulator. Enrolling in or completing a course does **not** grant any legal right to operate, any occupational licence, any insurance, or any guarantee of work, income, or onboarding with PetWash.

## 3. No automatic approval — critical
**Completing any Academy course, passing any assessment, or earning any Academy badge does NOT approve you as a PetWash provider, does NOT entitle you to receive bookings, and does NOT entitle you to any payout.** Provider approval and payout eligibility are separate processes governed by the Provider documents, require independent admin review, and depend on valid government licences, insurance, tax registration, and identity/KYC verification that the Academy neither provides nor replaces.

## 4. Eligibility
You must be 18 or over and able to form a binding contract under Israeli law. Some courses may require prerequisites, which are stated at enrolment.

## 5. Enrolment and fees
Course availability, content, duration, and fees are shown before enrolment. Where a fee applies it is stated inclusive of 18% VAT. Payment terms and any refund window are shown at checkout and governed by Israeli consumer law.

## 6. Your obligations
You agree to: provide accurate information; complete assessments honestly and personally (no impersonation, no sharing answers); not record, copy, or redistribute course materials; and follow the Academy Code of Conduct.

## 7. Content and changes
Course content is for personal learning. We may update, reorder, or retire courses, and may change schedules or instructors. The current published syllabus governs your enrolment.

## 8. Certificates and badges
Certificates and badges are issued under the Certificate & Badge Rules and may expire, require renewal, or be revoked under the Certificate Expiry/Renewal Rules and Badge Revocation Rules. They remain PetWash property and convey no licence.

## 9. Liability
