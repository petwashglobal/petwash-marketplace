# Badge Revocation Rules

Issued by **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel.

## 1. Purpose
These Rules state when a PetWash Academy certificate or badge may be suspended or revoked. Revocation protects the integrity of the Academy credential and the safety of pets and people.

## 2. Grounds for revocation
PetWash may suspend or revoke a certificate/badge where it reasonably finds that:
- the credential was obtained by cheating, impersonation, or false information;
- the student altered, forged, or misrepresented the credential, its level, or its scope;
- the student presented the badge as a licence, insurance, provider approval, or earnings entitlement;
- the student materially breached the Academy Code of Conduct or harmed a pet, person, or PetWash's reputation;
- the credential was issued in error.

## 3. Process
Other than in clear safety or fraud cases, PetWash will normally: notify the student of the concern; allow a reasonable opportunity to respond; and decide based on the evidence. Decisions and their basis are recorded.

## 4. Immediate suspension
PetWash may suspend a credential immediately, before a final decision, where there is a credible safety, welfare, or fraud concern.

## 5. Effect of revocation
A revoked credential must no longer be displayed or relied upon. PetWash may update its verification record to show the credential is revoked. Revocation does not, by itself, alter any separate provider approval, which is governed independently — but the conduct underlying revocation may be referred to the provider review process.

## 6. Reinstatement
PetWash may, at its discretion, allow a student to re-enrol and re-earn a credential after revocation, subject to any conditions PetWash sets.

## 7. No refund
Revocation for cause does not entitle the student to a refund of course or renewal fees.

## 8. Records and privacy
Revocation records are kept as required by Israeli law and handled under the Protection of Privacy Law, including Amendment 13.

## 9. Governing law
These Rules are governed by Israeli law. This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls.
