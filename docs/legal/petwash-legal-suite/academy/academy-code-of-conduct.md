# Academy Code of Conduct

Applies to all students and participants of the PetWash Academy, operated by **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel.

## 1. Respect
Treat instructors, staff, and fellow students with respect. Harassment, discrimination, threats, or abuse — in any session, chat, or channel — are not tolerated.

## 2. Honesty and integrity
Complete all assessments yourself and honestly. Do not impersonate another person, share or solicit answers, plagiarise, or submit work that is not your own. Integrity breaches may void results and credentials.

## 3. Animal welfare
Where a course involves real animals or practical handling, follow all welfare and safety instructions. Never cause distress, pain, or harm to an animal. Report any welfare concern immediately.

## 4. Safety
Follow all equipment, hygiene, and venue safety instructions. Do not operate equipment you have not been authorised and shown how to use.

## 5. Honest representation
Do not claim a qualification, level, licence, insurance, or PetWash provider status you do not hold. An Academy credential is education only and never a licence, insurance, or provider approval.

## 6. Content respect
Follow the Content & Copyright Rules. Do not record, copy, or redistribute course materials.

## 7. Privacy
Respect the privacy of other students and instructors. Do not photograph, record, or share others without consent. Handle any personal data only as permitted.

## 8. Lawful conduct
Comply with Israeli law at all times during Academy activities.

## 9. Consequences
Breaching this Code may result in a warning, removal from a session or the Academy, voiding of results, and revocation of certificates or badges, without refund where the breach is the student's fault.

## 10. Reporting
Report concerns or breaches to **academy@petwash.co.il**.

## 11. Governing law
This Code is governed by Israeli law. This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls.
