# Certificate & Badge Rules

Issued by **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel.

## 1. What a certificate/badge is
A PetWash Academy certificate or digital badge is a record that a named student completed a specific educational course or assessment on a specific date. It is an educational credential only.

## 2. What it is NOT
A certificate or badge is **not**:
- a government, municipal, or professional **licence** or permit;
- proof of **insurance** or fitness to work;
- approval as a **PetWash provider**;
- any entitlement to **bookings, work, income, or payout**.

Provider approval and payout are separate PetWash processes that depend on valid licences, insurance, tax status, and identity verification. **A badge never replaces any of these.** See the No-Licence-Replacement Notice.

## 3. Issuance conditions
A certificate/badge is issued only when: the student completed the required lessons and passed the required assessment; the Course Completion Declaration was signed; and no integrity flag is open against the student.

## 4. Ownership
Certificates and badges remain the property of PetWash. PetWash grants the student a personal, non-transferable right to display them truthfully. They may not be sold, transferred, altered, or attached to another person's record.

## 5. Tiers and levels
Where courses have levels (e.g. foundation, advanced), each badge states its level and scope. A student may display only the levels actually earned.

## 6. Truthful display
A student must not: alter a certificate/badge; misstate its level, date, or scope; imply it is a licence or insurance; or imply it grants provider approval or earnings. Misrepresentation may lead to revocation and removal from the Academy.

## 7. Verification
PetWash may operate a verification record (e.g. a badge ID or check page). PetWash may correct, suspend, or withdraw any certificate/badge found to be issued in error or obtained improperly.

## 8. Expiry and revocation
Certificates and badges may expire and require renewal (see Certificate Expiry/Renewal Rules) and may be revoked (see Badge Revocation Rules).

## 9. Governing law
These Rules are governed by Israeli law. This English version is for convenience; once issued, the Hebrew version controls.
