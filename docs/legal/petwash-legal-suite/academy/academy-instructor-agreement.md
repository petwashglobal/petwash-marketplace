# Academy Instructor Agreement

This Agreement is between **PET WASH LTD / פט וואש בע״מ**, company no. **517145033**, Israel ("**PetWash**") and the individual or entity engaged to deliver PetWash Academy training (the **"Instructor"**).

## 1. Engagement
The Instructor is engaged to prepare and/or deliver Academy content and assessments as agreed in writing. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship unless a separate employment contract is signed; an Instructor engaged as an independent contractor is responsible for their own tax, National Insurance, and any required business/Osek registration.

## 2. Instructor warranties
The Instructor warrants that they: are lawfully entitled to provide the services in Israel; hold any qualifications represented to PetWash; will deliver content accurately and safely; and will not make false, exaggerated, or misleading claims about animal handling, hygiene, or equipment.

## 3. Content ownership and licence
All course materials the Instructor creates for PetWash, and all PetWash-supplied materials, are owned by PetWash (or licensed to it). The Instructor assigns to PetWash all intellectual property in commissioned course materials. The Instructor may not reuse, publish, or sell PetWash materials outside the Academy.

## 4. Standards and conduct
The Instructor will follow the Academy Code of Conduct, the Content & Copyright Rules, and all PetWash safety and animal-welfare standards. The Instructor will not collect students' personal data for their own purposes and will handle any data only as PetWash directs, consistent with the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law (including Amendment 13).

## 5. No authority to approve providers
The Instructor has **no authority** to approve any student as a PetWash provider, to promise bookings, work, income, or payouts, or to represent that a certificate or badge replaces a government licence or insurance. Provider approval is a separate PetWash admin process.

## 6. Assessment integrity
The Instructor will assess students honestly and consistently, will not issue or recommend certificates for incomplete or failed work, and will report suspected cheating or impersonation.

## 7. Fees and invoicing
Fees are as agreed in writing and are paid against a valid tax invoice issued in line with Israeli law (VAT 18% where applicable). PetWash may withhold tax at source where required.

## 8. Confidentiality
The Instructor will keep PetWash business, student, and operational information confidential during and after the engagement.

## 9. Term and termination
Either party may terminate on written notice as agreed. PetWash may suspend or terminate immediately for breach of safety, integrity, confidentiality, or conduct obligations.

## 10. Liability and indemnity
The Instructor is responsible for their own acts and omissions in delivering training and will indemnify PetWash against third-party claims arising from the Instructor's breach, negligence, or false claims.

## 11. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by Israeli law; the competent courts of the Tel Aviv–Jaffa district have exclusive jurisdiction.
