# SDD: Supplier & Multi-Service Provider Verification, Invoice Control, SUMIT/Accountant Handoff, Fraud-Control & Israeli Compliance (Pet Wash Ltd)

Status: Draft (design only — no code, no migrations, no routes). Date: 2026-05-22. Method: `.github/skills/sdd-writer-iterative/SKILL.md`. Feature flags (all default OFF): `ff.supplier_invoice_control.enabled`, `ff.provider_compliance_declarations.enabled`, `ff.supplier_invoice_control.sumit_send.enabled`.

Secret handling (exact): SUMIT credentials are already stored in Pet Wash secrets and must be accessed server-side via environment variables. The design must not expose, duplicate, request, or document the secret.

Design principle (reuse-first): This SDD does not introduce a new legal-signature system. It extends the existing versioned signature, declaration, archival, and evidence-retention backbone — `subcontractorSignatures`, `Israeli2025SignatureService`, DocuSeal, `DriveArchivalService`/`googleDriveBackupService`, `googleSheetsIntegration`, and the append-only `businessLegalIdDocuments` pattern — to cover all provider service arms. No duplicate signature, storage, or legal-document workflow is created; every signed document, declaration, snapshot, IP/UA record, hash, Drive archive and Sheets row stays traceable, versioned and audit-ready.

## Company, accountant and SUMIT reality (existing, not theoretical)

Pet Wash Ltd is a real registered Israeli company — פט וואש בע"מ / Pet Wash Ltd, company number 517145033, incorporated 2 April 2025 — with an active Bank Mizrahi-Tefahot account and a live bookkeeping relationship. Pet Wash Ltd already works with an accounting firm, רו"ח קופרברג, עזרא ושות', which already connected Pet Wash to SUMIT and uses SUMIT to request and collect bookkeeping documents, expenses and invoices. The accountant is an existing external stakeholder/system user, not optional and not "later." SUMIT is the existing, trusted bookkeeping/document-collection platform connected to the accounting firm. The Pet Wash system must NOT replace SUMIT or the accountant; it sits BEFORE them as the internal control layer (supplier/provider verification, fraud detection, invoice approval, bank verification, job matching, payment control) so that only clean, approved, traceable documents ever reach SUMIT/accountant. Correct flow: provider uploads invoice/document → Pet Wash verifies provider, supplier status, tax documents, bank account, invoice, duplicate/fraud risk and job match → only clean approved invoices go to SUMIT/accountant → accountant/bookkeeper handles official accounting → payment is released only after all approval rules are satisfied.

Sensitive company-records handling: Pet Wash Ltd's own legal/financial documents (certificate of incorporation, bank-management confirmation, accountant engagement/fee invoice) seed the company entity, accountant and supplier records. Sensitive values — bank account number, IBAN, SWIFT — are NEVER written into this SDD, code, logs, commits, screenshots or tests; they are stored encrypted server-side only, exactly like the SUMIT secret. This document references the company's verified Mizrahi-Tefahot account only as "on file (encrypted)."

## Strategic direction: one backbone, many arms (Uber-style)

Pet Wash Ltd should be a trusted provider platform across several pet-service arms, like a multi-arm platform with strong independent business lines. Arms: Pet Wash (K9000), Pet Sitting (The Sitter Suite), Dog Walking (Walk My Pet), Pet Wash Academy, Pet Training, grooming, transport, and future pet-care services. The design principle: one company backbone; many service arms; one trusted provider identity; different provider permissions per service; central supplier documents, central bank verification, central fraud controls, central audit trail, central SUMIT/accountant handoff, central payment-control rules; and service-specific requirements per arm (documents, training, insurance, compliance checks, pricing rules, job matching, payment logic). A provider may later be approved for additional service categories, each with its own requirements.

## Core rules (non-negotiable)

- No supplier is trusted until verified.
- No provider can work in a service category until approved for that category.
- No invoice is trusted until scanned.
- No invoice is paid until matched to real work and approved.
- No bank account is used until verified.
- SUMIT/accountant receive only clean, approved, traceable documents.
- The Provider Legal Agreement and Semi-Annual Status Declaration is a required legal/compliance layer. It creates evidence, workflow, reminders, audit trail and controls; it does NOT replace accountant/legal review.

## Provider Legal Agreement and Semi-Annual Status Declaration (required legal/compliance layer)

Purpose: protect Pet Wash Ltd by holding signed, versioned evidence that each provider/supplier declared their own business/tax status, bank details, invoice responsibilities and compliance status — good-faith-compliance evidence if an authority, accountant, auditor or legal claim later questions how Pet Wash dealt with providers. This does NOT replace accountant/legal review; it creates evidence, workflow, reminders, audit trail and controls so Pet Wash can prove it asked, recorded, verified and monitored provider status.

Onboarding agreement: every provider/supplier must digitally sign onboarding terms before they can work or be paid, confirming their details are true, their tax/business status is correct, invoices will be accurate, duplicate invoices are forbidden, bank changes require approval, and Pet Wash may delay or block payment if documents, tax status, bank details, job match or fraud checks are not clean. Reuse `Israeli2025SignatureService`/`DocuSealService` + `subcontractorSignatures` with the existing `providerHostAgreement` version text; pin the signed version in the new `provider_agreements`.

Status declaration: every provider/supplier must digitally declare their current legal/tax status — עוסק מורשה / עוסק פטור / חברה בע"מ / individual-private (only if legally allowed and approved by accountant/legal) / other (only if explicitly approved). The declaration captures: legal name; business name; business number (ח.פ / ע.מ / ת.ז where legally required); VAT status; whether they issue tax invoices/receipts; whether they have valid אישור ניהול ספרים; whether they have valid אישור ניכוי מס במקור; bank account owner name; bank, branch, account number (stored encrypted, never in this SDD); insurance status if relevant; service categories they provide; confirmation the information is true and current; and consent that Pet Wash may store the declaration and supporting documents for compliance, bookkeeping, fraud prevention, audit and accountant review.

Recurring six-month rule: every six months each active provider must re-sign/re-confirm current status by email link, digital form or provider portal. If not completed: status becomes compliance_pending; new jobs are blocked or limited by risk; invoice approval becomes yellow/manual or blocked by policy; payment may be held until the declaration is complete, subject to legal/accountant advice.

Versioning (never overwrite): every signed contract and six-month declaration is saved as evidence; old declarations are preserved when a provider changes status (e.g. עוסק פטור→עוסק מורשה, individual→company, or bank change) — model on `businessLegalIdDocuments` (`supersededById`, SHA-256 hash, retention). `provider_declaration_versions` keeps each version immutably.

Storage as evidence: per-provider records — a per-provider Drive folder (reuse `googleDriveBackupService` folder + `folderLink`), the signed PDF archived via `DriveArchivalService` (non-blocking, writes Drive ID + SHA-256), an index row in a new `googleSheetsIntegration` "Provider Declarations" tab next to the existing LEGAL_AGREEMENTS/E_SIGNATURES tabs, the backend provider profile record, and an audit-log entry with timestamp, IP/user-agent if available, email used, document version, and signature/approval method. Capture responses in-app (Google Forms is only a manual-setup stub).

## 6. Invoice upload

Sources: provider portal, admin, email parser, WhatsApp, manual. On upload: store PDF (KYC storage pattern), compute SHA-256 file hash, run OCR to extract number/supplier/business-number/date/amounts/VAT/visible bank, run checks (section 7), set status ready_for_approval (GREEN) / needs_review (YELLOW) / blocked (RED). Provider sees only a neutral status; fraud logic is never exposed to the provider.

## 7. Duplicate and fraud detection

Reuse `analyzeReceipt` + `fraudScan`; one `invoice_checks` row per rule. Rules and indicative score impact: exact duplicate file hash RED +100; duplicate invoice number same supplier RED +100; job/PO already invoiced RED +100; bank mismatch vs verified account RED +80; business-number mismatch RED +80; supplier-name mismatch YELLOW +40 (RED if business number also mismatches); same amount + close date + same supplier YELLOW/RED +40; high layout/visual similarity with matching amount/bank/number YELLOW/RED +50; missing tax documents YELLOW +30; bank details missing from invoice YELLOW +25; no job/PO linked for operational expense YELLOW +20; VAT math mismatch or VAT from an exempt dealer YELLOW +20; amount over threshold needs finance-manager approval +15. Levels: GREEN 0–24 (normal), YELLOW 25–69 (manual note), RED 70–100 (cannot be paid; only finance manager/super admin may override, override audited).

## 8. Bank-account verification

Accounts carry status pending/verified/rejected/old/blocked + method (manual/bank document/micro-deposit/accountant-confirmed). New or changed accounts always require manual finance approval and are never auto-used. Invoice bank details not matching a verified account → RED; missing → YELLOW. All bank changes are audited and notified and create a bank_change_pending state. Sensitive fields encrypted at rest; never written to docs/logs.

## 9. Job matching

Operational invoices link to a completed booking/job (reuse `bookings`/`superAppPayouts.bookingId` + dual-approval completion at `server/routes/booking-requests.ts:1455`): job completed, invoice provider equals job provider, amount within tolerance, job not already invoiced (RED if it is). General business expenses (accountant, rent, software) match a `supplierContract`/PO or recurring-approved expense; no-job-linked is YELLOW for operational suppliers, acceptable for general expenses.

## 10. SUMIT / accountant handoff

The accountant is the existing firm רו"ח קופרברג, עזרא ושות', already using SUMIT to collect Pet Wash's bookkeeping documents/expenses. The system does not replace SUMIT or the accountant; it feeds them only clean, approved, traceable documents. Only an internally approved, non-RED (or explicitly overridden) invoice from an approved supplier may be sent; status → sent_to_sumit, send audited. Connector is server-side only with a safe config wrapper and supports at least one of: SUMIT API call, SUMIT expense-email forward, or a manual export package (ZIP of PDFs + CSV + supplier docs + approval log), modeled on the existing `sendTaxReportToAccountant` + monthly-package flow. Secret rule restated: SUMIT credentials are already stored in Pet Wash secrets and must be accessed server-side via environment variables; the design must not expose, duplicate, request, or document the secret. Accountant statuses: not_sent → sent_to_sumit → received_by_accountant → (missing_details → back to admin/provider) → entered_in_books → rejected_by_accountant. The accountant is read-only + comments + bookkeeping status; the accountant cannot confirm operational work was done — only Pet Wash admin confirms service delivery.

## 11. Israeli bookkeeping, tax, VAT, withholding and compliance

Pet Wash Ltd has live obligations: מס הכנסה, מע״מ, ניהול ספרים, ניכוי מס במקור, supplier documentation, and reporting. Per invoice store amount-before-VAT, VAT, total, VAT rate (ISRAEL_VAT_RATE 0.18) and VAT-eligibility; flag VAT math errors and VAT from exempt dealers. Store each supplier's withholding rate (from their אישור ניכוי מס במקור; reuse `resolveWithholdingRate`/`withholdingRemittanceLedger`); flag missing withholding approval for accountant review. Remind before אישור ניהול ספרים / ניכוי מס במקור / insurance / agreement / declaration expire. Respect SHAAM e-invoicing thresholds when an allocation/clearing number is required. Full immutable audit trail per invoice. Extend `providerTaxCompliance` to add חברה בע"מ (company) and ניהול ספרים/bookkeeping-approval status, backing the richer entity types currently only in the rule engine.

## 12. ביטוח לאומי / employee-vs-contractor risk flagging

## Acceptance criteria (SDD approved only when all satisfied)

Existing signature infrastructure is reused (no new signature system). Existing legal text files (`providerHostAgreement.ts`, `providerDeclaration.ts`) are reused. No duplicate legal/signature system is introduced. Every agreement and every declaration is versioned and snapshotted (exact accepted text preserved). IP address and user agent are captured. DocuSeal integration is preserved. Drive archival is used. Sheets audit logging is used. SHA-256 evidence hashing is used. Supersession follows the existing append-only evidence model (`businessLegalIdDocuments` `supersededById`). Six-month declaration reconfirmation is included. Reminder tiers follow the existing `insuranceMonitoring.ts` pattern (30/14/7/expiry-day). Auto-suspension logic is included and is arm-specific where possible. Platform-arm-specific compliance is supported (a provider may be compliant for one arm and not another). Admin review is supported (fully/partially compliant, pending declaration, pending agreement, expired, suspended, requires manual review). Failure states are visible and never silently ignored: if DocuSeal signing succeeds but Drive archival fails, the signed record is still retained and queued for archival retry; if Sheets append fails, retry or flag admin; if hash generation fails, the record is not marked fully archived; an expired declaration whose reminders failed is still flagged internally; no provider is considered compliant merely because a background task failed silently. No code is written before SDD approval.
