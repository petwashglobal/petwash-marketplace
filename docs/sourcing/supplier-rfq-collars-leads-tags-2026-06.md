## Decision gates before ordering
- [ ] Sample received + quality checked (stitching, hardware, engraving contrast).
- [ ] Israel shipping + total landed cost (duty + VAT 18%) confirmed.
- [ ] Trade Assurance / payment protection in place.
- [ ] Per-product VAT-inclusive retail price set (use the shop's pricing).
- [ ] Brand/packaging artwork supplied (logo, care label — "engraved in Israel" if final personalization is done locally).

## Note on "engraved in Israel"
Fetchingware's hook is "designed/engraved in Australia." PetWash's equivalent — **import blank/standard collars + tags, do the personalization (engraving) locally in Israel** — lets you truthfully say "personalized in Israel," keeps fast turnaround on custom names, and avoids per-order China lead time. This is why the in-house engraving machine (see machine-decision doc) matters.
