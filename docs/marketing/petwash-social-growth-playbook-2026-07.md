## 0. Hard guardrails (read first — non-negotiable)

Every post, caption, comment reply, and reshared story MUST comply. These are Israeli marketing-law + PetWash brand rules:

- **No fake reviews / fabricated testimonials / fake UGC.** Real customers only, WITH written consent. A new station/provider shows "New" — never an invented rating or "trusted by thousands."
- **No guaranteed-safety / outcome claims.** Banned: "100% בטוח", "מובטח", "guaranteed", "always safe." Safe form: *"אנחנו פועלים בתהליכי בדיקה, בטיחות ופיקוח סבירים."*
- **No medical / veterinary claims.** Cosmetic wash only. No treating/curing/preventing skin or health conditions.
- **No unverified product claims.** No "טבעי", "אורגני", "היפואלרגני", "chemical-free" unless certification is on file. Safe form: *"מוצרי רחצה ייעודיים לכלבים."*
- **No fake/countdown discounts.** Wash is **₪55 (כולל מע"מ)**. Discounts are K9000-only, capped ~10% (member 5%, Black 15%). Never invent "was ₪X now ₪Y" or a blanket municipal discount.
- **No employment-style language for providers.** They are *ספקים עצמאיים* (independent providers), never "employees/staff/salary/shift."
- **Product & brand names stay English, LTR:** PetWash™, K9000. Copy is Hebrew-first, English secondary.
- **No PII in public copy** — no customer address, phone, ID, or exact provider home address.
- **Truthful, substantiated claims only.** One live station (Park Wald / פארק יצחק ולד, כפר סבא, 2 K9000 bays). A second Kfar Saba station is "בקרוב" — say "opening soon," never "already open."

### Positioning statement
> PetWash™ is the premium way to wash your dog yourself — a clean, well-lit K9000 self-service station where you do the fun part and we handle the mess, the water, and the warm dryer. לוקסרי, לא מסובך.

**One-line (He):** "רחצת הכלב שלך — פרימיום, עצמאית, בלי הבלגן בבית."
**One-line (En):** "Wash your dog like a pro. Skip the bathroom flood."

**Tone:** warm, confident, a little playful, visually clean (white / black / gold). Dogs are the heroes; the brand is the calm luxury frame around them.

### The 5 content pillars

| # | Pillar | What it is | Why it works | Cadence |
|---|--------|-----------|--------------|---------|
| P1 | **Before / After transformations** | Muddy dog → fluffy clean dog, real washes with consent | Instant proof, endlessly shareable, zero claims needed | 2×/wk |

> Rough mix per week: 40% P1+P3 (reach), 25% P2 (conversion), 20% P4 (local), 15% P5 (brand/loyalty).

## 2. Platform strategy 2026 — short-form video first

**Principle:** ONE weekly pillar shoot → repurposed into **5–7 vertical clips** across TikTok, IG Reels, and FB Reels. Short-form video is the growth engine; static posts and stories are support.

### Platform roles
| Platform | Primary role | Format priority | Posting rhythm |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|----------------|
| **TikTok** `@petwashltd` | Reach / discovery / personality | 9–20s vertical, trend-aware, native captions | 5–7×/wk |
| **IG Reels** `@petwashltd` | Reach + conversion + brand look | 9–25s vertical, polished, on-brand covers | 5×/wk + 3–5 stories/day |
| **FB Reels** `/petwashltd` | Local Kfar Saba reach (older dog owners) | Same Reels, re-uploaded natively (not just cross-post) | 4–5×/wk |

**Rules of the road**
- **Vertical 9:16 only** for video. Shoot in landscape? Re-frame vertical before posting.
- **Native upload each platform** (download clean export, re-upload) — do NOT rely on IG→FB auto-cross-post; native gets more reach and lets captions differ.
- **Captions in the video** (burned-in Hebrew text) for sound-off viewing.
- **First 1.5 seconds = the hook.** Muddy dog, funny face, or a bold on-screen line.
- **Hebrew-first captions**, brand names in English LTR. One clear CTA per post.

### The weekly repurposing workflow (the core engine)

**One 45–90 min shoot per week produces the whole week's content.**

1. **Shoot day (once/wk, ~1 hr at Park Wald):** capture 2–3 real washes (with signed consent). Get: the muddy arrival, the wash, the conditioner, the warm dry, the fluffy reveal, plus 2–3 candid dog reactions and a couple of clean B-roll shots of the bay.
2. **Cut the hero clip (P1 before/after, 15–20s):** the transformation. This is the anchor of the week.
3. **Repurpose that same footage into 5–7 clips:**
   - Clip A — P1 full before/after (hero).
   - Clip B — P2 "how it works in 60s" (calm walkthrough from the same session).
   - Clip C — P3 reaction moment (the dryer face / zoomies), 9–12s.
   - Clip D — P1 "just the reveal" cut, fast, trending audio.
   - Clip E — P4 local: same dog + a Park Wald/Kfar Saba frame + geo tag.
   - Clip F — P5 soft brand/member perk mention over B-roll.
   - Clip G (optional) — a stitch/duet or a "reply to a comment" clip.
4. **Caption + CATch pass:** write Hebrew caption, add burned-in hook text, one CTA, 4–8 targeted hashtags. Run through legal guardrails.
5. **Schedule across the week** (native upload each platform), spacing clips so P1/P3 anchor high-reach days.
6. **Stories/support:** slice stills and 5s teasers for daily IG/FB stories; poll stickers, "which dog?" questions.

> Result: 1 shoot → ~6 Reels/TikToks + ~15 story frames = a full week, sustainably, on a lean budget.

## 4. Hook formulas + caption templates (Hebrew-first)

### Hook formulas (first 1.5s, burned-in text)
1. **Transformation tease:** "מבוץ ל[תוצאה] תוך [X] דקות" → "מבוץ לפרוותי תוך 15 דקות."
2. **Curiosity gap:** "הפרצוף שהוא עשה כש..." → "הפרצוף שהוא עשה למייבש."
3. **Number/list:** "3 טעויות ברחצת כלב" — saves-bait.
4. **Relatable pain:** "בלי הצפה באמבטיה בבית."
5. **Local flag:** "בוקר של כלבים בפארק יצחק ולד, כפר סבא."
6. **Reverse reveal:** show the clean dog first, then "רגע... ככה הוא הגיע."

### Caption template (fill-in)
```
[הוק במשפט אחד עם אימוג'י אחד]
```

## 5. Local-first growth tactics

The whole business is one station in Kfar Saba. Growth = owning the local dog community first, not chasing national vanity reach.

1. **Geo-tag everything.** Every post tags **פארק יצחק ולד / Park Wald, Kfar Saba**. Turn on location on TikTok posts. Local reach converts far better than random reach.
2. **Local hashtags:** `#כפרסבא #כלביםכפרסבא #כלבים #פארקולד` alongside brand tags. Keep the set tight (4–8), rotate.
3. **Show up in local dog communities (as a member, not a spammer):** Kfar Saba / Sharon-area dog owner Facebook groups and WhatsApp communities. Post genuinely helpful before/after clips and answer questions; disclose you're PetWash. No fake accounts, no fake praise.
4. **Real UGC — with consent, always.** In-station consent card / one-line form: *"אני מאשר/ת ל-PetWash™ לצלם ולפרסם תוכן מהרחצה שלי ברשתות."* Only content with a signed yes gets posted. Tag the customer only if they agree. New brand = no fabricated ratings, ever.
5. **In-station QR to follow.** A clean white/black/gold sign at each bay: "צילמתם? תייגו אותנו @petwashltd" + QR that opens the IG/TikTok profile. Turns every wash into a potential post and a follow.
6. **Reward real tags (within discount policy).** If a customer posts and tags us, a member-level perk (within the K9000-only, ~capped policy) — framed truthfully, never a fake "limited-time" pressure. Keep it simple; don't overpromise.
7. **Local micro-collabs:** invite Kfar Saba dog owners with real followings for a genuine wash + honest post. Disclose any collaboration ("שיתוף פעולה") per Israeli disclosure rules. No paid-for-fake-praise.
8. **"Opening soon" for station #2:** build a local waitlist/anticipation — but always labeled "בקרוב," never "כבר פתוח."

## 6. KPIs to track weekly (maps to an in-house dashboard)

Track these every week (per platform + combined). This list is the spec for what an in-house social dashboard should surface — no paid analytics vendor needed; native TikTok/IG/FB insights cover it.

| Metric | What it tells us | Weekly target signal |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| **Followers (net new)** | Audience growth | Trending up week over week |
| **Reach / accounts reached** | Top-of-funnel size | Non-followers % rising = discovery working |
| **Saves** | Content usefulness (esp. P2) | High saves = strong "how-to" resonance |
| **Shares** | Virality / word-of-mouth | High shares = P1/P3 hitting |
| **Profile taps / visits** | Interest → consideration | Rising after reach spikes |
| **Link clicks (bio/CTA)** | Intent → action | The real conversion proxy |
| **Best posting time** | When our audience is on | Feeds scheduling |
| **Top format / pillar** | What to make more of | Double down weekly |
| **Avg watch time / completion %** | Hook & retention quality | ≥ platform benchmark for short-form |
| **Comments + comment sentiment** | Community health | Real, unprompted engagement |

**Derived weekly numbers to compute:**
- **Engagement rate** = (saves + shares + comments + likes) / reach.
- **Discovery ratio** = non-follower reach / total reach.
- **Click-through proxy** = link clicks / profile taps.

**Cadence:** a 15-minute Monday review — pull the 10 metrics, note the single top clip and the single weakest, and let that decide the coming week's emphasis. Log to a simple sheet (or the in-house dashboard) so trends are visible over 90 days.

> **Guardrail on KPIs:** never buy followers/likes/views and never publish a follower/engagement figure externally as social proof until it's real and material. Vanity metrics that imply popularity we don't have = a misleading-claim risk.

## 7. 90-day "fly it up" ramp

Realistic milestones for an early-stage single-station brand on a lean budget. Numbers are directional goals, not guarantees.

### Days 1–30 — Foundation & rhythm
- Optimize all 3 profiles: real logo, white/black/gold, Hebrew-first bio, Park Wald location, one working bio link, consistent handle.
- Lock the **weekly 1-hour shoot** habit and run the repurposing workflow.
- Ship the 30-day calendar. Post daily short-form + daily stories.
- Install the in-station QR + consent card at both bays.
- Start posting genuinely in 2–3 local Kfar Saba dog communities.
- **Milestone:** consistent daily posting live; first 5–10 real before/after clips banked; baseline KPIs recorded; first consented UGC captured.

### Days 31–60 — Local traction
- Identify the 2–3 top-performing formats from month-1 KPIs; shift the mix toward them.
- Land 2–3 real local micro-collabs (disclosed) and reshare consented customer content.
- Turn on light story interactivity (polls, "which dog?", Q&A) daily.
- Begin an "opening soon" thread for station #2 to build local anticipation.
- **Milestone:** meaningful local reach in Kfar Saba; rising profile taps & link clicks; a repeatable content engine that runs from one weekly shoot; first wave of tagged, consented customer posts.

### Days 61–90 — Compounding & conversion
- Publish a monthly "best before/afters" compilation (P1) as an anchor piece.
- Tie social CTAs to the loyalty/membership signup path (soft, truthful) and watch link-click → signup.
- Formalize a small always-on UGC loop: QR → tag → (policy-safe) member perk → reshare.
- Prep launch content for station #2 so it opens with an audience already warmed.
- **Milestone:** clear week-over-week growth curve across followers/reach; a bank of 30–40 reusable clips; social driving measurable profile taps and link clicks; local brand recognition in Kfar Saba dog circles; content ready for station #2 launch.

**Guiding principle throughout:** consistency + real transformations + local-first beats budget. One honest muddy-to-fluffy clip a day, tagged to Kfar Saba, compounds. No shortcuts that touch a guardrail.
