# How PetWash Works

**For customers — Version 1.0, 22 June 2026 · Pet Wash Ltd**

> A plain-language guide to what PetWash is, how pricing works, and your rights. This is the
> companion to the Provider & Host Rulebook. Customer-facing copy; the Hebrew version mirrors this
> one-to-one (he/RTL).

---

## What PetWash is

PetWash is two things in one place:

1. **K9000 self-service wash stations** — our own smart pet-wash machines, owned and run by
   PetWash Ltd. Wash your dog yourself in minutes, pay per wash or with credits.
2. **The marketplace** — a network of independent, verified providers (groomers, walkers, sitters,
   trainers, transporters, hosts) you can book directly through the app.

Plus **Prestige membership** — your member pass, wallet, credits, and benefits, all in the app.

---

## Your pass & membership

- Your member pass lives **in the app** — open it any time to show your QR at a station or to a
  provider. It works on its own; no extra download needed.
- Members get credits, faster checkout, and member benefits.
- Apple Wallet support is rolling out; until then your in-app pass does everything you need.

---

## How pricing works (no surprises)

- **The price you see is the price you pay.** Totals are shown all-in, before you confirm —
  consistent with Israeli consumer law. No hidden fees added at the end.
- **K9000 washes:** fixed, simple pricing (single wash or multi-wash credit packs — better value
  per wash).
- **Marketplace services:** each provider sets their own fair price (above a quality minimum, so
  no junk listings). You see the full price before booking.
- **Discounts & promo codes** apply to **K9000 washes** (our own machines). Members and eligible
  customers get capped, honest discounts — we never advertise a discount we don't actually give.

---

## Booking a provider

1. Browse verified providers near you.
2. See real prices, real reviews, and availability.
3. Book and pay securely in the app — keep all chat **in the app** so everything's logged and
   protected. No need to share your phone number.
4. After the service, your payment is released to the provider once the booking is complete and
   your cancellation window has passed.

Every provider is identity-verified and agrees to our Rulebook (welfare, conduct, safety).

---

## Cancellations & refunds

- You have cancellation rights under Israeli Consumer Protection law, including a **14-day
  cooling-off** period for qualifying purchases, with any cancellation fee capped at the **lower of
  5% or ₪100**.
- Cancel earlier and you get more back — refunds are **tiered and fair**, shown to you before you
  confirm.
- If a service can't be delivered, you're made whole through the app. Refunds in progress show
  clearly as **"refund being processed"** — we never pretend money moved before it did.

---

## Your privacy

- We protect your data under the Privacy Protection Law (including Amendment 13).
- We don't over-collect, and we don't sell your personal data.
- In-app chat keeps your contact details private from providers.

---

## A note on safety & insurance

- Providers are independent professionals responsible for their own service and insurance.
- PetWash is a platform that vets and connects — **not an insurer**. For total peace of mind,
  agree expectations in writing with your provider before a stay or service.
