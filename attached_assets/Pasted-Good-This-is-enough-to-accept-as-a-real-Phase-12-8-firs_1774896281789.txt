Business verdict

You did the right thing by not building this as a generic inbox.

You built it as an exception queue, which is exactly what operations and finance need.

That matters because once volume grows, nobody cares about “all events”.
They care about:
	•	what is broken
	•	what is aging
	•	what breaches SLA
	•	who owns the next action
	•	what money or trust risk exists right now

That is the correct business logic.

Why I would approve it

1. The queue is built around business exceptions, not noise

You separated:
	•	disputes
	•	settlement mismatches
	•	unresolved refunds

That is the right split because each one has:
	•	different urgency
	•	different owner
	•	different resolution workflow
	•	different financial impact

2. SLA logic makes it operational

This is the important part.

Without age and SLA, the queue is just a list.
With SLA, it becomes a control tool.

That is what owners actually need.

3. Severity + owner + direct booking trace link is correct

This is strong.

A queue row must answer three questions immediately:
	•	how bad is this
	•	who owns it
	•	where do I go next

You covered all three.

4. Network boundaries are preserved

Also very important.

If a franchise owner can see another network’s disputes or refunds, that becomes a trust and legal problem.
So keeping the same scope model as 12.7 is the right move.

⸻

What this phase is now

I would describe it as:

“an exception management queue for disputes, mismatches, and refunds, with SLA visibility and ownership.”

I would not describe it yet as:
	•	full workflow orchestration
	•	enterprise case management
	•	full finance operations platform

That keeps the claim honest.

⸻

One thing still matters a lot

You said:

The queue is accessible at /case-queue

That is good, but from a business standpoint access path matters.

If users need to remember a URL, the feature is not operationally finished.

So before calling this fully polished, the next small closure step should be:

Add visible entry points

From:
	•	franchise dashboard
	•	company finance dashboard
	•	station dashboard
	•	booking trace page when dispute exists

Because exception queues should be one click away from where pain is felt.
