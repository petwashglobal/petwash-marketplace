PETWASH PLATFORM
FINANCIAL LOGIC & MONEY FLOW ARCHITECTURE
FULL SPECIFICATION – 2026

Purpose
The PetWash platform operates as a hybrid system containing both:
1) Marketplace transactions involving third-party providers
2) Direct platform sales made by PetWash Ltd itself

These two flows must never be mixed in financial logic, taxation handling, reporting, receipts, or legal explanations.

Every payment in the system must be classified, processed, and reported according to its true financial nature.

------------------------------------------------

SECTION 1 — CORE FINANCIAL PRINCIPLE

Every financial transaction must belong to a clearly defined transaction type.

The system must never assume that every customer payment includes a provider.

There are multiple valid financial flows in the platform.

The system must treat each one separately.

------------------------------------------------

SECTION 2 — TRANSACTION TYPE CLASSIFICATION

Every transaction stored in the database must include a mandatory field:

transactionType

Allowed values:

marketplace_booking
direct_platform_sale
egift_sale
wallet_topup
provider_payout
platform_fee
processing_fee
vat_entry
escrow_hold
escrow_release
escrow_refund
refund
adjustment
chargeback

All financial calculations must branch based on this classification.

------------------------------------------------

SECTION 3 — FLOW A
MARKETPLACE BOOKING (WITH PROVIDER)

Example:
Customer books a pet sitter / walker / host via PetWash.

Financial flow:

Customer payment
↓
Payment processor / Nayax
↓
Payment captured
↓
VAT calculated where legally required
↓
Platform commission calculated
↓
Processing fees deducted
↓
Escrow / trust hold created
↓
Service completed
↓
Provider payout executed
↓
Final settlement recorded

Data elements required:

customerId
providerId
bookingId
grossAmount
platformFee
vatOnPlatformFee
processorFee
escrowAmount
payoutAmount
currency
timestamp

Important rule:

Provider earnings must always be calculated from the booking logic.

The platform never mixes provider earnings with platform-only sales.

------------------------------------------------

SECTION 4 — FLOW B
DIRECT PLATFORM SALE (NO PROVIDER)

Example:

User buys:
• e-gift card
• wallet credit
• promotional credit
• service bundle

directly from PetWash.

Financial flow:

Customer payment
↓
Payment processor / Nayax
↓
Processing fee deducted
↓
VAT handled on PetWash company sale
↓
Revenue recorded as PetWash income

Key rule:

NO provider exists in this flow.

Therefore:

No provider payout
No provider commission
No provider tax explanation
No provider ledger entry

Fields required:

saleId
customerId
grossAmount
processorFee
vatAmount
netRevenue
saleType (egift / wallet / bundle)

------------------------------------------------

SECTION 5 — WALLET / E-GIFT LOGIC

Gift cards and wallet credits represent stored value.

They require two accounting stages:

Stage 1 — purchase

Customer pays PetWash
↓
Revenue temporarily recorded as liability
↓
Wallet balance created

Stage 2 — redemption

Customer uses credit
↓
If service with provider → convert to marketplace flow
↓
If platform service → convert to direct sale

System must track:

walletBalance
walletSource
walletTransactionHistory

------------------------------------------------

SECTION 6 — ESCROW / TRUST MODEL

Marketplace bookings must support escrow protection.

Escrow states:

pending
held
released
refunded
disputed

Escrow lifecycle:

Payment captured
↓
Funds held in escrow
↓
Service confirmed
↓
Escrow released
↓
Provider payout

If dispute:

Escrow held
Admin review
Manual decision

------------------------------------------------

SECTION 7 — VAT HANDLING

VAT must only be applied where legally required.

Important distinction:

Marketplace flow
→ VAT applies to platform service portion.

Direct platform sale
→ VAT applies to the PetWash sale itself.

VAT must always be labeled as VAT.

It must never be labeled as income tax.

------------------------------------------------

SECTION 8 — PROVIDER TAX EXPLANATION

Provider tax explanation pages must clarify:

Income tax is progressive.

Example ranges shown for explanation only:

10%
14%
20%
31%
35%
47-50%

National insurance example ranges:

9% – 17%

These are informational only.

They do not represent taxes collected by the platform.

The platform must not present them as platform charges.

------------------------------------------------

SECTION 9 — PROCESSOR FEES

Payment processors (example: Nayax) charge fees.

Processing fees must be recorded as:

transactionType = processing_fee

Fields required:

processorName
feePercentage
feeAmount
currency
timestamp

SECTION 14 — DATA INTEGRITY RULES

The system must enforce:

Provider payouts only allowed when providerId exists.

Direct platform sales must never generate provider payouts.

Escrow records must always link to bookingId.

Wallet transactions must always link to customerId.

------------------------------------------------

SECTION 15 — ERROR CONDITIONS

System must detect and prevent:

Provider payout with no provider
VAT labeled incorrectly
Duplicate payouts
Wallet negative balance
Escrow release without booking completion
Provider tax labels applied to direct platform sales
