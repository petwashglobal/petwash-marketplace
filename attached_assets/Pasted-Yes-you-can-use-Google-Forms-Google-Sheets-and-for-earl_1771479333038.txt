Yes, you can use Google Forms + Google Sheets, and for early-stage onboarding it’s often the fastest way to stop “registration not saving” headaches.

But it’s not always “best” for KYC and anything involving ID documents. Here’s the clean 2026 way to think about it:

When Google Forms + Sheets is a good idea

Use it for non-sensitive intake and operations:
	•	Provider application (name, phone, service type, city, availability)
	•	Loyalty club sign up (email, phone, address, pet details)
	•	Booking inquiry requests (date, time window, service type)
	•	Support tickets and feedback
	•	Admin checklists and internal workflows

Why it helps
	•	Near zero bugs: Google handles form validation and saving
	•	Instant audit trail: timestamp + edits history
	•	Easy sharing with staff
	•	Easy export to CRM later

When Google Forms + Sheets is NOT the best idea

Avoid it for anything that includes government IDs, disability certificates, passports, selfies, biometric images:
	•	Forms can accept file uploads, but it stores files in Drive and links them in Sheets.
	•	That creates more places for leaks if permissions are wrong.
	•	Harder to enforce strict retention and deletion policies.
	•	Harder to do strong access control (KYC-admin role, per-record access).

For KYC, a safer pattern is:
	•	Your app uploads to secure storage with strict rules and auto-delete TTL.
	•	You store only verification result (pass/fail, score, timestamps, audit log) in your database.
	•	If you need Sheets, you push a “summary row” only (no files, no ID numbers).

Best “hybrid” setup for you

Front end: your site/app
Backend: your database + secure storage
Google Sheets: for operations visibility only

Examples:
	•	Sheet row contains: userId, name, phone, city, role, status, createdAt, lastStep, approvalDecision
	•	Sheet does not contain: passport number, ID number, file links, raw OCR text

Why your dev “Sheets only” approach keeps failing sometimes

Common causes:
	•	Using a public Apps Script endpoint without proper CORS
	•	Script not deployed as “Web App” correctly (permissions wrong)
	•	Rate limits (Apps Script has quotas)
	•	Field mismatch between frontend and Sheets columns
	•	Retries without idempotency cause duplicates or errors

	2.	Use the platform DB for accounts, authentication, roles, bookings, payments
	3.	Use secure KYC flow separate from Sheets, and push only a summary row
