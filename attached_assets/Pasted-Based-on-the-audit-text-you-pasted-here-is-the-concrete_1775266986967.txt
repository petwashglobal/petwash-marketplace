Top 20 issues from your pasted audit, cleaned up and de-duplicated

1) Fake payment success endpoint

Severity: Critical
Files: server/routes.ts:5156-5197
Issue: /api/express-checkout returns success for credit card flow without charging, auth, escrow, or DB write.
Why it matters: Customers can think they paid when they did not, or services can unlock without real payment.
Fix first: Disable immediately with 503 until real gateway flow exists.
Sensitive: Security, legal/compliance

2) Booking transition endpoint has no real ownership enforcement

Severity: Critical
Files: server/routes/marketplace-bookings.ts:548-577
Issue: Caller-controlled role and no verified booking-party ownership before transition.
Why it matters: Any authenticated user with a booking ID may change another booking’s state.
Fix first: Fetch booking, verify customerId or providerId, derive role server-side only.
Sensitive: Security, money flow

3) Customer can self-complete both sides of booking

Severity: Critical
Files: server/routes/marketplace-bookings.ts:630-665, shared/schema.ts transition map
Issue: Same user can submit both owner and provider completion states, then trigger payout release.
Why it matters: Escrow can be released fraudulently.
Fix first: Completion history must verify distinct actors tied to booking parties.
Sensitive: Security, payment

4) Dual escrow architectures running in parallel

Severity: Critical
Files: server/services/EscrowService.ts, server/services/BookingLifecycleService.ts, server/backgroundJobs.ts
Issue: Firestore escrow and Postgres escrow are both active with separate jobs and different statuses.
Why it matters: Double release, missed release, invisible disputes, broken reconciliation.
Fix first: Freeze new work on one path and choose one canonical escrow system.
Sensitive: Payment, compliance

5) SQL injection in prestige-pass.ts

Severity: Critical if still open
Files: server/routes/prestige-pass.ts multiple raw pool.query() interpolations
Issue: Raw string interpolation for status, month, assignee and other inputs.
Why it matters: Data exfiltration or data manipulation.
