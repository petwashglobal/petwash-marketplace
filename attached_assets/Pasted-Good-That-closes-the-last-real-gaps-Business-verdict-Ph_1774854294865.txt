Why these last 3 fixes matter

1. CHECK constraint

This is not cosmetic.

Without it, one bad write like:
	•	corp
	•	internal
	•	franshise

could silently corrupt finance behavior.

Now the database itself protects the business model.

2. Composite index

Also not cosmetic.

Your financial endpoints increasingly answer questions like:
	•	show franchise stations only
	•	show company-owned only
	•	aggregate by ownership scope

So indexing (franchise_id, ownership_type) is exactly the sort of quiet detail that keeps reporting fast when the network grows.

3. Two-segment route shape

This is the biggest architectural improvement.

/api/network/:ownerType/:ownerId/... is much cleaner than overloading one param and hoping "company" is understood specially.

Now the API says clearly:
	•	what kind of owner this is
	•	which owner this is

That reduces confusion later in auth, UI, exports, and integrations.
