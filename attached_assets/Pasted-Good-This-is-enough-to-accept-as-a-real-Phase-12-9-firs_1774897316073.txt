Why this is strong

1. Ownership is now real

This is the most important part.

A queue without ownership is just a complaint list.

Now each case can have:
	•	an assigned owner
	•	reassignment
	•	unassignment
	•	visible accountability

That means no more “someone should handle this”.
Now it becomes:
👉 this person owns this issue

That is serious operations logic.

⸻

2. Internal notes were necessary

Also correct.

Customer-facing comments and internal notes must never be mixed.

Internal notes are where teams record:
	•	what was checked
	•	what was decided
	•	what is blocked
	•	what still needs follow-up

Without this, people repeat the same work.

So yes:
👉 internal-only note storage is the right design

⸻

3. “My cases” vs “All cases” is not cosmetic

This matters operationally.

Managers need “all”.
Workers need “mine”.
Owners need both.

That one toggle is the difference between:
	•	overview
and
	•	execution

So this was the right feature to add now, not later.

⸻

4. Bulk actions were the correct next move

Very important.

When volume rises, one-case-at-a-time dies.

Bulk actions are what let the system survive:
	•	spikes
	•	operational backlog
	•	staffing gaps
	•	repeated low-risk cleanup

As long as bulk actions stay controlled, this is exactly right.

⸻

5. Partial unique index on active assignment = good logic

This is one of the best decisions here.

It means:
	•	one active owner at a time
	•	history preserved
	•	reassignment is traceable
	•	no fake duplicates

That is the right business rule.
