LEGAL THRESHOLD RULE

if transaction is B2B and taxable amount before VAT exceeds the legal threshold in force on the issue date, request allocation number before issuing the tax invoice. As of 1 Jan 2026 the threshold is 10,000 NIS before VAT, and from 1 Jun 2026 it is 5,000 NIS before VAT. (Government of Israel)

NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES

- mobile app must NEVER issue the legal invoice itself
- do NOT generate fake allocation numbers
- do NOT use Date.now as legal invoice numbering
- do NOT label internal PDFs as tax invoices
- payment processor moves money, but does not replace accounting issuance
- escrow is accounting/payment state, not wording on a PDF
- if the platform marketplace model is not finalized, do not guess the issuer
