Phase 11 - Execution Order (clean and strict)

The key principle:

👉 Money first → then visibility → then control → then comparison

Do NOT mix these. If you do, you’ll build messy dashboards with unreliable numbers.

✅ Step 1 - Canonical Financial Layer (foundation)

Task 27 - Franchise Financial Aggregation Engine

Before any UI.

You need one trusted source of truth for money at the franchise level.

Build:
	•	GET /api/franchise/:franchiseId/finance/summary

Returns:
	•	total revenue (gross)
	•	platform fees
	•	franchise share
	•	station payouts
	•	net margin (if applicable)

Time scopes:
	•	today
	•	MTD
	•	last 30 days

Source:
	•	station_settlements ONLY
(not bookings, not estimates)

👉 This is critical:
Everything in Phase 11 must anchor to settlements, not bookings.

⸻

✅ Step 2 - Station P&L Layer

Task 28 - Station Financial Breakdown (P&L style)

Now you go one level deeper.

Build:
	•	GET /api/franchise/:franchiseId/stations/financials

Per station:
	•	revenue generated
	•	station earnings
	•	platform cut
	•	franchise cut
	•	booking count
	•	avg order value
	•	dispute impact (optional but powerful)

This is your unit economics layer.

👉 This is where weak stations become visible.

⸻

✅ Step 3 - Payout & Reconciliation View

Task 29 - Payout Calendar & Settlement Status

Now show cash flow timing, not just totals.

Build:
	•	GET /api/franchise/:franchiseId/payouts

Returns:
	•	settlements grouped by payout cycle
	•	status:
	•	pending
	•	approved
	•	paid
	•	expected payout date
	•	total per cycle

Optional (but strong):
	•	reconciliation mismatch flag (if any)

👉 This answers:
“Who do I owe money to, and when?”

⸻

✅ Step 4 - Override & Risk Audit Layer

Task 30 - Network Audit Feed

Now control behavior.

Build:
	•	GET /api/franchise/:franchiseId/audit-feed

Include:
	•	manual station overrides
	•	bookings bypassing recommendation
	•	dispute openings
	•	refunds / anomalies
	•	downtime spikes

Each item:
	•	type
	•	stationId
	•	timestamp
	•	severity

👉 This is your fraud / misuse / operational discipline layer
