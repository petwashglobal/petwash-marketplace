7. MATCHING ENGINE (THIS IS THE BUSINESS LOGIC YOU SAID IS MISSING)

DO NOT use postcode.

Use:

distance = calculateDistance(
customer.lat,
customer.lng,
provider.lat,
provider.lng
)

Example:
	•	Walkers within 5km
	•	Sitters within 10km
	•	Transport dynamic radius

SQL example:

SELECT *
FROM providers
WHERE ST_DistanceSphere(
point(provider.lng, provider.lat),
point(:userLng, :userLat)
) < 5000
