/************************************************************************************************
PET WASH LTD
GOOGLE SERVICES ARCHITECTURE POLICY
Israel 2026
Canonical Data, Google API Roles, Scopes, Failure Rules, Email Authority
************************************************************************************************

DOCUMENT PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This document defines the official Google services architecture for Pet Wash Ltd.

It exists to prevent architectural drift and future developer mistakes.

The main rule is simple:

POSTGRESQL IS THE SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH.
GOOGLE SERVICES ARE SECONDARY TOOLS ONLY.

This rule is mandatory and non-negotiable.

************************************************************************************************
SECTION 1 - CANONICAL DATA BOUNDARY
************************************************************************************************

1.1 Canonical system of record
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following data must live in PostgreSQL only as the canonical source of truth:

- bookings
- booking status
- payment records
- wallet balances
- gift balances
- transaction ledger
- tax documents
- receipts
- tax invoices
- credit notes
- provider payouts
- provider commission records
- reconciliation reports
- audit logs
- immutable stamps
- refunds
- chargebacks
- compliance logs
- customer legal records

No Google product may be used as the system of record for any of the above.

1.2 Google data is derived only
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Google services may receive:
- exports
- copies
- PDFs
- calendar mirrors
- intake submissions
- reporting views
- operational dashboards

They may not hold the canonical financial or legal record.

1.3 Data boundary rule
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Google services are downstream outputs, not upstream authorities.

Correct:
Database -> generate invoice -> save canonical record -> export PDF to Drive

Incorrect:
Google Sheet row created -> treat sheet row as official invoice record

************************************************************************************************
SECTION 2 - GOOGLE SERVICE ROLE DEFINITIONS
************************************************************************************************

2.1 Google Calendar
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: KEEP

PURPOSE:
- provider schedules
- booking events
- reminders
- appointments
- operational calendar visibility

ALLOWED DATA:
- booking title
- time
- date
- provider
- location
- service category
- internal reference ID

NOT ALLOWED AS CANONICAL:
- booking financial state
- payment state
- invoice state
- payout state

RULE:
Calendar is a scheduling mirror only.
Booking truth remains in PostgreSQL.

2.2 Google Drive
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: KEEP AND EXTEND

PURPOSE:
- PDF receipts
- PDF tax invoices
- PDF credit notes
- PDF statements
- signed contracts
- HR files
- compliance and legal exports
- archived reports

RULE:
Drive stores file copies only.
Drive never replaces DB records.

MANDATORY IMPLEMENTATION RULE:
When a tax document is issued:
1. Create canonical DB record first
2. Generate PDF second
3. Upload PDF to Drive asynchronously
4. Store Drive file ID + checksum back into DB

Drive upload failure must never fail payment issuance.

2.3 Google Sheets
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: KEEP, STRICTLY SCOPED

PURPOSE:
- reconciliation review
- accounting exports
- HR trackers
- sales dashboards
- operational monitoring
- manual review lists
- finance exports
- provider payout review

NOT ALLOWED:
- live wallet balances
- real-time payment authority
- invoice authority
- tax document authority
- booking financial truth

RULE:
Sheets is reporting and review only.
Never write business-critical finance logic that depends on Sheets availability.

2.4 Google Forms
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: KEEP, STRICTLY SCOPED

PURPOSE:
- provider onboarding
- lead capture
- surveys
- support intake
- internal forms
- franchise/contact forms

NOT ALLOWED:
- receipt generation
- tax invoice generation
- payment capture authority
- financial ledger storage

RULE:
Forms is intake only.
Responses may be imported into DB, but Forms is not a legal or financial record system.

2.5 Gmail
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: DEMOTE TO FALLBACK

PRIMARY TRANSACTIONAL EMAIL SYSTEM:
SendGrid

Gmail may be used only for:
- fallback emergency sending
- manual administrative sending
- internal notifications if needed

Gmail must not be treated as the primary transactional engine if SendGrid is active.

REASON:
Two parallel transactional email authorities create inconsistency, template drift, and delivery confusion.

SECTION 4 - FAILURE SEMANTICS
************************************************************************************************

4.1 Google-optional flows
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following flows must continue even if Google APIs are down:

- direct machine payment
- wallet top-up
- wallet redeem at K9000
- provider booking charge
- tax document creation
- refund
- chargeback
- reconciliation creation
- audit logging

These flows may queue Google-related secondary actions for retry later.

4.2 Google-dependent flows
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following are allowed to degrade gracefully if Google is down:

- calendar mirror creation
- sheet export append
- drive archive upload
- form submission syncing
- optional Gmail fallback

4.3 Failure handling rule
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Google outage must never block:
- money capture
- tax document issuance
- audit trail creation
- wallet balance update
- provider payout state updates

************************************************************************************************
SECTION 5 - TAX DOCUMENT PIPELINE
************************************************************************************************

OFFICIAL PIPELINE:
1. TransactionEngine completes financial event
2. TaxDocumentService creates canonical tax document row in PostgreSQL
3. Document receives immutable ID and hash metadata
4. PDF generator produces file
5. Drive uploader pushes PDF asynchronously
6. Drive file ID + checksum written back to pw_tax_documents
7. If Drive fails:
   - tax document remains valid
   - retry queue picks up PDF upload later

MANDATORY RULE:
DB first.
Drive second.
Never reverse this order.

************************************************************************************************
SECTION 6 - OAUTH AND SERVICE ACCOUNT POLICY
************************************************************************************************

6.1 Principle of least privilege
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every Google integration must use the minimum scope necessary.

Do not use broad scopes if a narrower scope exists.

6.2 Single-purpose service accounts
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Recommended:
- one service account for Drive/Sheets exports
- one service account for Calendar integration if needed
- keep permissions scoped per project need

Do not use one overly privileged service account for all tasks if separation is possible.

6.3 No human mailbox as system-of-record
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No production-critical automation may depend on a human personal mailbox.

************************************************************************************************
SECTION 7 - RECOMMENDED GOOGLE SCOPES
************************************************************************************************

7.1 Google Calendar
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Recommended scope:
- https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events

Use narrower calendar event scopes where possible.
Avoid broad account-wide calendar scopes if not needed.

7.2 Google Drive
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Preferred scopes:
- drive.file if app-created files only
- drive.metadata.readonly for metadata-only reads
- drive.readonly where write is not needed

Avoid broad full-drive access unless absolutely necessary.

7.3 Google Sheets
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Preferred scopes:
- spreadsheets
or narrower read-only where only review/export reading is needed

Rule:
Sheets write access must be limited to export jobs, not general app runtime logic.

7.4 Google Forms
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Only enable Forms scopes if Forms is actively used.
Forms scopes should be limited to creation/read/update of forms and responses only.

7.5 Gmail
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If Gmail fallback is kept:
Use send-only permissions where possible.
Do not use broad mailbox read/write unless absolutely required.

************************************************************************************************
SECTION 8 - DATA CLASSIFICATION RULES
************************************************************************************************

8.1 Financial data
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Financial source-of-truth data must remain in PostgreSQL:
- transaction amounts
- VAT split
- wallet balances
- provider payouts
- invoice states
- settlement states
- refunds
- chargebacks

8.2 Legal documents
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Legal truth:
- DB row is canonical
- PDF in Drive is secondary representation

8.3 PII
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do not spread financial PII unnecessarily into:
- Sheets
- Forms
- broad Drive folder structures

PII minimization must be applied to exports.

************************************************************************************************
SECTION 9 - RECONCILIATION RULES
************************************************************************************************

Daily reconciliation must compare:
- processor reports
- payments table
- tax document table
- provider payouts
- wallet ledger
- failed retry queues

Sheets may receive reconciliation export copies for accountant review.

Sheets must not be the reconciliation authority.

Canonical reconciliation result belongs in PostgreSQL.

************************************************************************************************
SECTION 10 - EMAIL AUTHORITY RULE
************************************************************************************************

PRIMARY:
SendGrid

SECONDARY / FALLBACK:
Gmail

MANDATORY RULES:
- all production transactional templates belong to one primary system
- suppression, bounce, and delivery tracking should remain in one primary path
- Gmail fallback should be explicitly marked as fallback
- avoid dual live template maintenance

************************************************************************************************
SECTION 11 - WHAT DEVELOPERS MUST NOT DO
************************************************************************************************

FORBIDDEN:
- using Sheets as a live financial database
- using Forms as invoice/receipt engine
- storing canonical payment truth in Drive filenames or folders
- making wallet or payment success depend on Google API uptime
- treating Calendar event existence as booking truth
- maintaining two equal transactional email authorities
- opening broad Google scopes without explicit need
- spreading tax/legal truth across multiple Google tools

************************************************************************************************
SECTION 12 - REQUIRED IMPLEMENTATION CHECKLIST
************************************************************************************************

Developers must ensure:

[ ] PostgreSQL remains canonical for all money/legal flows
[ ] Google Drive upload is asynchronous and non-blocking
[ ] Drive file ID stored back into tax document DB row
[ ] Google Sheets exports are read/review/report only
[ ] Google Calendar mirrors booking schedule only
[ ] Google Forms used only for intake/onboarding/leads
[ ] Gmail explicitly documented as fallback only
[ ] OAuth scopes documented and minimized
[ ] Google failures do not block payment or tax issuance
[ ] Retry queues exist for Google secondary jobs
[ ] PII minimization applied in Sheets/Forms/Drive exports

SECTION 14 - FINAL NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE
************************************************************************************************

No developer, contractor, architect, or future team member may redesign the system so that
Google tools become the source of truth for money, tax, wallet, or legal records.

All such records must remain canonical in PostgreSQL.

Google services are auxiliary, reporting, scheduling, intake, or archival layers only.
