🎯 What matters in the next few days (this is the real work)

Don’t add anything. Just observe with intent.

1. Promotion behavior (watch closely)

You want to see:
	•	rules not jumping too quickly to L4
	•	most rules stabilizing at L2–L3 first

If you see:
	•	fast promotion → tighten criteria
	•	no promotion at all → criteria too strict

⸻

2. Demotion sensitivity

This is your safety net.

Check:
	•	do bad rules drop fast enough?
	•	or do they linger too long at higher levels?

If something goes wrong:
	•	demotion should feel immediate and protective, not delayed

⸻

3. Domain caps vs real workload

This is where reality hits design.

You’ll likely discover:
	•	some domains are too restrictive (blocking useful automation)
	•	others may be too loose (allowing risky behavior)

This is normal. Adjust gradually.

⸻

4. Guardrails under real volume

This is critical.

Watch:
	•	are rules hitting daily limits too early?
	•	or never hitting them at all?

You want:
	•	guardrails that activate occasionally, not constantly and not never

⸻

5. Decision log readability (very important)

Open it like an operator would.

Ask:
	•	can I understand why something happened in 3 seconds?
	•	or do I need to think?

If thinking is required → improve structure later
This log becomes your single source of truth in incidents
