The biggest business-risk he found

This is the most important line in his whole response:

there are two separate event systems running in parallel
EventBus and EventPublisher

That is dangerous.

Why this matters

If:
	•	EventPublisher writes to domain_events
	•	but NotificationEventHandlers only listens to EventBus

then you get a fake sense of completion.

Meaning:
	•	event is “published”
	•	database row exists
	•	but no notification actually fires

That is not just technical. It breaks business flows:
	•	booking complete review request may never go out
	•	gift redemption messages may never go out
	•	provider approval may never go out
	•	loyalty tier upgrade may never notify

So the real issue is not just “missing templates”.
It is event pipeline fragmentation.
