PAYMENT LOGOS FIX (2026 style, compliant look)

1) Do not “edit” logos yourself
- Do not recolor
- Do not add shadows
- Do not change aspect ratio
- Do not stretch to fit a box

2) Get official brand assets (correct files)
Use only official assets from:
- Apple Pay mark assets (Apple guidelines)
- Google Pay brand guidelines
- Visa brand assets
- Mastercard brand center
- American Express brand assets
- Diners Club brand assets
(Download their official SVG or PNG packs)

3) Prefer SVG, fallback to PNG (not JPEG)
- Best: SVG (sharp on all screens)
- OK: PNG (at least 2x resolution for retina)
- Avoid: JPEG (compression ruins logos)

4) Put them on a neutral background
- White or very light background is safest
- Keep enough padding around each logo (clear space)
- Keep consistent visual height, not identical pixel height if it makes one look too big

5) If you cannot get official marks today
TEMP option that is safest:
- Remove logos completely
- Show text only: “Payments accepted: Credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay”
This is more professional than wrong marks.

6) Implementation (simple, professional)
Use a single row of SVG or PNG images, no filters, no opacity, no grayscale.

HTML:
<div class="pay-row" aria-label="Payments accepted">
  <img src="/pay/apple-pay.svg" alt="Apple Pay" loading="lazy" />
  <img src="/pay/google-pay.svg" alt="Google Pay" loading="lazy" />
  <img src="/pay/visa.svg" alt="Visa" loading="lazy" />
  <img src="/pay/mastercard.svg" alt="Mastercard" loading="lazy" />
  <img src="/pay/amex.svg" alt="American Express" loading="lazy" />
  <img src="/pay/diners.svg" alt="Diners Club" loading="lazy" />
</div>

CSS:
.pay-row {
  display: flex;
  gap: 12px;
  align-items: center;
  justify-content: center;
  flex-wrap: wrap;
  padding: 10px 12px;
}

.pay-row img {
  height: 26px;        /* adjust 22 to 30 for your design */
  width: auto;
  max-width: 110px;
  object-fit: contain;
  image-rendering: auto;
}

7) If your developer insists “it must be one image”
Use ONE PNG sprite, not JPEG:
- Create a single PNG at 2x or 3x scale
- Keep original colors
- Export with transparent background
- Place inside a container with fixed height and object-fit: contain

8) Fast check
If a logo looks fuzzy on iPad:
- It is not SVG
- Or the PNG is too small (needs 2x)
- Or CSS is scaling it up
