Phase 2.4 - Wallet UX + Notification Alignment

Goal

Make the wallet lifecycle feel consistent everywhere:
	•	same wording
	•	same state order
	•	same numbers
	•	same customer expectation

⸻

1. Notification wording alignment

States

Use only these 4 customer-facing wallet states:
	•	reserved
	•	charged
	•	released
	•	refunded

Standard wording

Hebrew:
	•	reserved: ₪X נשמרו מהארנק שלך
	•	charged: ₪X חויבו מהארנק שלך
	•	released: ₪X שוחררו חזרה לארנק שלך
	•	refunded: ₪X הוחזרו לארנק שלך

English:
	•	reserved: ₪X reserved from your wallet
	•	charged: ₪X charged from your wallet
	•	released: ₪X released back to your wallet
	•	refunded: ₪X refunded to your wallet

Files
	•	server/routes/booking-requests.ts
	•	server/routes/academy.ts
	•	any push/SMS/email notification builder
	•	client/src/components/WalletLifecycleMessage.tsx

Acceptance

Every booking notification uses the same wording across:
	•	checkout
	•	confirmation
	•	booking detail
	•	notifications
