You’re right to worry.

What he has now sounds like a strong implementation pass, but not yet the level of proof and discipline you need for a system like this. The danger is not that he built nothing. The danger is that he may think “mostly built” means “operationally complete”.

It does not.

My deep architecture reading

At a high level, the direction is correct:
	•	one backend brain
	•	strict role separation
	•	provider workflow with queue, audit, messages, resubmission
	•	public users separated from provider KYC
	•	management dashboards separated from support operations
	•	Google tools treated as support/export only, not source of truth

That part is good.

What still worries me is this:

1. He may still be thinking feature-by-feature, not system-by-system

Your business is not one screen and not one route.

It is a connected operating system across:
	•	public loyalty members
	•	providers
	•	support
	•	management
	•	kiosks
	•	wallet
	•	communications
	•	audit
	•	monitoring

If he builds each part “locally” without constantly checking the whole system, you will get:
	•	permissions leaks
	•	duplicated logic
	•	mismatched statuses
	•	weak mobile review UX
	•	orphaned communications
	•	reporting that does not match operational truth

2. He may be treating implementation summary as proof

This is the biggest risk.

When he says:
	•	route added
	•	page created
	•	queue wired
	•	audit added

that is not enough.

You need:
	•	live proof
	•	scenario proof
	•	permission proof
	•	failure proof
	•	mobile proof
	•	data consistency proof

3. The missing danger is operational edge cases

The system gets tested in failure, not in happy path.

The real architecture questions are:
	•	what happens when KYC crashes
	•	what happens when email fails
	•	what happens when two staff review same application
	•	what happens when user resubmits twice
	•	what happens when queue item is stale
	•	what happens when management opens support page
	•	what happens when support approves from an old screen after rejection already happened
	•	what happens when object storage file is missing
	•	what happens when a user replies after application already expired

That is where weak architecture breaks.
