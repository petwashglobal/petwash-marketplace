How it works, in plain logic:

Quote preview
	•	User enters booking flow
	•	Backend returns live loyaltyAvailableCents
	•	When toggle is on, backend calculates loyaltyRedeemedCents
	•	Total updates instantly
	•	No ledger write yet

Booking creation
	•	Booking is created
	•	Backend redeems the exact loyalty amount once
	•	Fingerprint prevents double debit on retry
	•	booking_requests.loyalty_redeemed_cents stores the result

Ledger
	•	Every credit movement is append-only
	•	Positive rows mean earned
	•	Negative rows mean redeemed or expired
	•	users.loyalty_balance_cents stays a fast cache, not the source of truth
