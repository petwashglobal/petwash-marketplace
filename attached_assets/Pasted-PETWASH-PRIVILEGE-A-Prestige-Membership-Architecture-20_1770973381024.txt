3. Tier Logic Architecture

We use an annual qualification model.

Tiers are calculated over a rolling 12-month period.

Qualification criteria combine:

• Total spend
• Number of washes
• Wallet top-ups
• Engagement activity

4. Reward Logic Engine

Instead of “points”, use:

Privilege Credit

Every 1 ILS = 1 Base Credit

Tier multipliers apply automatically.

Example:

Member = 1x
Signature = 1.05x
Elite = 1.1x
Privilege = 1.15x
Black Reserve = 1.25x

Credits can be redeemed at:

• Kiosk via QR
• Mobile wallet scan
• Auto-deduct option

Redemption logic:

• Minimum redemption threshold
• Credits expire after 18 months (to encourage activity)
• Bonus credits expire faster (to drive conversion)
