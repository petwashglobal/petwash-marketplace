What he got right

The most important finding was this:
	•	the bridge was already there
	•	EventPublisher already forwards into EventBus
	•	so the issue was not architecture missing
	•	the issue was broken subscriptions and missing handlers/templates

That is exactly the kind of bug that makes a system look “wired” while business actions never happen.

He fixed the right 3 things:

1. Broken event keys

This was serious.

If handlers were listening to:
	•	INCIDENT_REPORTED
but the actual emitted event was:
	•	incident.reported

then those handlers were dead from day one.

That is a real business bug, not cosmetic.

2. Missing business event handlers

Adding handlers for:
	•	booking.completed
	•	booking.cancelled
	•	loyalty.tier_upgraded
	•	provider.approved
	•	provider.rejected

is the right move.

Those are exactly the kinds of events that should drive customer/provider communications.

3. Templates actually present in DB

Also important.

He found that the database did not even contain the original 7 templates in this environment.
That means even “working” handlers would still fail at runtime or silently skip delivery.

Fixing that was necessary.

	•	live templates

	•	provider approval/rejection notification path
