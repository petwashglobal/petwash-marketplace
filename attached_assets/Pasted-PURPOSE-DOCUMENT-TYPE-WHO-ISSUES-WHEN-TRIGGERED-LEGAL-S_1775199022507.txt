1. Customer pays → money is captured → receipt MAY be required (depending structure)
2. Money is NOT revenue yet → it is HELD (escrow)
3. Platform may take fee → this part MAY be taxable revenue
4. Provider only earns when:
   - service completed
   - funds released
5. ONLY THEN:
   - provider may need to issue tax invoice (depending model)
6. Escrow statement = explanation of flow, NOT legal tax document
