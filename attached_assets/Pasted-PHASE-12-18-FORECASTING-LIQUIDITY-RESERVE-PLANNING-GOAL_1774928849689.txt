PHASE 12.18 - FORECASTING, LIQUIDITY & RESERVE PLANNING

GOAL
Move treasury from hindsight to foresight.

12.17 proved whether money moved correctly.
12.18 must show what money is likely to move next, what money is trapped in reserve, where liquidity pressure is building, and where the business may face payout stress before it happens.

This phase is not bookkeeping.
It is forward-looking cash control.

--------------------------------------------------
CORE BUSINESS QUESTIONS THIS PHASE MUST ANSWER
--------------------------------------------------

1. What cash is expected to leave the system over the next 7 / 14 / 30 days?
2. How much money is currently held in reserve, and how old is that reserve?
3. Which reserves are likely to release soon, and which are getting stuck?
4. Which franchises or stations create the highest payout pressure?
5. Are we approaching a payout day with more expected outflow than treasury should comfortably release?
6. Where is cash blocked by disputes, mismatches, failed payouts, or unresolved approvals?
7. What is the difference between:
   - gross expected payout
   - releasable payout
   - held payout
   - risky payout
   - failed payout

--------------------------------------------------
T181 - FORECAST SNAPSHOT ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------

BACKEND
Build a forecasting engine that computes future payout expectations from existing data.

SOURCES
- station_settlements
- payout_batches
- payout_failures
- booking_disputes
- financial_approval_log
- reconciliation_results

OUTPUT WINDOWS
- next 7 days
- next 14 days
- next 30 days

RETURN
For each window:
- expected_gross_outflow_cents
- expected_releasable_outflow_cents
- expected_held_outflow_cents
- expected_risky_outflow_cents
- expected_failed_retry_outflow_cents

DEFINITIONS
- expected_gross_outflow = all settlement-linked money likely to become payable
- releasable = not disputed, not mismatched, not held, approved
- held = reserve or hold active
- risky = approval pending, dispute open, mismatch unresolved
- failed_retry = failed payout batches likely to be retried

RULE
Do not guess.
Only count money based on explicit state.

--------------------------------------------------
T182 - RESERVE AGEING MODEL
--------------------------------------------------

BACKEND
GET /api/treasury/reserve-ageing

GROUPS
- 0-7 days
- 8-14 days
- 15-30 days
- 31+ days

RETURN
- reserve_count
- reserve_amount_cents
- avg_age_days
- oldest_reserve_days

BREAKDOWN
- by company
- by franchise
- by station

BUSINESS LOGIC
Money held for 2 days and money held for 45 days are not the same problem.
Treasury must know whether reserve is normal friction or structural blockage.

RULE
Every held settlement must have:
- hold_reason
- hold_started_at
If missing, the item is invalid and must be surfaced as treasury data quality issue.

--------------------------------------------------
T183 - LIQUIDITY PRESSURE ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------

BACKEND
GET /api/treasury/liquidity-pressure

PURPOSE
Show whether upcoming payout obligations create operational pressure.

RETURN
- next_payout_day_expected_outflow
- next_7_days_expected_outflow
- next_14_days_expected_outflow
- total_held_amount
- failed_batches_waiting_retry
- unresolved_approval_amount
- unresolved_dispute_amount
- pressure_level

PRESSURE_LEVEL
- low
- medium
- high
- critical

RULE LOGIC
First pass can be rule-based:
- LOW: expected outflow well below normal threshold
- MEDIUM: large but manageable payable amount
- HIGH: large outflow plus significant held/risky money
- CRITICAL: failed batches + high reserve + unresolved high-value approvals

IMPORTANT
Do not pretend this is bank balance forecasting.
This is treasury pressure forecasting from internal obligations.

- disputes

BUSINESS LOGIC
This is not the historical payout calendar from 12.17.
This is the forward-looking payout calendar.

--------------------------------------------------

BACKEND

--------------------------------------------------
T187 - FORECAST VS ACTUAL
--------------------------------------------------

BACKEND
GET /api/treasury/forecast-vs-actual

PURPOSE
Measure whether treasury forecasting is useful.

RETURN
Per completed payout cycle:
- forecasted_releasable_cents
- actual_paid_cents
- variance_cents
- variance_pct

WINDOWS
- last 7 cycles
- last 30 days

BUSINESS LOGIC
If forecast says 500k and actual paid is 120k, the engine is wrong.
This endpoint is the discipline layer for the forecast model itself.

--------------------------------------------------
T190 - DATA DISCIPLINE RULES
--------------------------------------------------

1. Forecast must never mix:
   - releasable
   - held
   - risky
   - failed
2. Reserve ageing must be based on explicit hold timestamps
3. Forecast must be ownership-aware:
   - company-owned stations
   - franchise-owned stations
4. Forecasting must not invent bank balance
5. Warning severity must be deterministic, not subjective
6. Forecast vs actual must use completed historical cycles only
7. Every forecasted amount must be traceable back to settlement states

--------------------------------------------------

- treasury can see expected outflow for 7 / 14 / 30 days
- treasury can see reserve ageing clearly
- treasury can see payout pressure before payout day
- treasury can compare risk across franchises and stations
- treasury gets explicit warnings, not silent buildup
- finance can compare forecast to real paid outcomes
- all large forecast numbers are drillable
