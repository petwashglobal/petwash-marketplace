Yes - that is the next big layer after chat.

What you approved now is mostly the transaction communication layer.
A real provider or host dashboard for 2026 needs a much bigger operating system around it.

What a serious provider dashboard must include

1. Availability and calendar control

Provider must be able to:
	•	mark available / unavailable
	•	block whole days
	•	block time ranges
	•	recurring weekly schedule
	•	holiday mode
	•	emergency unavailable toggle
	•	buffer time between jobs
	•	travel radius / service area
	•	max jobs per day
	•	max simultaneous bookings if relevant

Very important:
calendar logic must drive matching, not just display information.

⸻

2. Booking decision controls

Provider should have:
	•	accept job
	•	reject job
	•	suggest another time
	•	request more info
	•	cancel accepted job with reason
	•	mark no-show
	•	mark arrived
	•	mark in progress
	•	mark completed
	•	escalate dispute
	•	report unsafe customer / pet / address

And every action should be:
	•	timestamped
	•	reason-coded
	•	auditable

⸻

3. Provider settings panel

This must include switches and controls like:
	•	auto-accept on/off
	•	instant booking on/off
	•	require manual approval on/off
	•	same-day bookings on/off
	•	weekend jobs on/off
	•	night jobs on/off
	•	repeat customers only on/off
	•	new customer requests on/off
	•	chat notifications on/off
	•	push notifications on/off
	•	email notifications on/off
	•	emergency SMS notifications on/off
	•	AI assistant suggestions on/off

⸻

4. Pricing controls

Provider needs flexible pricing, not just one price.

Examples:
	•	base price
	•	service duration
	•	size-based pricing
	•	extra pet pricing
	•	holiday surcharge
	•	urgent booking surcharge
	•	travel surcharge
	•	cancellation fee rules
	•	late-night fee
	•	repeat-customer discount
	•	promo participation on/off

⸻

5. Calendar intelligence

This is where Gemini or AI can really help.

AI assistant can suggest:
	•	best time slots to open
	•	when to block overload days
	•	likely cancellation risk
	•	high-demand hours
	•	price optimization suggestions
	•	gaps in the day that can fit another booking
	•	whether to accept a job based on distance, pet type, and earnings

So Gemini should not just be chat.
It should act like a provider business assistant.

⸻

6. Provider performance dashboard

They should see:
	•	bookings today / week / month
	•	acceptance rate
	•	completion rate
	•	cancellation rate
	•	response time
	•	average rating
	•	repeat customer rate
	•	earnings pending
	•	earnings released
	•	disputes count
	•	flagged messages / risks
	•	top service types

⸻

7. Safety and control features

Very important for hosts/providers:
	•	customer notes
	•	pet behavior notes
	•	blocked customer list
	•	blocked addresses
	•	blacklist / safety alerts
	•	ID / verification status
	•	insurance status
	•	compliance reminders
	•	document expiry reminders

⸻

8. Operational workflow tools

For a serious host/provider dashboard:
	•	task checklist before job
	•	arrival confirmation
	•	live tracking if applicable
	•	photo upload before/after
	•	service notes
	•	incident logging
	•	payout summary
	•	invoice / tax export
	•	dispute evidence upload

	•	recurring availability engine

What Gemini should do inside provider dashboard

Gemini should have a dedicated provider assistant panel with actions like:
	•	“Summarize today’s bookings”
	•	“Which jobs should I accept first?”
	•	“Show me risky bookings”
	•	“Suggest best availability for next week”
	•	“Draft a polite message to customer”
	•	“Explain why payout is delayed”
	•	“Summarize cancellation trends”
	•	“Recommend pricing changes”

That is much more powerful than just chatbot answers.
