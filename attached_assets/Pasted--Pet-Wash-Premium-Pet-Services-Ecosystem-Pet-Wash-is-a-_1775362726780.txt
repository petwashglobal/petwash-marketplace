🐾 Pet Wash™ - Premium Pet Services Ecosystem

Pet Wash™ is a multi-platform pet services infrastructure combining a free growth engine with regulated, payment-enabled marketplaces in one unified system.

This is not just a marketplace.
It is a financial, operational, and compliance platform designed for scale.

⸻

🧠 Platform Logic

Pet Wash™ operates on a 2-layer model:

🆓 1. Growth Layer (Free Entry - TRACTION ENGINE)

PawFinder - Lost & Found Pets
	•	100% free
	•	no friction entry
	•	emotional use case (lost pets)
	•	map-based discovery + rewards
	•	designed for viral sharing

Purpose:
	•	drive organic traffic (SEO + social)
	•	bring users into the ecosystem
	•	build trust before monetization

👉 This is the main entry point of the entire platform

⸻

💳 Payments & Escrow
	•	Nayax captures payment
	•	EscrowService holds funds (72 hours)
	•	WalletLedger records:
	•	double-entry accounting
	•	SHA-256 hash chain

Flow:
	1.	payment captured
	2.	funds held in escrow
	3.	service completed
	4.	funds released to provider

⸻

🇮🇱 Israeli Tax Compliance (2026 Ready)
	•	direct ITA API integration (api.taxes.gov.il)
	•	OAuth2 authentication
	•	real-time allocation number (מספר הקצאה)
	•	required for invoices above ₪5,000

Flow:
	1.	payment completed
	2.	invoice sent to ITA
	3.	allocation number returned
	4.	stored for 7 years
	5.	PDF generated after confirmation

⸻
